Here is the current **core impasse** and a set of **facilitator proposals** to move the discourse forward.

## Core impasse
The deadlock is not over whether the company needs restructuring; all parties now accept that it does. The impasse is over **who bears how much of the adjustment, and whether protected domestic capacity and retiree obligations can be preserved without breaking standalone viability**.

The conflict clusters into three linked issues:

1. **Retiree trust treatment**
   - Labour/retiree trust wants a durable, real claim.
   - The task force, management, and bondholders reject anything that leaves the operating company cash-flow impaired.
   - Bondholders also resist any arrangement where the trust is treated more favorably than unsecured creditors.

2. **Footprint reduction vs domestic plant protection**
   - The expert says viability requires meaningful downsizing.
   - Neighbouring governments want binding protection for domestic plants and an ownership stake.
   - Others insist plant closures must be based on economics, not political geography.

3. **Depth and distribution of creditor impairment**
   - The task force and management want deep unsecured haircuts.
   - Bondholders accept impairment but reject being the “default dumping ground.”
   - Labour insists sacrifice must be shared and not concentrated on workers and retirees.

---

## Facilitator compromise proposals

### Proposal 1: Split the retiree obligation into a protected core and a risk-sharing layer
**Structure**
- Preserve a **core retiree benefit instrument** with real value, but not full parity with an operating-company senior burden.
- Any amount above the company’s affordable level is converted into a **contingent instrument** tied to future performance or recoveries.
- Add limited external support or backstop funding only if pre-agreed milestones are met.

**Why it may bridge parties**
- Gives labour/retirees a real instrument, not a hollow claim.
- Removes the full cash-flow burden from the operating company.
- Reduces bondholder concern that the trust is being insulated entirely.

---

### Proposal 2: Core domestic plant protection only, with explicit economic test and sunset review
**Structure**
- Protect only a **defined core set of domestic plants** that meet objective cost/utilization thresholds.
- Any other domestic sites get no automatic protection.
- Include a **sunset review** after restructuring completion to reassess production allocation based on actual performance.

**Why it may bridge parties**
- Gives neighbouring governments some binding domestic protection.
- Preserves the task force and expert’s requirement that the footprint remain economically viable.
- Prevents a blanket political freeze on all domestic capacity.

---

### Proposal 3: Tiered creditor recovery with shared upside
**Structure**
- Unsecured bondholders take a deep haircut, but receive meaningful equity/warrants.
- The retiree trust also receives an instrument with real upside participation.
- Allocate new equity so that no constituency appears to be the sole absorber of losses.
- Tie any extra recovery to post-restructuring performance.

**Why it may bridge parties**
- Addresses bondholders’ objection to being singled out.
- Gives labour/retirees an enforceable stake.
- Supports the task force’s demand for a genuinely deleveraged balance sheet.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To the Government Task Force
**What is the maximum level of retiree-obligation burden and unsecured debt the restructured company can carry while still remaining financeable on your own standards?**

### To the Industrial Union & Retiree Health Trust
**What specific form of reduction or conversion in the retiree trust would you accept that still leaves the trust with real, enforceable value but removes enough cash burden to make the company viable?**

### To Company Management
**Which plants, brands, and dealer capacity are you prepared to close or consolidate immediately, and what hard savings do those actions produce in year one?**

### To the Unsecured Bondholders’ Committee
**What haircut range and equity/warrant recovery would you accept as commercially meaningful, even if it is below par, provided the company is demonstrably viable?**

### To the Neighbouring Governments
**Which domestic plants are economically essential enough to justify binding protection, and what specific equity/governance rights are you seeking in exchange for co-funding?**

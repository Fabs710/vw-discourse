### Core impasse

The main blockage is **how the losses are allocated across stakeholder classes** while also deciding **what must be protected in the restructured company**.

There are three overlapping fault lines:

1. **Creditor treatment vs. legacy obligations**
   - Bondholders want no materially inferior treatment relative to other unsecured claims.
   - The union/retiree trust wants the retiree obligation preserved in durable form, not wiped out.
   - Management and the task force want a balance sheet light enough to survive.

2. **Domestic footprint vs. viability**
   - Neighbouring governments want binding protection for domestic plants and jobs.
   - The task force and management want significant plant/brand/dealer rationalization.
   - The union wants closures to be selective, not a disguised liquidation.

3. **Speed/finality vs. stakeholder protections**
   - The task force wants a fast, decisive restructuring.
   - Several parties want more favorable treatment or safeguards, which risks slowing agreement.

---

### Facilitator compromise positions

#### 1. “Minimum viable company + protected domestic core” package
A restructuring could define:
- a **minimum domestic production floor**,
- a **core set of plants/brands to remain open**,
- and a broader set of closures or consolidations elsewhere.

In exchange:
- the neighbouring governments co-fund only if that domestic floor is contractually binding,
- management gets authority to rationalize the rest of the footprint,
- and labour accepts targeted concessions limited to the protected core.

This bridges **viability** and **domestic protection** without freezing the entire footprint.

---

#### 2. “Equal unsecured treatment with compensating instruments”
To address the bondholder/retiree trust conflict:
- treat the retiree trust and unsecured bondholders as part of a common recovery pool,
- but allow the retiree trust to receive a **distinct instrument** only if bondholders receive an **offsetting amount of equity, warrants, or contingent value**.

This would preserve the idea of equal economic value across comparable unsecured claims while still giving retirees a durable recovery form rather than a pure write-off.

---

#### 3. “Staged public support tied to milestones”
Public funding from the task force and co-funders could be released in tranches tied to:
- finalized debt reduction,
- plant closure execution,
- labor/benefit concession implementation,
- and verified domestic production commitments.

If milestones are missed, financing stops and the matter moves to court-supervised restructuring.

This bridges **taxpayer protection**, **speed**, and **credibility** by making support conditional rather than open-ended.

# Moderator Summary — After Round 2

## 1. Points of Agreement / Convergence

- **Deadline mechanics:** Task Force has clarified (and Management has accepted) that only narrow, documentation-stage delays are eligible for accommodation on day 55 — not substantive extensions. No party has contested this.
- **Trust and Committee conversion headlines have converged numerically:** both now propose 55–60% of claim value converting to equity/warrants, with the remainder as a non-cash, longer-dated instrument (trusteed funded obligation with capped escalator, or CVR/deferred note respectively). The gap is no longer about percentages — it is about whether a cost-basis/acquisition-history adjustment applies on top of that shared number.
- **Neighbouring Governments' capital treated as new money, not claim conversion:** Committee proposed this framing; Neighbouring Governments explicitly accepted it. This appears settled as a structural principle, pending sizing/pricing detail.
- **Joint information/consultation process on plant closures:** Management has committed to sharing facility-by-facility data with the Union and Neighbouring Governments before Round 3; both parties have accepted consultation rights short of a veto.
- **Footprint depth:** Management has put concrete numbers on record — 4 of 8 brands eliminated, ~35% assembly/powertrain capacity reduction, ~40% dealer reduction (structured, funded wind-down), ~30% corporate overhead cut.
- **Neighbouring Governments' contribution is now quantified:** $4.1B new capital, including up to $600M earmarked for competitiveness retooling, contingent on plant-specific floors.

## 2. Core Disagreements Still Unresolved

- **The cost-basis question.** The Union argues equitable treatment should account for acquisition cost/timing and inability to renegotiate; the Committee argues this is not a legitimate variable under bankruptcy priority logic and warns it would trigger exactly the equitable-treatment challenge the Task Force wants to avoid. This is now the central legal/economic fault line the Task Force must rule on.
- **Whether the finite equity pool gets sized down proportionally or asymmetrically.** Management flagged that all conversion asks combined may exceed sustainable equity headroom; the Committee insists any pool-sizing correction must apply equally across claimants, not compress its share alone.
- **What "cover the delta" means for Neighbouring Governments' protected plants.** Neighbouring Governments will fund closable competitiveness gaps but will not pay indefinitely to keep structurally uncompetitive plants open — and will walk if comparably-costed capacity elsewhere is preserved while their plants close. Management has not yet confirmed how this test will be applied.
- **Dealer network cuts:** Management is holding firm on a structured, compensated wind-down rather than a viability-only cut; no other party has yet contested or endorsed this specific mechanism.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Not Yet Addressed

- **Warranty/consumer-confidence backstop:** Management has asked the Task Force for a funded mechanism (bridge financing or government-backed guarantee) to assure warranty honoring through the restructuring. The Task Force has not yet responded to this ask, and it materially affects how aggressively Management can move on brands/dealers.
- **Governance seat allocation across three parties (Trust, Committee, Neighbouring Governments) on the same board** — proportionality formula not yet reconciled across all three simultaneously.
- **Total equity pool ceiling:** no party has proposed an actual number for total post-restructuring equity available, against which all conversion/governance asks can be tested together.
- **What happens if the Task Force's equal-rank test produces a number the Union or Committee will not sign** — no fallback framework has been discussed.

## 4. Refocus for Round 3

The Task Force has asked for numbers; it now has them. Round 3 should focus on:

1. **Task Force ruling** on the cost-basis question — is acquisition history a valid variable in calibrating Trust vs. Committee treatment, yes or no — since this gates whether the two converging proposals can actually be finalized as parallel or must diverge.
2. **Reconciliation of the equity pool** — Management, Task Force, and both creditor bodies should confirm whether pool-sizing constraints (for post-emergence capital headroom) apply proportionally across all converting/new-capital parties.
3. **Facility-by-facility data exchange** between Management and Neighbouring Governments/Union, testing the "closable gap vs. structural gap" distinction concretely against the $4.1B/$600M offer.
4. **Task Force response** to Management's warranty-backstop request, since it affects the feasibility of the brand/dealer timeline already put on record.

# Facilitator Summary — Following Round 4

## 1. What Has Been Agreed

- **Incentive plan**: Provisionally set at 1.5% (down from Management's proposed 3%), pending an independently-selected benchmark against 3–5 comparable restructurings. Union and Committee to see the benchmark set before finalization; actuary has declined this task as outside actuarial mandate — an independent compensation consultant is needed.
- **Capital-raise reserve**: Approved at up to 12%, conditional on consent rights. Management proposed a workable structure — majority-inclusive threshold (not unanimity) with a time-bound response window — rather than a design that could paralyze future capital raises. Committee has accepted this structure in principle, subject to exact numbers being fixed in closing documents rather than left as "examples."
- **Reserve inclusion confirmed for Trust**: Management explicitly confirmed the Trust's converted stake is included in the consent mechanism on the same proportional basis as the Committee's.
- **Instrument-equivalence methodology**: Confirmed as symmetric by the Task Force and detailed by the actuary — same rigor, externally-sourced trend rate and enterprise-value assumptions, both point-estimate and distributional/variance comparison to be reported (not just expected-value matching). Union and Committee have both made reciprocal, formal commitments to accept a downward correction if the test runs against them.
- **Facility data process**: Task Force ruled against Management's delay; five-day delivery window ordered. Management accepted this, proposed a seven-day joint review clock following delivery, with extensions available for specific plants on documented cause rather than extending the whole process.
- **Warranty backstop**: Management delivered a preliminary sizing ($1.1–1.4B), structured last-resort/non-senior/behind reserves. Actuary will independently verify using loss-reserving methodology, incorporating Union shop-floor data, and will size to a confidence threshold rather than a mean estimate.
- **Dealer network wind-down**: Uncontested this round; standing as previously structured.
- **Governance seat calculation**: Principle settled (proportional to claim/contribution size); only the arithmetic remains, pending final claim and contribution figures.

## 2. Where Core Disagreements Remain

- **Neighbouring Governments' consent-right parity**: NG has requested the same explicit confirmation given to the Trust — that their board representative is included in the reserve consent mechanism on equal footing. This has not yet been stated by Management or ruled on directly.
- **Sequencing/timing gap**: NG has raised a concrete arithmetic problem: the facility review-and-determination process (five days + seven days = ~12 days) may not resolve before final allocation numbers are due, potentially forcing NG to sign on an unresolved figure or trigger a deadline default over a timing issue rather than substance. NG has proposed a provisional lock with a true-up mechanism; this has not yet been responded to by the Task Force or other parties.
- **Footprint depth — "fuller viability lens"**: Union and NG want supply-chain depth, engineering capacity, and production flexibility formally incorporated into the viability analysis, not just near-term unit cost. Management says it already models this and will make it visible in review; Union/NG have not yet seen that modeling and reserve the right to object if the review turns out to be "the same unilateral model with a new label."
- **Sequencing dependency between facility review and CVR valuation**: The actuary has flagged that the enterprise-value forecast feeding the CVR equivalence test cannot be finalized on placeholder assumptions if the footprint outcome will materially change those assumptions. The actuary recommends running the equivalence test twice (preliminary and post-review-final), with only the second treated as dispositive. This recommendation has not yet been adopted or rejected by the Task Force.
- **Committee's conditional sign-off**: The Committee has stated it will sign once it sees its net percentage (after carve-outs) compared directly against the Trust's net percentage on the same basis — this comparison has not yet been produced.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **Speed vs. protection on the reserve consent mechanism**: Exact threshold numbers and response-window length remain to be fixed in binding language, not just "for example" figures.
- **Court-filing deadline vs. NG's true-up proposal**: The Task Force has not yet indicated whether the true-up/provisional-lock approach is an acceptable way to reconcile the 12-day review clock against the day-60 deadline, or whether NG's final number must be actually fixed by day 60 regardless.
- **Benchmark curation for the incentive plan**: Who selects the comparable restructuring cases, and whether that selection is genuinely independent of Management's influence, remains unresolved procedurally.
- **Running the equivalence test twice**: Whether the Task Force will formally sequence sign-off to require a post-facility-review final version of the CVR/Trust equivalence test, as the actuary recommends, rather than accepting a single earlier version.

## 4. Refocus for Next Round

With this being the final scheduled round, the remaining work is narrow but load-bearing. The next session should prioritize, in order:

1. **Task Force ruling on the Neighbouring Governments' consent-parity request and the proposed true-up/provisional-lock sequencing mechanism** — this determines whether NG can close on schedule at all.
2. **Confirmation or rejection of the actuary's two-pass equivalence-test proposal**, given its direct bearing on both the Committee's and Trust's sign-off conditions.
3. **Delivery and joint review of facility data on the agreed clock**, with visible incorporation of supply-chain/capability modeling as Management has committed to show.
4. **Production of the completed allocation table with actual net percentages**, once Trust claim size and NG's final contribution figure are fixed — enabling the Committee's stated comparison test.
5. **Independent benchmark for the incentive plan and finalized reserve consent-threshold numbers**, both flagged as needing precise, non-provisional figures before signature.

**Company Management (New Leadership) — Round 3**

The Task Force's rulings this round give us the clarity we need to move from framework to close. We'll respond directly, and we're bringing the two hardest numbers we were asked for: the equity pool ceiling and the facility-specific test result.

**On the equal-treatment ruling and pool sizing.** We support both. The cost-basis carve-out would have created an administrability problem we'd have inherited operationally — every subsequent claim dispute would have invited the same argument, and we don't have the capacity or the sixty days to litigate acquisition history across a $27 billion bondholder base. The instrument-equivalence approach is workable and we'll support the actuary's process fully, including making our own claims data and historical retiree medical cost experience available immediately — the actuary needs that, and stalling on data delivery would be its own form of bad faith.

**The equity pool ceiling — the number the Task Force asked for.**

- **12% reserved, authorized but unissued** — headroom for future capital raises. This is not a discretionary management cushion; it exists because a restructuring that leaves zero equity currency for year-three capital needs simply repeats this crisis on a longer fuse. We will accept Task Force oversight on when and how this reserve is issued.

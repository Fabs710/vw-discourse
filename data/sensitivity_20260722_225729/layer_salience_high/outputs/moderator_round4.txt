## Core impasse
The impasse is not mainly about whether restructuring is needed. The shared diagnosis is that it is.

The core deadlock is this:

- **Management, owners, and investors** want a restructuring that is proven on economics, with closures and stronger structural measures still available if needed.
- **Works council, IG Metall, and Lower Saxony** want a binding no-closure / no-forced-redundancy framework before employees concede anything, and they reject closure as bargaining leverage.

So the conflict is over **sequencing and conditionality**:
1. whether closure remains a live instrument during negotiation,
2. whether employee concessions can be requested before site-specific proof is complete,
3. and whether a closure-free package can be treated as the primary test rather than a fallback.

---

## Facilitator compromise proposals

### Proposal 1: Time-limited non-closure test with hard audit gates
A structured trial period could be set in which:
- no German plant closures and no compulsory redundancies apply for a defined period,
- an independent auditor tests the closure-free restructuring site by site,
- and the package must meet pre-agreed economic thresholds by a set deadline.

If the thresholds are met, the no-closure package becomes binding for the agreed horizon.  
If not, all parties re-enter negotiations on the basis of verified plant economics.

**Purpose:** bridge labour’s demand for protection with capital’s demand for proof.

---

### Proposal 2: Conditional employee contribution in exchange for binding guarantees
A temporary workforce contribution could be linked to a package of enforceable protections, such as:
- no German plant closures during the agreement period,
- no compulsory redundancies during the agreement period,
- binding investment and product commitments by site,
- and a shared governance mechanism for monitoring implementation.

The employee contribution would be limited in duration and scope, and automatically expire unless management and owners deliver their side of the package.

**Purpose:** address the “workers pay first, guarantees later” concern by making concessions reciprocal and reversible.

---

### Proposal 3: Site-by-site classification with differentiated outcomes
Instead of a single all-sites rule, each German plant could be placed into one of three categories after audit:
- **Core sites:** protected with binding future product and investment commitments.
- **Restructuring sites:** kept open, but with agreed capacity reduction, redeployment, and voluntary exit measures.
- **Review sites:** subject to a second-stage decision only if the economics remain unresolved after a defined period.

This would avoid treating every site identically while still preventing immediate closure decisions.

**Purpose:** create a middle path between blanket no-closure demands and open-ended closure threats.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To the Works Council
What specific economic evidence or threshold would make you accept that a plant-by-plant non-closure test has failed and must move to a second-stage decision?

### To IG Metall
Would you accept a temporary employee contribution if it were tied to legally binding no-closure, no-forced-redundancy guarantees and audited site-by-site economic targets?

### To Lower Saxony
Will you use your blocking role to insist on a binding audit-first process, even if that means accepting a temporary uncertainty period before final site decisions?

### To Management
Will you commit to a defined period in which the non-closure restructuring is tested first, with closures and forced redundancies suspended unless the independent audit fails on agreed criteria?

### To the Controlling Owners
What concrete burden-sharing from ownership are you prepared to put on the table, beyond asking labour to absorb adjustment risk?

### To Institutional Investors
If a closure-free package meets the agreed savings and margin targets, will you support it even if it preserves all German sites?

### To the Expert / Auditor
What minimum data set and what pass/fail metrics would you require to determine, plant by plant, whether the non-closure restructuring is economically sufficient?

We are not prepared to accept plant closures or compulsory redundancies in Germany.

Our position is clear:

- Every German plant must remain open.
- There must be no operational layoffs.
- The historic job-security framework must be preserved, not dismantled.
- Any restructuring must be negotiated, binding, and socially responsible.
- The workforce must have a genuine co-determination role in shaping the future path, not be presented with a fait accompli.

We recognize that the company faces serious competitive pressure. But we will not accept that the burden is placed primarily on employees through closures, job cuts, and the tearing up of guarantees that were promised for years. That would be unfair, reckless, and destructive for the workforce, the regions, and the company’s long-term stability.

If management wants a viable solution, it must come with:
- binding site and employment guarantees through 2030,
- no forced layoffs,
- no closure of German sites,
- and a credible industrial plan that secures investment, future products, and jobs together.

We are prepared to discuss alternative savings measures, including capacity adjustments, working-time solutions, and fair employee contributions — but only within those red lines. If the proposal is built on closures or compulsory layoffs, then we will oppose it.

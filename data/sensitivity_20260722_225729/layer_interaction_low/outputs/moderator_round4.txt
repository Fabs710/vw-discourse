Core impasse, procedurally stated:

1. **The parties agree on the need for quantified restructuring.**  
   The disagreement is not about whether numbers are needed; it is about what those numbers are allowed to prove.

2. **Management, owners, and investors want closure options kept live until the audit shows otherwise.**  
   The works council, IG Metall, and Lower Saxony want closures excluded as a bargaining lever unless non-closure options are first shown to fail.

3. **The entire dispute has therefore shifted to the burden of proof.**  
   One side says: “Show that no-closure works.”  
   The other says: “Show that closures are unavoidable.”  
   That is the deadlock.

4. **There is also a trust impasse.**  
   The labor side sees “review” language as a disguised countdown to closure.  
   The management/capital side sees “no closures” as a way to block a real reset before the numbers are tested.

## Facilitator proposals for bridge positions

### Proposal 1: Conditional no-closure package with objective trigger points
A settlement could be framed as:
- **No German plant closures during the agreement term**
- **No compulsory redundancies during the agreement term**
- **But** plant-by-plant review with predefined financial triggers
- If a site fails agreed thresholds after specific non-closure measures are implemented, the parties must enter a second-stage negotiation on that site only

This would attempt to separate:
- immediate employment protection
from
- later site-specific economic review

### Proposal 2: Mixed restructuring with mandatory burden-sharing on all sides
A compromise could combine:
- working-time flexibility
- voluntary exits
- shift consolidation
- internal redeployment
- management overhead cuts
- bonus moderation
- and binding product/investment commitments for German sites

In return, labor would accept measurable productivity and cost targets, while management and owners would accept:
- defined contributions from their side
- no unilateral closure announcement during the review period
- and an external audit that reports on non-closure and closure economics in the same format

### Proposal 3: Site-by-site classification instead of a single all-or-nothing rule
Instead of treating all German plants identically, the parties could classify sites into:
- **protected sites** with binding investment and no-closure status,
- **adjustment sites** with internal capacity reduction and workforce measures,
- **exception review sites** where closure or major rationalization remains legally and economically assessable only after audit-defined steps

This would preserve the labor side’s concern about blanket closure pressure while giving management a route to address the most structurally stressed locations.

## One pointed question to each stakeholder

### To management
**Which specific site-level non-closure measures would you accept as sufficient to test before any closure discussion begins, and what savings threshold would you use to declare them inadequate?**

### To the works council
**If the audit shows one or more sites still fall materially short after working-time, voluntary exit, shift, and overhead measures, what further non-closure measures would you be prepared to negotiate before ruling out site rationalization entirely?**

### To IG Metall
**Are you prepared to support a quantified package that includes labor concessions if, and only if, it comes with binding no-closure and no-forced-layoff guarantees for the agreement period?**

### To Lower Saxony
**Would you support a settlement that protects sites and jobs for the term, but allows site-specific corrective action only after a jointly reviewed audit trigger is reached?**

### To the controlling owners
**Would you accept a temporary no-closure commitment if the audit is structured to test whether non-closure measures can reach your required NPV target within a defined period?**

### To institutional investors
**Would you back a package that delays closure optionality in exchange for verified recurring savings, if the audit and implementation milestones are transparent and enforceable?**

### To the expert / auditor
**Can you define a single comparison table that shows, for each site, the residual cost gap after non-closure measures, so the parties can see whether closures are truly necessary or only economically preferable?**

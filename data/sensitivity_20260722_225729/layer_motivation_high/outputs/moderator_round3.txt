## Core impasse

The central impasse is **not over whether Volkswagen must restructure**. All parties accept that it must.  
The impasse is over **the method of achieving durable cost and capacity reduction**:

- **Management, owners, and investors** want proof that a non-closure package can close the structural gap.
- **Works council, IG Metall, and Lower Saxony** reject closures and compulsory redundancies as a starting point and want binding protection.
- The **expert input** says the deciding issue is whether the company has mainly a labor-cost problem or a fixed-cost overcapacity problem — but the needed site-by-site numbers are still missing.

So the dispute is stuck at this point:  
**Is a no-closure restructuring economically equivalent to a closure/consolidation-led restructuring, and if so under what conditions?**

---

## Concrete compromise positions to bridge the conflict

### 1) Site-by-site “closure-last” test with hard triggers
A phased restructuring could be agreed in which:
- every German site is first tested against a **common economic scorecard**,
- non-closure measures are applied first,
- closure or consolidation is only considered if a site still fails clearly defined thresholds.

**Possible trigger metrics**
- minimum utilization rate,
- minimum cost gap after labor and flex measures,
- minimum future product allocation,
- defined time horizon for viability.

This gives labour and Lower Saxony a structured safeguard while giving management and investors a numbers-based route to hard decisions if needed.

---

### 2) Binding employment and investment pact, conditional on quantified labour contribution
A negotiated package could combine:
- **no compulsory redundancies for a defined period**,
- **voluntary exits, redeployment, retraining, and working-time flexibility**,
- **temporary wage restraint or bonus moderation**,
- and **binding product/investment commitments** for German sites.

In return, management would need to provide:
- a quantified target for annual savings,
- a site-level implementation plan,
- and a review mechanism if savings are not achieved.

This would preserve the principle of burden-sharing without requiring an immediate closure commitment.

---

### 3) Modular plant-network restructuring with protected core sites
Instead of treating all German sites identically, the parties could agree on:
- a **core network of protected sites** with binding product allocation,
- **specialization** of each site to reduce duplication,
- and selective consolidation of lines or functions rather than full plant closure.

This could satisfy the call for capacity reduction while giving works council and Lower Saxony a framework that protects the industrial base and reduces the sense of open-ended closure threat.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To Group & Brand Management
**What exact site-by-site savings gap remains after all non-closure measures are applied, and at what threshold would you say a site is still economically viable without closure?**

### To the Works Council
**If closures are excluded as a matter of principle, what quantified combination of labor, capacity, and product-allocation measures would close the cost gap at each site?**

### To IG Metall
**Which specific employee contributions are you prepared to make in a binding agreement, and what measurable savings must management commit to in return?**

### To Lower Saxony
**Under what objective economic conditions, if any, would you accept consolidation or site adjustment, and how would those conditions be governed so they cannot be used as a backdoor to arbitrary closures?**

### To the Controlling Owners
**Will you support a restructuring package that avoids closures only if the site-by-site numbers show equivalent durability, even if the short-term financial effect is slower than a closure-led solution?**

### To Institutional Investors
**Would you endorse a closure-last settlement if it produces verified, recurring annual savings and a credible path to margins within a defined timeframe, even if the path is politically more complex?**

### To the Expert
**What minimum data set and decision rule would you require to determine whether a non-closure package is truly equivalent to closure on a site-by-site basis?**

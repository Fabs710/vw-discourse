## Core impasse

The plateau is centered on **one unresolved question**:

**Can Volkswagen achieve closure-level, durable savings without plant closures or compulsory redundancies, and if so, what exact combination of measures makes that credible?**

That breaks into three linked conflicts:

1. **Risk allocation**
   - Union / works council / Lower Saxony want job and site security first.
   - Management / owners / investors want the right to use closures and redundancies if needed to secure savings.

2. **Credibility threshold**
   - Employee side says non-closure measures can be sufficient and should be proven first.
   - Capital/management side says “no closures” is only acceptable if it produces equivalent fixed-cost and capacity reduction.

3. **Sequencing**
   - Employee side wants alternatives exhausted before closures are treated as normal fallback.
   - Management/owners want closures to remain live during bargaining so the economics are not pre-empted.

---

## Facilitator compromise positions

### 1) “Non-closure first, with a hard equivalence test”
**Proposal:** Agree on a time-limited non-closure package first, but define in advance the quantitative benchmark it must hit.

**Core elements:**
- No immediate closures or compulsory redundancies during the negotiation window.
- A jointly signed savings target, capacity target, and headcount-reduction target.
- An agreed deadline for proving delivery, e.g. 12–18 months.
- If the package misses pre-set milestones, the parties reopen the footprint question.

**Why this might bridge sides:**
- Gives the union / Lower Saxony a genuine chance to prove alternatives.
- Gives management / owners / investors a written escape route if the package underperforms.

---

### 2) “Site-by-site differentiation rather than a blanket rule”
**Proposal:** Distinguish between viable, strategic, and structurally weak sites rather than applying one universal rule to all German plants.

**Core elements:**
- Every German site gets a joint viability review.
- Viable sites receive binding protection and investment commitments.
- Weak sites are addressed through reconfiguration, specialization, or conversion before any closure decision.
- Closures are not ruled in or out globally; they are only assessed after site-specific alternatives are tested.

**Why this might bridge sides:**
- Respects the employee side’s rejection of blanket closure logic.
- Respects management’s and investors’ need for operational realism.
- Creates a narrower and more evidence-based decision path.

---

### 3) “Pain-sharing package with conditional protections”
**Proposal:** Bundle employee concessions with matched contributions from management and owners, and tie job/site protections to the delivery of savings.

**Core elements:**
- Employee contribution: wage moderation, deferred pay, bonus restraint, working-time flexibility, voluntary exits.
- Management contribution: overhead cuts, layer reductions, investment rephasing, admin simplification.
- Owner contribution: governance backing for the package and, where relevant, stronger reinvestment commitment.
- In return: no compulsory redundancies and no closures for the agreed restructuring period, provided milestones are met.

**Why this might bridge sides:**
- Addresses the repeated concern that workers are being asked to carry the full burden.
- Keeps pressure on all parties to produce measurable savings.
- Preserves the possibility of job/site protection without making it unconditional.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### IG Metall
**What specific combination of voluntary exits, working-time changes, wage moderation, and product/volume reallocation would you accept as truly equivalent to closure-level savings, and what proof would you require to call it sufficient?**

### Controlling owners
**What exact savings threshold and enforcement mechanism would make you accept a non-closure package as a substitute for keeping closure options open?**

### Lower Saxony
**Under what quantified conditions would you support a restructuring package that protects sites but requires substantial labor-cost and capacity concessions, and what would you block if those conditions are not met?**

### Industrial turnaround specialist
**What is the smallest credible package of non-closure measures that would still qualify as closure-equivalent in a Volkswagen-sized footprint, and what are the first indicators that the package is underdelivering?**

### Group & Brand Management
**Which two or three measures do you believe are indispensable for achieving durable savings without closures, and which specific fallback would you activate if those measures fail?**

### Institutional investors
**What level of quantified annual savings and what implementation milestones would be enough for you to support a no-closure settlement as value-accretive rather than merely delay?**

### Works Council
**If closures and compulsory redundancies are off the table, what exact operational concessions would you be prepared to recommend to employees in order to close the savings gap credibly?**

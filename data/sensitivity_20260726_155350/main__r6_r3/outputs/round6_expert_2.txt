## Accounting/financial logic
A valid bridge analysis must distinguish:
- **one-off costs**: severance, restructuring charges, transition costs
- **recurring savings**: permanent labor and fixed-cost reduction
- **timing effects**: when savings actually appear
- **implementation risk**: whether measures are realistically executable under the governance setup

This matters because a package can look adequate on paper but still fail if savings arrive too slowly.

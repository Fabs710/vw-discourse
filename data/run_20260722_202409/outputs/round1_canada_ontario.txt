We are prepared to co-fund this restructuring, but only on terms that protect the domestic production footprint and workforce in our jurisdictions.

### Where we agree
- We agree with the task force that **this must be a deep, fast, and credible restructuring**, not a delay.
- We agree with management that the company must be made viable, and that **some excess capacity has to come out**.
- We agree with labour that the **retiree obligation cannot simply be erased** and that a durable settlement needs real value, not empty promises.
- We agree with creditors that **all unsecured claimants must face real sacrifice**.

### Where we disagree
What we cannot accept is a restructuring that **cuts domestic plants while preserving foreign footprint elsewhere**, especially if our taxpayers are asked to help finance the rescue.

If we are being asked to put public money in, then we need:
1. **Binding commitments on domestic production and employment**
   - no closure of the plants in our jurisdictions without a compelling, viability-based justification,
   - a clear allocation of future production to domestic facilities,
   - and enforceable job and investment commitments.

2. **A proportionate ownership position**
   - our co-funding must be reflected in the equity of the restructured company,
   - not treated as a junior political contribution with no commensurate say.

3. **Partnership treatment, not afterthought treatment**
   - we are not here to subsidize a plan designed entirely around the lead funder’s balance-sheet preferences while our plants are put at risk.

### On the current positions
- **To the task force:** if you want our funding, then domestic plant protection is not optional. We will not underwrite a rescue that leaves our industrial base weaker than before.
- **To management:** “viability” cannot be used as a blanket justification for offshoring production or concentrating cuts in our region.
- **To labour:** we support a settlement that preserves jobs, but it has to be one that can actually be financed.
- **To bondholders:** you are right that sacrifice must be shared. But claims on the paper capital structure cannot trump the real industrial footprint that is at stake.

### Our line
We are open to:
- co-funding,
- restructuring of labour and creditor claims,
- and even an expedited court-supervised route if needed.

But we are **not** open to funding without:
- binding domestic production commitments,
- protection for the plants in our jurisdictions,
- and an ownership stake proportionate to our contribution.

If those conditions are not met, we will not support the rescue.

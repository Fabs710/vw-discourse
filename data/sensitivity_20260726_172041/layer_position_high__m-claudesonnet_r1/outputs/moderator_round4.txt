# Moderator Summary — Round 4

## 1. What Has Been Agreed (Convergence)

- **Timeline:** Broad convergence around a **36–48 month implementation window** with a **front-loaded six-month first checkpoint**. Management holds a distinct position at 30–36 months.
- **Checkpoint trigger:** Works Council, Union, State, and Family Holding converge on **two consecutive adverse checkpoints** triggering an accelerated board return with renegotiation authority. Investors propose a hybrid: one adverse checkpoint triggers mandatory expedited review with disclosure; two consecutive triggers deeper renegotiation authority.
- **Checkpoint consequence (content):** Broad agreement that an accelerated return authorizes deeper working-time measures, increased capital/executive contribution, and expanded state instruments — **not** a closure resolution, per Works Council/State/Family Holding; Management and Investors dispute this exclusion (see below).
- **Sunset/reversal clause:** Union and Management have converged on **sustained, independently verified return-on-sales at target over a defined period** as a binding (non-discretionary) trigger, replacing single-quarter or open-ended formulations.
- **Evidentiary-exhaustion bar:** Works Council, Union, Management, and State agree to co-author this jointly, on the explicit condition that it documents what was attempted and does **not** function as an automatic authorization for closure. Union adds a specific criterion: exhaustion cannot be satisfied by hollowing out the collective agreement in substance.
- **New quantified commitments this round:**
  - Family Holding: provisional dividend reduction range of **25–40%**, scaled proportionately to management pay cuts, revisited at checkpoints.
  - State Government: co-financing package in the **"low-to-mid hundreds of millions of euros"** for site conversion/retraining, contingent on sites remaining open, subject to EU state-aid constraints.

## 2. Core Disagreements Remaining

- **The central fracture this round:** Works Council stated unconditionally that **no evidentiary outcome will produce board consent to closure**. Management responded by (a) stating the non-closure package must now close a much larger share of the gap, and (b) announcing intent to bring a closure-inclusive resolution to a public board vote regardless of expected outcome, and (c) naming investment/product-allocation decisions as a fallback outside the board's veto reach.
- **State Government and Family Holding have explicitly rejected Management's response** as an escalation — respectively characterizing a foreordained-to-fail public vote as a "campaign tactic" and product-allocation attrition as "circumvention of co-determination." Investors have sided with State/Family Holding on this specific point, describing de facto closure via ambiguity as an unpriceable, worse outcome than a negotiated one.
- **Management has not withdrawn either position** (the public vote intention or the naming of the fallback) as of this round; this remains an open, named disagreement rather than a resolved one.
- **Union has stated a parallel, independent red line:** the strike weapon and collective wage agreement are not reachable by any board vote outcome, and a peace obligation would lapse if closure were somehow imposed.
- **Timeline gap:** Management (30–36 months) vs. everyone else (36–48 months) remains unresolved.
- **Checkpoint trigger gap:** Investors' one-checkpoint disclosure mechanism vs. others' two-checkpoint threshold remains unresolved.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **Aggregate arithmetic:** Investors have posed the explicit open question — does the sum of all parties' maximum credible contributions (labour package + dividend cut + management pay cut + state instruments) actually reach parity with the identified gap, or does it structurally fall short once closure is confirmed unavailable? No party has yet produced this consolidated figure.
- **Family Holding's dividend range is provisional**, pending formal board process — not yet a final commitment.
- **State's co-financing figure is a range category**, not yet itemized against EU state-aid rules or tied to specific instruments (retraining vs. conversion vs. other).
- **Management has not yet specified** what "further capacity consolidation" or "deeper working-time measures" concretely means in numbers, if the non-closure package must now close a larger share of the gap.
- **No party has addressed** what happens if the verified aggregate, post-maximum-effort, still falls short — Investors flagged this as the decisive unresolved question; it has not yet been engaged directly by Management, State, or Family Holding in these terms.

## 4. Refocus for Round 5

1. **Management:** Clarify — is the intention to bring a foreordained closure vote, and to treat non-renewal of product/investment as a closure fallback, a settled strategy or a position open to reconsideration given the State's and Family Holding's explicit rejection of it? This needs a direct answer before other issues can be resolved cleanly.
2. **All parties:** Produce the consolidated aggregate arithmetic Investors requested — do combined maximum contributions actually close the gap, and if not, what is the honest size of the residual?
3. **State Government:** Itemize co-financing instruments with figures and state-aid-compliant structure.
4. **Family Holding:** Move the dividend range from provisional to committed, per the board process timeline.
5. **Resolve the two outstanding numeric gaps:** timeline (30–36 vs. 36–48 months) and checkpoint trigger (one vs. two adverse checkpoints).

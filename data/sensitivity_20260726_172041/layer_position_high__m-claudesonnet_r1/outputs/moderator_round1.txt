# Moderator Summary — End of Round 1

## 1. Points of Emerging Agreement
- **All parties** accept that a real cost/competitiveness gap exists (EV timing, China competition, shrinking European market) and that it must be addressed substantively, not cosmetically.
- **All parties** accept, in principle, the idea of an **independent, transparent process** to establish and verify the cost-gap methodology and score any proposed labour contribution against it (proposed by the State, explicitly backed by the Family Holding and Institutional Investors; not yet responded to directly by Works Council or Union).
- **Works Council and Union** have both offered a **quantified-in-principle** (though not yet numerically specified) employee contribution: bonus deferral, pay-increase pauses, working-time/capacity adjustments.
- **Management and Family Holding** have both stated openness to discussing **executive compensation restraint** and **dividend policy** as part of a package, without yet committing to figures.
- **No party has ruled out** a negotiated outcome that avoids closures *if* an alternative package is credible and durable — including Management and the Family Holding, who state they are not ideologically committed to closures as an instrument.

## 2. Core Disagreements Remaining
- **Sequencing**: Works Council and Union want binding site/job guarantees *before or alongside* finalizing savings measures; Management wants quantified savings demonstrated *first*, with guarantees following; the State wants both negotiated *in parallel* within one package.
- **Nature of the red line**: Works Council treats no-closures/no-compulsory-redundancies as absolute and non-negotiable; Management and Investors treat it as something that must remain conditional on the numbers; the State and Family Holding occupy a middle position (strong preference against closures, but not an unconditional veto).
- **Whose numbers govern**: No agreement yet on who defines "closing the gap" — Management's internal figures are contested by Works Council, Union, State, and Investors alike as needing independent verification.
- **Weight of "shared sacrifice"**: Union and Works Council treat proportional contribution from capital/management as a condition of legitimacy; Management and Investors treat it as marginal to the actual size of the required fix, not a substitute for closing the gap.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Not Yet Addressed
- No actual **numbers** on the table yet: no cost-gap figure, no quantified labour contribution, no executive/dividend restraint figure. Round 1 was entirely framework and conditions.
- No agreement on **timeline** for the independent scoring process, or who administers/selects it.
- No discussion yet of **what happens if the independent number falls between** "closes the gap" and "a fraction of the gap" — i.e., a partial-credit scenario.
- No treatment yet of **strike escalation timing** relative to the process — Union has stated the peace obligation has ended; Investors and State have flagged concerns about strikes damaging the process itself, but no proposal on how to sequence talks against ongoing/escalating industrial action.
- No specification of **which plants** or how many jobs are actually in scope beyond "those currently on the table" — scope of the closure list itself remains undefined.

## 4. Refocus for Round 2
To move from positioning to substance, the next round should address:
1. **Process mechanics**: Who designs and runs the independent cost-gap verification and contribution-scoring process, and on what timeline?
2. **First numbers**: Can Union/Works Council table an initial quantified contribution range, and can Management table the cost-gap figure/methodology it's working from, even provisionally?
3. **Sequencing compromise**: Is there a workable middle path between "guarantees first" and "substance first" — e.g., conditional or milestone-based guarantees tied to verified savings?
4. **Strike posture**: Whether the Union is willing to frame any near-term escalation relative to the launch of the independent process, without abandoning its leverage.

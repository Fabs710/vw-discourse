# Moderator Summary — End of Round 2

## 1. Areas of Agreement

- **Sequencing:** Broad convergence on "process first, simultaneous signature at the end" — the joint costing process runs, and once it produces a validated number, the guarantee and the workforce contribution are signed together in the same instrument. Works Council and Union have not explicitly endorsed this revised sequencing (see disagreements below), but Management, State Government, Family Holding, and Investors have converged on it.
- **Transparency:** Full, unfiltered access to management's cost model — including plant-level capacity utilization, product allocation history, and cost-per-vehicle assumptions — is now agreed across all six parties, including board-level access (not summary decks) for State Government seats.
- **Testing discipline applied symmetrically:** All parties agree that both the closure option and the labour-side levers (working-time reduction, voluntary separation, etc.) must be tested with equal rigor, on a *net*, not gross, savings basis.
- **Capital-side contribution now has shape:** Family Holding has committed to bringing a concrete proposal (dividend growth freeze weighted toward its own stake; management variable pay tied to the same metrics as labour/guarantee) to the supervisory board in parallel with the costing process, not after it. Investors have accepted the income effect on themselves as a condition of this being credible.
- **Timeline outer boundary:** "Weeks, not months" for the costing process; two-to-three years cited as the outer boundary for the bulk of savings to materialize, provisionally accepted by Management and flagged by Investors as a ceiling, not a target to relitigate upward.
- **Warning strikes continuing during the process is tolerated** by Management as consistent with good faith, provided the process is genuinely running (real model access, real timeline, real numbers from all sides).

## 2. Core Disagreements Still Open

- **Sequencing, precisely:** Works Council and Union have *not* accepted "guarantee signed only after process concludes" — their stated position remains guarantee-and-contribution negotiated and signed together *throughout*, not contingent on a management-validated endpoint. This is not yet resolved; it has been reframed by other parties as resolved, but not confirmed by the two parties whose consent is required.
- **Closures remaining "live" during the process:** Management and Family Holding insist this stays on the table as negotiating leverage until a validated alternative exists. Works Council, Union, and State Government treat closures as foreclosed by board arithmetic regardless of process outcome. This gap is unresolved and consequential — it shapes how each side reads the good faith of the other.
- **Strike escalation conditionality:** Union will maintain warning strikes but reserves the right to escalate immediately and "harder" if good faith breaks down; Management states that indefinite/all-out escalation before conclusion would itself change its willingness to sign an enforceable guarantee. Neither side has defined the trigger point precisely.
- **Size and character of the capital contribution:** A shape has been offered (dividend growth freeze, variable pay linkage), but no figure. Investors have flagged the risk of this being sized as symbolic rather than proportionate; Union and State Government have both said a concrete number, not just a mechanism, is required this round.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Not Yet Addressed

- **What "closing the gap" means numerically:** No party has yet put an actual figure — the size of the structural cost gap, the size of the capital contribution, or the net (post-compensation) savings from the four labour-side levers — on the table. All commitments so far are architectural, not quantified.
- **What happens if the joint process validates only partial closure of the gap** (Investors raised this explicitly — e.g., 60% closure via alternative levers): no party has addressed what the fallback is if the number is real but insufficient.
- **The relationship between the State Government's veto commitment and Management's insistence on closures staying live:** both statements have been made forcefully but their interaction — what specifically happens at the board table if the process outcome is contested — has not been worked through.
- **Timeline anchor date:** Investors explicitly asked for a specific calendar date for when the joint number will be published; this has not yet been supplied by State Government or Management.

## 4. Refocus for Round 3

Three concrete asks carried forward, directed at specific parties:

1. **State Government and Management:** Name an actual date (not "weeks") by which the joint costing process concludes and the validated number is published.
2. **Family Holding:** Bring an actual figure — not mechanism alone — for the dividend and compensation commitment, sized in a way that Union and Investors can evaluate for proportionality.
3. **Works Council and Union:** Respond directly to whether "process first, simultaneous signature at conclusion" is acceptable, or whether simultaneous signature throughout remains a firm condition — this is the one sequencing question presented by other parties as resolved that has not actually been confirmed by the two parties who raised it.

All parties should come prepared to discuss net, dated figures rather than categories or mechanisms.

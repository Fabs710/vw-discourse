# Moderator Summary — Round 3

## 1. What Has Been Agreed

- **Model access**: Management commits to opening full plant-level cost data (cost-per-vehicle, utilization, product allocation) to works council and union **this week**.
- **Calendar**: A **six-week window** from model access to a jointly validated, published number. State Government co-chairs this timeline formally.
- **Independent audit**: Broad, cross-party support for a jointly commissioned, independently certified validation of the final model — rather than a number produced unilaterally by management or contested unilaterally by labour. Family Holding has committed to direct its board representatives to support this formally.
- **Family Holding figure**: A percentage range (not final figure) on dividend freeze/variable pay linkage to be reported by **week three**, sized against a proportionality test. State Government will raise this as a formal board agenda item if it slips.
- **Sequencing**: Convergence around "process-first, signature-together-at-conclusion" — one legally binding instrument (per the union's request), conditional on genuine co-authorship of assumptions and shared validation rights over the model.
- **Reframing of closure**: Management has shifted from "closure as live pressure during negotiation" to "closure as a board option only if the certified process shows the alternative genuinely doesn't close the gap." Works council, union, state government, and investors have all acknowledged this as a meaningful, if not fully resolving, shift.
- **Net figures**: Works council and union agree to produce net, dated figures on working-time, voluntary separation, and deferred comp — conditional on model access — inside the six-week window. Management accepts this must be measured net of offsets, by plant.
- **Capacity measures short of closure**: All parties now accept that footprint/shift/capacity reduction options between "full closure" and "status quo" must be tested rigorously before closure is treated as the only residual lever.

## 2. Core Disagreements Remaining

- **Scope of co-authorship**: Works council/union want joint sign-off on assumptions *before* a number is called validated. Management and Family Holding accept co-authorship of assumptions but explicitly reject any veto over the conclusion a certified model produces. This boundary is stated but not yet tested in practice.
- **The auditor's mandate**: Investors have raised a specific unresolved question — does the independent auditor certify only the *size of the gap*, or also whether a given package *closes* it? This is unanswered.
- **The veto's conditionality**: Investors have posed a direct, unanswered question to the State Government: if a certified process shows a residual gap that no non-closure measure can close, does the veto hold unconditionally regardless of the number, or is it conditional on the number itself? This is the single most consequential open question flagged this round.
- **Capital figure timing/size**: Family Holding has committed to a week-three range but has not committed to a number today; investors and union have flagged that the figure will be judged strictly against proportionality to the labour-side ask, not in isolation.
- **Strike posture**: Union's warning strikes continue in parallel; escalation to indefinite action is conditioned on real model access, a dated conclusion, and a dated capital figure all materializing without vagueness. No change in position, but the conditions are now more precisely specified than in Round 2.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **The "floor" question**: What happens if the certified gap is only partially closed (e.g., 60%) and rigorous testing of non-closure measures still leaves a residual gap? No party has yet stated whether the veto holds absolutely in that scenario or is itself conditional on exhausting every alternative.
- **Auditor selection and mandate design**: Who selects and pays the auditor, and what precisely is certified (gap size only, vs. gap size *and* closure adequacy)?
- **Proportionality benchmark**: No agreed method yet exists for judging whether the Family Holding's week-three figure is "proportionate" to the labour-side contribution — this will need a concrete comparative basis when the number arrives.
- **Legal form of the single instrument**: Union's demand for one legally binding collective agreement covering both guarantee and contribution has been accepted in principle by the Family Holding as a drafting matter, but not yet worked through with management or the state government.

## 4. Refocus for Next Round

1. **State Government**: Directly answer the investors' floor question — is the veto conditional on the certified number, or unconditional regardless of outcome?
2. **All parties**: Define the independent auditor's mandate and selection process — gap certification only, or gap *and* closure certification.
3. **Family Holding**: Confirm the week-three reporting commitment stands as scheduled; begin framing the proportionality test explicitly.
4. **Works Council / Union**: Begin specifying what "genuine co-authorship of assumptions" looks like procedurally, now that model access is starting — to reduce ambiguity before the six-week clock runs.
5. **Management**: Bring initial visibility, as available, on which capacity/footprint measures short of closure will be tested, to give the joint process concrete scope early rather than late in the window.

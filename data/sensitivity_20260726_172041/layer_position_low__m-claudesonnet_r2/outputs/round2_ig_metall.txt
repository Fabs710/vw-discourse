**On the "reviewable guarantee" drift — I back the Betriebsrat's line without qualification.** The state government, family holding, and investors all used language this round that needs to be pinned down before it hardens into board text. I want a direct answer from management and the family holding on this, not a restatement of the general principle. If your version of "reviewable" includes a closure trigger, say so now, because that changes what we're negotiating entirely — from a settlement to a deferred ultimatum, and we will not sign a deferred ultimatum.

I will not put a final figure on the table in this room before it's been scored against your actual cost data, because an unscored number is exactly the kind of "category dressed as a plan" you rightly criticized us for last round, and I won't hand you a number I can't defend to my own members with the arithmetic behind it. That scoring happens this week, as the Betriebsrat offered. That is not a delay tactic — it's the difference between a durable deal and a number that unravels under scrutiny in month four.

**On the family holding and investors' point about the cost of an eighteen-month closure fight — take that seriously, because it's not a threat, it's arithmetic on our side too.** We have already led warning strikes since the peace obligation lapsed. If management or the board moves toward closures, this does not stay at warning strikes. Full-scale industrial action across every affected site, coordinated with the works council's board vote, is the next step — and I'd ask the investors in this room to price that scenario with the same rigor you're demanding of our job numbers. The capital allocation into an EV strategy that hasn't delivered was a management and board decision, not a shop-floor decision, and the fact that you won't concede that point doesn't make the fairness question go away — it just means we keep bringing it to every session until the top-of-house contribution is more than "proportionate and symbolic." **On investors' repricing threat — understood, and I'd say the same thing back.** You'll reprice against a "cosmetic settlement."

**On the union's point that a ratification vote will fail if closure language remains — I take this seriously as a fact about your members, and I still don't think it changes what I sign.**

You know your membership better than I do, and I'm not going to argue with you about what they'll ratify. **On the works council's "you're the one hedging, not us" argument — I'll answer it directly rather than let it stand as a gotcha.**

Yes. I am hedging. I've never claimed otherwise. **On the union's challenge — "if you won't drop it, you don't trust the mechanism" — no. I don't trust the future. Those are different things.**

I trust the self-scaling contribution as designed, based on what I've seen this round — it's the best instrument produced in this discussion and I said so last round.

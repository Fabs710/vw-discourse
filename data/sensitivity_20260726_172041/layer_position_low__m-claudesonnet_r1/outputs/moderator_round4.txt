# Moderator Summary — Round 4

## 1. Areas of Agreement

- **Closure guarantee:** Management has offered an unconditional no-closure commitment through 2030, removing the conditionality objected to in Round 3. Works Council, Union, State Government, and Family Holding have all indicated support for writing this into the resolution.
- **Compulsory redundancy:** All parties now accept a floor of no compulsory redundancies at any stage. Management has conditioned this on voluntary/structural tools (attrition, severance, redeployment, working-time adjustment) delivering the required headcount reduction, with an agreed fallback (extended timeline, deeper working-time measures) rather than reopening compulsion if voluntary uptake falls short.
- **Graduated-response mechanism:** Broad convergence around a two-cycle structure (12–18 months each) with binding factual determination by a joint body. State Government has requested formal participation as a party (not observer) in this process; no objection recorded yet.
- **Dividend/executive-pay recovery symmetry:** Management has committed executive compensation recovery to the same performance triggers as workforce wage recovery. Family Holding has offered dividend-recovery symmetry bounded to the term of this agreement (through 2030), reverting to ordinary board judgment thereafter. Investors have endorsed this formulation as structurally sound.
- **Plant-level breakdown:** Management has committed to deliver a full site-by-site, cost-category breakdown before the next session.
- **Non-labour lever sequencing:** Management has accepted the principle that non-labour savings should be sized and locked before further labour-side scaling is requested, and has given an indicative range (quarter to a third of the gap, phased over 2–3 years).

## 2. Core Disagreements Still Open

- **Product/platform allocation as a substitute accountability lever:** Management has disclosed that it intends to retain investment/product allocation decisions as a real consequence mechanism for missed milestones. Works Council and Union have not yet responded directly to this specific proposal; State Government and Family Holding support subjecting it to joint-body transparency; Investors oppose any construction that gives the joint body de facto influence over capital allocation decisions. The boundary between "visibility" and "co-management" is undefined.
- **Named consequence for a failed second milestone cycle:** Works Council and Union have proposed that the consequence be confined to further labour/capital renegotiation and non-labour measures. Management's countered consequence is deprioritization in product/investment allocation. Whether labour accepts this substitution has not yet been confirmed.
- **Dividend symmetry scope:** Family Holding's offer is time-bounded to the agreement's term; State Government has stated it will not treat the symmetry question as resolved until it hears Works Council's and Union's response to this specific limitation.

## 3. Unaddressed Trade-offs

- Whether the sum of the labour package, the indicative non-labour lever, and any further measures actually closes the stated cost gap on a credible timeline — Investors have flagged this as still unresolved and the determining factor in whether they will treat the deal as investable.
- The split between structural and utilization-driven cost within the "~20%" figure has not yet been delivered, despite repeated requests from Works Council, Union, State Government, and Investors.
- The precise mechanics of the fallback if voluntary redundancy measures underperform (extended timeline vs. deeper working-time reduction vs. redeployment scale) remain to be specified rather than named in principle.

## 4. Refocus for Round 5

Participants should prioritize:
1. **Works Council and Union response** to (a) management's product-allocation consequence as a substitute for closure-linked leverage, and (b) the Family Holding's time-bounded dividend-symmetry offer.
2. **Management and State Government** to jointly define where "joint-body transparency" on product allocation ends and ordinary capital-allocation authority begins.
3. **Delivery of firm numbers:** the plant-level cost breakdown and a non-indicative figure for the non-labour lever, enabling a direct test of whether the combined package closes the stated gap.
4. Confirmation from Union and Works Council on whether, given the numbers once delivered, the labour package is prepared to scale further.

# Moderator Summary — End of Round 1

## 1. Areas of Emerging Agreement
- **No party has endorsed a closure list as a starting position.** Management, the Family Holding, and Institutional Investors all state they are "agnostic on mechanism" — open to alternatives to closure provided they close the competitiveness gap.
- **All parties accept the underlying problem is real**: German cost base is structurally uncompetitive, the market is shrinking, and return-on-sales has been missing targets for multiple cycles.
- **Broad support for a "shared sacrifice" principle in concept** — Works Council, Union, State Government, and (with more caveats) Family Holding and Investors all accept some form of proportional contribution from management/ownership alongside any workforce contribution. Investors specifically flag executive pay as an easy, low-cost signal.
- **Convergence on the need for a jointly designed, costed alternative package** rather than sequential ultimatums — though parties disagree sharply on sequencing (see below).
- **General preference for avoiding prolonged confrontation**: Family Holding and Investors explicitly frame strikes/deadlock as value-destructive to all sides, not just leverage for management.

## 2. Core Unresolved Disagreements
- **Sequencing**: Works Council/Union/State Government want binding site and job guarantees *first* (or in parallel, jointly reviewed) as the precondition for negotiating workforce contributions. Management, Family Holding, and Investors want a *sized, costed alternative package first*, treating guarantees as conditional on delivered performance.
- **Nature of guarantees**: Labour side wants guarantees that are binding and not unilaterally revocable. Management wants guarantees tied explicitly to capacity/cost delivery, with real consequences if targets slip. State Government has proposed a "shared-risk, jointly reviewed" middle structure, but its enforcement mechanics remain undefined.
- **Magnitude and specificity**: No numbers have yet been put on the table by labour side (contribution size, capacity reductions, sites/timelines). Management and Investors have explicitly flagged this absence as the central credibility gap.
- **Scope of capital/management sacrifice**: Union and State Government treat proportional owner/executive contribution as a *precondition* for any workforce concession. Management and Family Holding resist this as a sequencing condition, and Investors distinguish between (acceptable) executive pay cuts and (resisted) dividend suspension as a substitute for structural savings.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed
- What specific **capacity reductions** (which sites, which lines, what volume) might substitute for closures — not yet named by anyone.
- What **magnitude of wage/working-time contribution** the union/works council are actually prepared to propose, and over what timeframe.
- What **enforcement mechanism** would apply if a negotiated package underperforms — none of the parties has proposed concrete terms (triggers, review points, consequences).
- What **specific proportional contribution** from executive compensation and dividend policy would be considered adequate rather than symbolic.
- How **binding guarantees** and **performance conditionality** can be structurally reconciled — this is the crux the whole negotiation currently pivots on, and no bridging proposal exists yet.

## 4. Refocus for Round 2
Parties should move from position statements to **specifics**. Concretely, the next round should aim to surface:
- From **Works Council / Union**: an actual first-pass number — indicative scale of capacity reduction and wage/working-time contribution they are prepared to put forward.
- From **Management**: the size of the competitiveness gap in concrete terms (€ or % cost reduction needed) and what a "credible timeline" means in practice.
- From **State Government / Family Holding**: a more concrete sketch of what a "shared-risk, jointly reviewed" guarantee structure could look like — triggers, review intervals, consequences.
- From **all parties**: initial reactions to proportional management/capital contribution — what specifically might be offered (executive pay, dividend policy, capital investment commitments).

The goal for Round 2 is to begin narrowing from stated principles toward a structure — even provisionally — that could hold both a guarantee mechanism and a performance mechanism together.

# Moderator Summary — Round 2

## (1) Areas of Convergence / Agreement

- **Numbers are now on the table.** The Union has specified a concrete package: mid-single-digit % reduction in effective labour costs over 3–4 years, via deferred tariff increases, working-time reduction with partial pay compensation, restructured bonus/profit-sharing, plus shift reductions, accelerated attrition, and a meaningfully sized voluntary severance program. The Works Council has endorsed this framework and added specifics on capacity tools (redeployment, line-specific shift cuts).
- **Management has moved on enforcement design.** It now accepts that unilateral management judgment on target-delivery is not credible, and has offered joint design of review criteria, works council/union participation in evaluation, and shared transparent data — a shift from its Round 1 position.
- **Management and Family Holding have both signaled willingness on capital-side contribution**: proportional executive compensation reduction (management), and a principle that capital contribution will scale in step with labour's actual package size, plus dividend policy review tied to the same performance horizon (Family Holding).
- **A "standing joint oversight body" (state, works council, union, management) has emerged as a candidate mechanism**, proposed by the State Government, viewed by multiple parties (Family Holding, Investors) as preferable to external arbitration — provided design questions are resolved.
- **Broad acknowledgment** that asymmetric sacrifice (labour quantified, capital vague) is not viable — stated explicitly by Union, State Government, and Investors, and not contested by Management or Family Holding.

## (2) Core Disagreements Still Open

- **Sequencing:** Works Council/Union want binding site guarantees with joint/arbitration enforcement locked in *before or alongside* delivery of numbers. Management resists binding, unconditional guarantees preceding a functioning delivery mechanism, calling this unfinanceable.
- **Tie-breaking authority:** Unresolved who decides when a joint review body disagrees. Works Council wants external arbitration; Management rejects this as too slow and undermining enforceability; State Government proposes a standing joint body instead but has not secured agreement on its tie-break power; Family Holding insists any such body must report *into*, not sit above, the supervisory board.
- **Whether closures remain a live parallel track.** Family Holding declines to permanently foreclose closures as a stewardship matter; State Government, Works Council, and Union treat this as incompatible with genuine partnership and reject it as a live fallback.
- **Sizing adequacy:** Management states the union's package, as currently sized, does not close the competitiveness gap — but has not yet quantified that gap despite being asked directly by State Government, Family Holding, Union, and Investors.

## (3) Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **Management's quantified gap figure** — repeatedly requested, not yet provided. Without it, no party can assess whether the union's package (as is, or scaled up) is sufficient.
- **Family Holding's actual dividend/compensation number** — principle stated, figure deferred pending final package size; Investors and Union want this disclosed simultaneously with labour's figures, not after.
- **Design mechanics of the standing joint body** — composition, tie-break process, timeline for corrective action (Investors want "weeks not years"), and its legal relationship to the supervisory board remain unresolved.
- **What "real consequences" in a bidirectional review actually look like in practice** — neither management nor others have specified what happens, concretely, if milestones are missed or exceeded on either side.

## (4) Refocus for Round 3

1. **Management: provide the quantified competitiveness gap** (percentage or euro figure) that any package is being measured against — this is now a blocking item for every other party.
2. **Family Holding: indicate the mechanism and rough scale/timing for disclosing the capital-side figure**, even if the final number awaits package completion.
3. **All parties: respond concretely to the standing joint oversight body proposal** — specifically its tie-break mechanism, timeline for corrective action, and relationship to the supervisory board's authority.
4. **Family Holding and Management: clarify directly whether closures remain an active parallel consideration during this process**, given the State Government's explicit request for an unambiguous answer.

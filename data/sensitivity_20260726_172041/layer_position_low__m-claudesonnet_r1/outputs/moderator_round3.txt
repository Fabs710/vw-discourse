# Moderator Summary — Round 3

## 1. What Has Been Agreed (or Substantially Converged)

- **The gap number is now on record.** Management has stated a structural cost gap of "in the order of 20%" on unit production cost, requiring "several billion euros annually" in sustained savings — the first quantification offered after three rounds of requests. Management has acknowledged withholding it earlier was an error.
- **Binding factual determination on milestones.** Management, Works Council, Union, State Government, Family Holding, and Investors all now converge on: the joint oversight body's determination of *whether* milestones were delivered should be binding and self-executing, not advisory. Consequences flowing from that determination (capital allocation, strategy) remain with the supervisory board.
- **A time-bound, conditional closure/redundancy guarantee.** Management has offered a binding commitment through 2030 — no closures, no compulsory redundancies — conditional on jointly-verified delivery, rather than "no current intention." Family Holding supports this formulation specifically.
- **A "second lever."** Management has introduced non-labour savings (procurement, platform consolidation, fixed-cost measures) as a supplementary path to closing the gap. State Government, Family Holding, and Investors have all endorsed pursuing this "hard and now," calling it the least contentious source of savings in the package.
- **Dividend calculation method stated.** Family Holding has committed to a method matching management's executive-pay standard: percentage reduction from baseline dividend, no smaller than labour's percentage contribution, during the adjustment period — with a stated qualification that this is not a permanent cap once the business recovers.
- **Graduated response to missed milestones, in principle.** State Government, Family Holding, and Investors converge on: a missed milestone should trigger renegotiation before closures re-enter consideration, not automatic reversion — with Investors and Family Holding both insisting this must be a *fixed, finite* number of cycles, not open-ended.

## 2. Where Core Disagreements Remain

- **Precision of the gap figure.** State Government, Union, Works Council, and Investors all consider "in the order of 20%" / "several billion" too imprecise to model or bargain against. A plant-by-plant, cost-category breakdown has been demanded before round four closes.
- **Scope of the closure guarantee.** Works Council and Union want an unconditional, delivery-tied removal of closures as a live option through 2030 — full stop. Management and Family Holding will only accept a guarantee conditional on jointly-verified delivery, explicitly rejecting a permanent, outcome-independent commitment.
- **What happens on a missed milestone.** The principle of "renegotiate before reversion" has support, but the critical unresolved question — posed sharply by State Government — is whether a missed milestone triggers automatic reopening of closures or a genuine second process. No party has yet specified the number of cycles, timeline per cycle, or what happens at the end of a failed graduated process.
- **Whether the current labour package, even scaled, closes the gap.** Management states a mid-single-digit labour cost reduction closes "a meaningful share" but not all of the stated 20% gap. Works Council and Union have not yet responded to whether/how far their package scales further, pending the plant-level breakdown.
- **Capital's comparability.** Investors and Union have accepted Family Holding's stated dividend method in principle but the qualification ("not a permanent cap once healthy") has not yet been tested against Union/Works Council's likely objection that this could allow dividends to rebound faster than wages.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **Breakdown vs. urgency:** A plant-by-plant, cost-category breakdown of the 20% figure is now demanded by four parties before further numbers can be scaled — this is a prerequisite, not a parallel task, and will consume round four if not delivered promptly.
- **Sizing the non-labour lever:** No party has yet put a number or timeline on procurement/platform savings, despite everyone agreeing it should be pursued "hard, not as a footnote."
- **The graduated-response mechanism's actual numbers:** cycle count, timeline per cycle, and the real consequence at the end remain undefined — flagged by State Government, Family Holding, and Investors as the single design element that determines whether the whole guarantee structure is credible or cosmetic.
- **Union/Works Council's scaling decision:** Whether labour's package increases further is explicitly contingent on the plant-level breakdown — not yet decided either way.
- **Dividend qualification's acceptability:** Family Holding's "not a permanent cap" qualifier has not yet been directly tested by Union or Works Council, who may view it as reopening the proportionality question just answered.

## 4. Refocus for Round Four

Round four should concentrate on turning round three's principles into numbers:

1. **Management** to provide the plant-by-plant, cost-category breakdown of the 20% gap, and a sized, time-lined proposal for the non-labour lever.
2. **Works Council/Union** to state, once the breakdown is available, whether and how their package scales — or to specify what would need to be true in the breakdown for it not to.
3. **All parties** to negotiate the graduated-response mechanism down to specifics: number of renegotiation cycles, length of each, and the defined consequence if delivery still fails at the end.
4. **Family Holding** to clarify how the "not a permanent cap" qualification on dividends interacts with the proportionality standard, anticipating Union/Works Council scrutiny.
5. **Works Council/Union** to respond directly to whether management's conditional (rather than unconditional) 2030 closure guarantee, now paired with binding fact-finding and a graduated-response mechanism, could meet their stated bottom line — or to specify precisely what remains missing.

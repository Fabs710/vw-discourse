# Moderator Summary — Round 4

## 1. What Has Been Agreed

- **Board vote mechanics clarified:** All parties now converge on describing the vote as conditional on the audited package closing the gap — Works Council, Management, Holding, and State Government each frame their position as contingent on the same test, though they disagree sharply on what follows if the test is only partially met (see below).
- **Line-level and numerical specificity delivered as promised:** Works Council provided plant-specific attrition/shift-consolidation detail; Union provided a modeled bonus-suspension range and solidarity-fund rationale tied to the consolidation mechanics; both parties committed to full figures at week one.
- **Management's baseline gap figure will be audited with equivalent scrutiny** to other parties' numbers — specifically, a transitional-versus-structural cost breakdown, delivered at week one alongside the site-by-site breakdown.
- **Discount methodology for State funding:** Agreement that the same auditor certifying the sunset trigger and wage-agreement condition should set the discount methodology using disclosed standard practice, rather than it being negotiated bilaterally — with Investors adding that the method must be published *before* application.
- **Wage-agreement trigger mechanism:** Union's proposal — auditor certification of the combined package closing the gap, with automatic entry into force on certification — was accepted by Management and Investors as specified.
- **Holding's directional signal:** The Holding stated it expects its eventual proportionality figure to land "at or above the standard, not comfortably below it." Investors, Union, and Works Council accepted this as genuine movement, while flagging it as now a testable commitment.

## 2. Core Disagreements Remaining

- **The "shortfall scenario" (the central live dispute):** Works Council maintains that any residual gap after the package is costed sends the parties "back to the model," with closure permanently excluded regardless of magnitude. Management, State Government, Holding, and Investors have each rejected this as an outcome-blind veto. State Government and Holding propose an alternative standard — closure as a last resort, contingent on the size of the residual gap and only after every genuine lever is exhausted — which Investors and Management have endorsed. Union has not yet stated a position on this reformulated standard.
- **Symmetry of veto logic:** Investors explicitly state they would view an outcome-blind workforce veto and an outcome-blind shareholder push for closure with equal skepticism — both sides have not yet reconciled on where "genuinely exhausted" is determined and by whom.
- **No agreed threshold** for what constitutes a "small, closeable" residual gap versus a "large, structural" one — this is the mechanism that would operationalize the State/Holding standard, and it remains undefined.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **Definition of the residual-gap threshold:** Investors have proposed the auditor define this now, before the actual number is known, to prevent post-hoc argument. No party has yet responded to this specific proposal.
- **Union's position on the revised shortfall standard:** The Union has not indicated whether it accepts the State/Holding/Investor formulation or remains aligned with the Works Council's absolute position — this alignment (or split) will materially affect the workforce bench's ten board votes.
- **Composition scrutiny of Management's gap figure:** Committed to, but not yet delivered — the transitional/structural split is pivotal to sizing every other contribution and has not yet been tested against real numbers.
- **Holding's figure remains unquantified**, even directionally in numeric terms — only a qualitative expectation has been offered.

## 4. Refocus for Next Round

1. **Union:** State clearly whether it accepts the State Government/Holding formulation on the shortfall scenario (last resort, contingent on magnitude) or maintains the Works Council's absolute exclusion — this is now the pivotal open question for workforce-side alignment.
2. **Auditor/task force design question for all parties:** Respond to Investors' proposal that a residual-gap threshold ("closeable" vs. "not closeable by any combination of levers") be defined in advance of week one, and who should propose it.
3. **Management:** Deliver the promised transitional-versus-structural composition breakdown of the baseline gap figure, as this materially affects the sizing of every other party's contribution.
4. **All parties:** Begin surfacing how "every genuine lever exhausted" would be evidenced and verified — this phrase now carries significant weight in three parties' positions but lacks a defined test.

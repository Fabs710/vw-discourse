# Moderator Summary — Round 2

## 1. What Has Been Agreed

- **Joint task force with independent auditor**: All parties support Management's proposal — Works Council, Union, State Government, and Family Holding representatives embedded alongside management, with an independent auditor adjudicating disputed assumptions rather than management unilaterally.
- **Timeline**: Six weeks to a jointly costed alternative model; twelve weeks to a formal proposal to the supervisory board. Accepted by Management, Investors, Family Holding, and (conditionally) State Government. Works Council and Union accept this pace *contingent on* genuine joint authorship from the outset.
- **Wage agreement treated as distinct**: Management has confirmed any bonus/variable-pay contribution will run through proper collective bargaining, not be imposed unilaterally plant-by-plant — addressing the Union's core red line on this point.
- **Sunset/recovery-trigger principle**: Broad agreement in principle that any labour-cost contribution should be time-limited and scale back if margins recover — though the mechanics remain unspecified (see below).
- **Executive pay / dividend restraint**: Family Holding has committed to bringing a defined proposal to the supervisory board within the same twelve-week window, mechanically linked and sunset-structured — a shift from "not ruling it out" to a committed mechanism and date, though without a figure.
- **State Government embedded in task force**: Management has not yet explicitly confirmed this; State Government has requested it as a condition of support.

## 2. Where Core Disagreements Remain

- **Unconditional site/job guarantee vs. conditional guarantee**: Works Council and Union hold that no-closures/no-compulsory-redundancies is a non-negotiable floor. Management, Family Holding, and Investors hold that any guarantee must remain contingent on the joint-costed package closing the structural gap. This gap is unchanged from Round 1.
- **Scope of the cost model**: State Government and Works Council want the joint model to account for the broader regional/social cost of closure (supplier ecosystem, regional contraction, conflict cost). Investors explicitly oppose folding these into the task force's financial comparison, arguing this pre-loads the model and that externalities belong in each stakeholder's own vote, not in a "clean" audited comparison. This is now a sharp, direct disagreement, not just a framing difference.
- **Board vote vs. task force process**: Family Holding has clarified the task force informs but does not substitute for the required two-thirds supervisory board vote — a governance point not yet reconciled with the State Government's and Works Council's expectation of being "co-authors" of the outcome itself.
- **Size/nature of shareholder contribution**: Family Holding has offered a mechanism and date but declined to name a figure; Investors and Union have both signaled that a merely symbolic contribution will not be accepted as equivalent to itemized workforce sacrifice.

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- **Precise sunset/recovery-trigger mechanics**: No party has yet specified the trigger metric, averaging period, or whether it applies symmetrically to labour contributions and to any dividend/executive-pay restraint. Investors have flagged this as a specific gap to close before the twelve-week mark.
- **How "incomplete" costs get weighed if not in the joint model**: If regional/social costs of closure are excluded from the task force's financial comparison (per Investors' objection), it remains unresolved *where* and *how* those costs enter the State Government's or Family Holding's ultimate board decision.
- **Materiality benchmark for shareholder contribution**: No agreed standard yet for what counts as "proportionate" shareholder/executive sacrifice relative to the workforce contribution — flagged by Investors and Union as a decisive test still to come.
- **What happens if the joint model's numbers are disputed at the margins**: The auditor-adjudication mechanism is agreed in principle, but no process yet exists for what happens if the auditor's findings are contested by a party unwilling to accept the result.

## 4. Refocus for Next Round

Next round should move from process architecture (largely settled) to **specification**:

1. Confirm State Government's embedded role in the task force explicitly (Management to answer).
2. Define the sunset/recovery-trigger mechanics precisely — metric, trigger threshold, averaging period, symmetry across labour and shareholder contributions.
3. Family Holding to indicate, if not a figure, at least the *proportionality standard* it intends to apply relative to the workforce contribution.
4. Resolve the still-open dispute over whether regional/social costs of closure are (a) part of the joint task force's model, (b) a separate input to the State Government's board vote only, or (c) excluded entirely — this needs an explicit decision, not continued parallel assertion.
5. Parties should begin bringing initial site-level volumes and euro figures for the alternative package, as promised, so the six-week costing model has real inputs to work with from day one.

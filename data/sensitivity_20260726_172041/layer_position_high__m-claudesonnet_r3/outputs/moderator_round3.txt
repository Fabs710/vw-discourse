# Moderator Summary — After Round 3

## 1. What Has Been Agreed

- **Disaggregation of the €4bn gap**: Management has provided a breakdown — ~€1.6bn direct labour cost, ~€1.5bn capacity-driven fixed-cost overhead, ~€0.9bn mixed logistics/tooling/site overhead. This will serve as the starting methodology for external verification.
- **Horizon**: Management has withdrawn 2028 and accepted **2030** as the framework horizon, conditional on narrow, objective, jointly-verified reopening triggers (percentage-based, tied to defined baselines).
- **Top-level contribution**: The Family Holding has converted its conditional support into a concrete commitment — the distribution freeze (10% compensation reduction plus freeze) will extend to **special distributions and share buybacks**, on the same horizon and triggers as the rest of the package.
- **Bounded process structure**: Broad convergence around a **sixteen-week sequence** — four weeks to finalize disaggregated figures and terms of reference, twelve weeks for joint costing of the full package (labour contribution plus the complete non-closure capacity menu: shifts, line idling, transfers, attrition, solidarity fund).
- **Co-design principle**: Agreement that Works Council and Union are full co-designers of the verification methodology for their own package; State and Family Holding have visibility/input into terms of reference but not veto over labour-side modeling.
- **Two-instrument framing**: Emerging agreement (Investors, Family Holding, State) that the labour package and capacity/footprint measures should be tested separately against their respective portions of the disaggregated gap, rather than one package judged against the whole €4bn.

## 2. Where Core Disagreements Remain

- **What a verified shortfall authorizes**: This is now the central fault line.
  - *Management*: A genuine, verified shortfall after the full menu is costed must allow the board to reach an actual decision, including capacity measures or closure — the process needs a terminus.
  - *State / Family Holding*: A shortfall is **information**, not authorization — it triggers renewed negotiation on further non-closure measures, not automatic closure eligibility.
  - *Investors*: Propose a **second bounded round** (6–8 weeks) of further non-closure measures if the first shortfall is confirmed; if shortfall persists after that, expect an actual decision, possibly including capacity measures.
  - *Works Council / Union*: Reject any framing where a partial shortfall converts toward closure eligibility at all; insist the full non-closure menu (including several specific levers) must be costed and exhausted first, with no predetermined endpoint that includes closure as a default option.
- **No closures / no compulsory redundancies**: Works Council and Union hold this position unconditionally; management holds that closure must remain a possible board outcome if the gap proves genuine and durable.

## 3. Key Trade-Offs Still Unaddressed

- **Trigger specificity**: Everyone has conditioned support on triggers being "tight" — but no actual thresholds have been drafted or reviewed jointly yet. This is a live gap, not just a formality.
- **Number of negotiation "tiers"**: Investors propose exactly two bounded rounds; State/Family Holding describe an open-ended return to negotiation; Works Council/Union resist any framing that presumes an endpoint. This gap has not been reconciled.
- **Union's wage-contribution figure**: Union has stated it will not price an actual percentage/duration until the disaggregated figure and full non-closure menu costing exist — meaning the labour-side numbers management and Investors are waiting on are still not on the table.
- **Ratification timing**: Union has flagged that the sequencing of the dividend commitment and labour concessions matters for a ratification vote — this is now resolved on paper (Family Holding closed it) but not yet tested against an actual mandate vote.

## 4. Refocus for Next Round

1. **Trigger design**: Produce draft, specific, percentage-based triggers (market decline threshold, milestone-shortfall threshold) for joint review — this was promised as parallel to the costing timeline but has not yet been tabled.
2. **Second-tier mechanism**: Resolve the gap between "information requiring renegotiation" (State/Family Holding/Works Council/Union) and "bounded second round with an eventual decision point" (Investors/Management). This is the single largest unresolved structural question and should be addressed directly rather than left to implication.
3. **Full non-closure menu costing**: Begin the parallel costing of shift reduction, line idling, attrition, transfers, and solidarity-fund extension alongside the labour package, per the sixteen-week timeline — with clear ownership of who costs what by when.
4. **Union's actual numbers**: Clarify what triggers the Union's commitment to submit a specific wage-contribution figure — is it the disaggregated figure alone (now available) or completion of terms of reference and co-design (still pending)?

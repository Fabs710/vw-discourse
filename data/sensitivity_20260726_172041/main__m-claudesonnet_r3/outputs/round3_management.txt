**Now — where I hold my ground, and I want to be exact about it.**

On "closing the gap doesn't default to layoffs or closures if it falls short" — I understand the appeal of this framing, but I will not accept it as a standing rule agreed in advance of seeing any numbers. A gap that's 90% closed by a durable package is a very different situation from a gap that's 40% closed. I will genuinely engage with extending measures if the shortfall is modest and the trajectory is credible. I will not commit today to treating a large, structural shortfall the same way. That distinction has to survive into the audit outcome, not be pre-empted by a blanket principle now. So: I'll agree that a shortfall does not automatically trigger a closure resolution the next board meeting — that's consistent with the reciprocal pause logic and I can extend that principle a reasonable distance into the post-audit period. I will not agree that closures are removed from the outcome space regardless of the audit's findings. That is the actual disagreement here, and I'd rather name it than let it sit disguised inside "extended measures."

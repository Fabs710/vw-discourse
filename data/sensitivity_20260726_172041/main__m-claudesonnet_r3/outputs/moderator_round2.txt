# Moderator Summary — End of Round 2

## 1. What Has Been Agreed

- **Process and timeline:** All six parties accept a **three-week window** for producing a joint, audited, like-for-like comparison between the closure case and a non-closure alternative package (attrition, working-time reduction, capped labour-cost contribution).
- **Joint methodology:** Agreement that both models must be built on shared methodology, agreed in advance, including externalities on both sides (severance, legacy obligations, litigation/reputational risk vs. lost output, competitive erosion, cost of continued instability), and that the State Government and Family Holding will see both models in parallel.
- **Reciprocal pause:** Management's offer of a time-boxed mutual pause (no board closure resolution during the three weeks, contingent on no further strike escalation) has been explicitly endorsed by Management, State, Family Holding, and Investors as the working structure, though not yet accepted by Works Council/Union.
- **Guarantee structure, in principle:** Convergence around guarantees with metrics fixed at signature, a narrow pre-agreed set of exceptional circumstances triggering renegotiation (not unilateral override), and a joint monitoring body — with the State insisting on its own inclusion in that body.
- **Sequencing principle:** Broad agreement that this should conclude as a **single, simultaneously signed/ratified package** rather than staggered concessions — though the Family Holding has specifically confirmed the *ownership/management contribution framework* will be presented **before** workforce ratification, not after.
- **Distinct instruments:** Some convergence (Investors, Family Holding, Management) that executive pay and dividend policy are functionally different levers, while Union/Works Council/State maintain both must be visibly, proportionately addressed as part of shared sacrifice.

## 2. Core Disagreements Remaining

- **Public vs. reciprocal-private suspension of closures:** Works Council and Union want a public, board-minuted statement taking closures off the table for the negotiation's duration; Management, State, Family Holding, and Investors favor the time-boxed reciprocal pause instead. This is unresolved and Union has flagged possible escalation over it.
- **Nature of "irrevocability" in guarantees:** Union wants guarantees irrevocable, full stop, once targets are met; Management wants revisitability under narrowly defined exceptional circumstances. The gap has narrowed but is not closed.
- **Dividend policy as quantified commitment vs. contingent framework:** Union/Works Council/State want a visible range/mechanism on record before ratification; Family Holding has agreed to sequencing but not committed to magnitude; Investors maintain dividend treatment should be functionally determined by capital needs, not symbolically matched to workforce concessions.
- **Whether closures are a genuine live option or a leverage device:** Not resolved — Management maintains closures remain a real fallback absent a credible package; Works Council/Union argue the board-vote arithmetic makes this a negotiating tool; State and Family Holding occupy a conditional middle position ("exhausted alternatives" standard).

## 3. Key Trade-offs Still Unaddressed

- No party has yet specified **what threshold or percentage of gap-closure** would be considered sufficient to avoid closures — this remains the central unresolved number.
- The **magnitude** (not just sequencing or mechanism) of ownership/executive contribution remains undefined.
- No agreement yet on **what happens if the three-week comparison is contested** (e.g., dueling assessments of "durability" or externality accounting) — an audit/arbitration mechanism has been referenced but not specified.
- The **relationship between strike activity and the pause's continuation** is unresolved — Union has reserved the right to escalate rather than de-escalate depending on how it reads Management's posture.

## 4. Refocus for Round 3

Round 3 should move from process architecture — now substantially agreed — to:
1. **Confirmation or rejection** by Works Council and Union of the reciprocal three-week pause structure, specifically addressing the State's direct question of whether the sticking point is substance or public framing.
2. **Concrete parameters for the joint audit**: who conducts independent verification if required, what constitutes "durability" (time horizon, sensitivity to demand/competitive shifts), and how disputes over the comparison will be resolved.
3. **Movement toward a magnitude or range** for ownership/executive contribution, even preliminary, to give the workforce parties something concrete to weigh against their own numbers.
4. Clarification of the **precise exceptional circumstances** that would permit guarantee renegotiation, so this can be tested rather than argued in the abstract.

The government task force’s position is straightforward:

We will support a restructuring only if it produces a viable carmaker, not a delay tactic.

That means three things are non-negotiable:

1. **Deep sacrifices from all stakeholders**
   - Management has already changed; the operating plan must now match the crisis.
   - Labour must accept meaningful concessions on labor cost and legacy obligations.
   - Unsecured creditors must take a substantial haircut.
   - Taxpayers cannot be the only party absorbing the loss.

2. **A credible balance-sheet and cost-structure reset**
   - We will not finance an unrestructured company.
   - Any proposal that simply bridges cash burn while leaving debt, retiree obligations, plants, brands, and overhead fundamentally intact is unacceptable.
   - The company must emerge with a cost base and capital structure that can survive without permanent public support.

3. **Speed and finality**
   - The 60-day window is real.
   - If stakeholders cannot agree quickly, we are prepared to use the court-supervised route to impose terms.
   - Open-ended bargaining, holdout behavior, or attempts to preserve legacy claims at taxpayer expense will not be rewarded.

What we are prepared to support:
- A fast restructuring with major plant and brand rationalization.
- Meaningful restructuring of the retiree health obligation and labor costs.
- A substantial exchange or write-down for unsecured bondholders.
- A clean ownership structure for the restructured company, with upside allocated only after private claimants have taken real losses.
- Co-funding from neighboring governments if it reduces taxpayer exposure and comes with enforceable conditions.

What we will not accept:
- An open-ended subsidy.
- A plan that leaves the company structurally unviable.
- A deal where one group is protected while others and the public purse bear the burden.
- Any attempt to stretch the process past the deadline in hopes of extracting a better payout.

Our objective is not consensus for its own sake. It is a viable company, at minimum credible taxpayer cost, achieved quickly and with sacrifice shared across all claimants.

### Core impasse
The deadlock is not about whether restructuring is needed. It is about **what counts as a legitimate restructuring outcome**.

The main split is between:
1. **Employee-side stakeholders**: restructuring yes, but **no closures, no compulsory redundancies, and binding site/job security**.
2. **Management / owners / investors**: restructuring yes, but only if it delivers a **durable structural cost reset**, which they say may require **closures, deep downsizing, or forced reductions**.

So the impasse is:
- **jobs/sites security vs. structural cost reduction**, and
- **a political/industrial red line vs. a financial/operational proof requirement**.

A second layer of impasse is procedural:
- employee-side stakeholders say **prove closure necessity plant by plant**;
- management-side stakeholders say **no-closure alternatives have not been shown to close the gap**;
- both sides are asking for **quantified proof**, but not yet agreeing on the **model, assumptions, or decision rule**.

---

### Facilitator compromise positions to test

#### 1) “No compulsory redundancies, but conditional capacity adjustment”
A possible bridge is:
- **no compulsory redundancies as a first principle**,
- but agreement on **voluntary exits, redeployment, retraining, working-time adjustments, and targeted product shifts**,
- combined with **site-specific capacity reduction** where needed.

This would avoid forced layoffs while still allowing meaningful footprint adjustment.

#### 2) “Protected footprint reduction with binding safeguards”
A second compromise is:
- no immediate full closures,
- but agreement to **downsizing/repurposing of selected sites** with:
  - guaranteed minimum operations,
  - investment commitments,
  - review milestones,
  - and co-determined safeguards against hollowing out.

This gives management some structural correction while addressing the fear of disguised closure.

#### 3) “Phased restructuring with hard data gate”
A third option is:
- implement an initial package now,
- then trigger a **joint independent plant-by-plant review** before any closure decision can be taken,
- with a pre-agreed rule that closure is only considered if the review shows non-closure measures cannot reach a defined savings threshold.

This preserves bargaining space and forces both sides onto the same evidence base.

### Pointed question to each stakeholder

#### To the Works Council
**If an independent plant-by-plant model shows that one or more sites cannot remain economically viable at current scale, what specific downsizing or repurposing options would you accept that still avoid closures and compulsory redundancies?**

#### To the Industrial Union
**What level of temporary employee contribution would you accept, and for how long, if management and owners simultaneously commit to no compulsory redundancies and binding investment and job-security guarantees?**

#### To Group & Brand Management
**What exact plant-by-plant data and threshold would you accept as sufficient to rule in or rule out closures, and would you commit to a non-closure option if it meets that threshold?**

#### To the Regional State Government
**Would the state support a protected downsizing/repurposing package if it preserves a majority of jobs and industrial capacity, even if it does not keep every site unchanged?**

#### To the Controlling Family Holding
**What tangible owner contribution are you prepared to put on the table before asking labour to accept any permanent structural change?**

#### To Institutional Investors
**Would you support a settlement that achieves lower margins than an aggressive closure case, if it materially reduces conflict risk and preserves execution certainty?**

#### To the Expert on Industrial Restructuring
**What minimum data set and decision rule would you use to determine whether a non-closure package is genuinely sufficient for 2030 competitiveness?**

#### To the Expert on Plant-Network Finance
**Under what exact conditions would you consider a non-closure package financially equivalent to closure or deep footprint reduction?**

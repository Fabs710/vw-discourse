1) **What has been agreed**
- The company faces a real competitiveness and cost-base problem.
- Any restructuring must be durable, not merely symbolic or temporary.
- Future investment matters, and prolonged conflict would be value-destructive.
- There is support in principle for an **independent plant-level comparison** of options.
- All sides acknowledge that plant-level data are needed before any final judgment.

2) **Where the core disagreements remain**
- **Closures and compulsory redundancies:** Labour and the state oppose them; management, investors, and the family holding say they must remain available if needed.
- **No-closure principle vs. conditional openness:** The employee and state side want German sites protected as a starting point; the ownership/management side reject any blanket pre-commitment.
- **Scope of employee contributions:** Labour insists they must be limited, temporary, reversible, and fair; management/owners say they may be insufficient unless structurally meaningful.
- **Role of the state:** The state wants a strong protective role for employment and sites; management/owners accept influence but not an effective veto over structural change.

3) **What key trade-offs are still unaddressed**
- The exact **savings gap** in euros and margin points is still not quantified.
- The relative contribution of **labor costs vs. overhead, procurement, and capital allocation** remains unclear.
- It is not yet shown whether a **site-preserving package** can permanently remove enough **fixed cost and excess capacity**.
- The comparison between:
  - a **site-preserving restructuring**, and
  - a **partial consolidation / closure-based package**
  has not yet been completed with plant-level data.
- The durability of proposed employee-side measures versus the structural savings from capacity reduction remains unresolved.

4) **Brief refocus for the next round**
- Keep the discussion on **numbers, not positions**.
- Use the independent auditor to produce a **side-by-side, plant-level comparison** of the two packages.
- Require a clear answer on:
  - savings gap,
  - fixed-cost removal,
  - capacity reduction,
  - implementation certainty,
  - and savings durability.
- After that comparison, the parties can test whether a no-closure solution is economically sufficient or whether deeper structural measures are actually required.

### Core impasse
The central impasse is **not** whether restructuring is needed. All sides now accept that it is.

The impasse is **what form “durable” restructuring must take**:

- **Employee side**: durable restructuring must be possible **without plant closures and without compulsory redundancies**, with binding site and job protection.
- **Management / capital side**: durable restructuring must produce **near-closure-level savings, high implementation certainty, and structural cost reduction**, and they are not willing to preclude closures or forced layoffs in advance.
- **State / family**: both are leaning toward **no automatic closures**, but only if a deep, economically credible alternative can be shown.

So the negotiation is stuck between:
1. **job/site protection as a hard condition**, and
2. **economic equivalence as a hard condition**.

The analyst’s input sharpened this: a **light** no-closure package likely fails, while a **deep hybrid** may work. That is the opening for compromise.

---

### Facilitator compromise proposals

#### Proposal 1: Deep hybrid restructuring with conditional closure fallback
A compromise could be structured as:

- **No immediate plant closure**
- **No compulsory redundancies as the first mechanism**
- **Permanent or multi-year capacity reduction through line/shift consolidation**
- **Voluntary exit program, early retirement, internal transfer, and non-renewal of fixed-term staff**
- **Multi-year labor-cost contribution**
- **Overhead and support-function cuts**
- **Binding investment commitments for German sites**
- **A clear fallback trigger**: if agreed savings, utilization, or headcount milestones are not met by fixed dates, a narrowly defined site measure can be reopened for review

This bridges:
- employee demands for no immediate closures,
- management’s insistence on measurable durability,
- and investors’ demand for enforceability.

---

#### Proposal 2: Site-preservation pact with quantified equivalence benchmark
A second compromise could be:

- **All German sites remain open for an agreed minimum period**
- In exchange, the workforce accepts:
  - temporary wage restraint,
  - longer working-time flexibility,
  - reduced special payments,
  - and voluntary reduction measures
- Management and capital must then prove, via an agreed model, that the package reaches a **specified equivalence threshold** versus consolidation
- If the threshold is not reached, the parties enter a second-stage negotiation on deeper structural measures

This bridges:
- the works council’s and union’s insistence on no-closure as a starting point,
- management’s demand for quantified savings,
- and the investor/family requirement for measurable comparison.

---

#### Proposal 3: Hybrid specialization instead of full closure
A third compromise is to replace “closure vs no closure” with **specialization vs duplication reduction**:

- No full plant shutdown
- But one or more sites are converted into **specialized production or support roles**
- Duplicate processes, shifts, and overhead layers are removed
- Workforce reductions occur primarily through voluntary and natural pathways
- Investment is concentrated on the retained core sites
- Co-determination gets formal monitoring rights over implementation

This bridges:
- the state’s goal of preserving regional industrial presence,
- management’s need for meaningful capacity reduction,
- and the workforce’s goal of avoiding outright site destruction.

**What specific package of capacity cuts, voluntary exits, working-time changes, and labor-cost contributions would you accept as economically sufficient if it preserves all German plants?**

#### To Industrial Union
**What is the maximum multi-year labor-cost contribution you are prepared to defend to members if management commits to no closures, no compulsory redundancies, and binding investment guarantees?**

#### To Group & Brand Management
**What exact savings gap remains after a deep hybrid package, and which elements of that gap cannot be closed without a site decision?**

#### To Regional State Government
**Will you support a conditional no-closure pact only if it includes objective milestones and a fallback mechanism, or do you require unconditional site protection?**

#### To Controlling Family Holding
**Would you back a hybrid restructuring if it preserves industrial presence but delivers slightly less savings than a consolidation case, provided the execution risk is lower?**

#### To Institutional Investors
**What minimum risk-adjusted NPV threshold must a deep hybrid package achieve before you would regard it as superior to consolidation?**

#### To the Industrial Restructuring Analyst
**Can you define, in concrete terms, the savings and headcount reduction threshold at which a no-closure package becomes a credible substitute for consolidation?**

#### To the Restructuring Finance Modeler

## Core impasse

The central blockage is **not** whether restructuring is needed. The impasse is over **the restructuring instrument mix**:

- **Employee side** wants: no closures, no compulsory redundancies, strong site security, and burden-sharing before any footprint reduction.
- **Management and capital side** want: a quantified, durable cost reset that may require plant closures or equivalent permanent footprint reduction.
- **State and family** sit between: they reject abrupt damage to jobs and regional stability, but do not want to lock in an uneconomic structure.

So the real dispute is:

**Can the required residual savings gap be closed by non-closure measures alone, or does the company need permanent site/footprint reduction?**

That question remains unresolved because the parties are still arguing from principle before the independent plant-by-plant numbers are on the table.

---

## Facilitator compromise proposals

### Proposal 1: Independent “bridge-to-gap” package
A staged compromise in which the parties first agree on a binding alternative package, then test whether it closes the gap.

**Elements:**
- voluntary departures only
- internal transfers and retraining
- early retirement / partial retirement
- working-time reduction / shift rationalization
- management overhead and executive restraint
- owner-side capital support
- binding monitoring by an independent restructuring accountant
- a pre-agreed review point after the numbers are mapped

**Purpose:**
This allows the employee side to preserve the no-forced-layoff principle while giving management a credible test of whether the gap can be bridged without closures.

---

### Proposal 2: Time-bound site security with objective escape triggers
A compromise that protects sites for a defined period but does not freeze the company indefinitely.

**Elements:**
- no plant closures and no compulsory redundancies during the protected period
- objective performance and utilization triggers
- mandatory first-use of non-closure measures if triggers are hit
- a re-opener clause only after independent verification
- clear rules that the review cannot jump directly to closures without exhausting alternatives

**Purpose:**
This addresses employee concerns about trapdoor closure clauses while addressing management/investor concerns that the company needs an enforceable adjustment mechanism.

---

### Proposal 3: Hybrid downsizing without immediate closures
A middle path between full preservation and closure-heavy restructuring.

**Elements:**
- selective capacity reduction through product concentration
- single-shift conversions where volume justifies it
- site role specialization rather than shutdown
- voluntary workforce reduction aligned with capacity changes
- management and owner contributions tied to the same timetable
- protection of core German sites, but not necessarily every current line or function

**Purpose:**
This gives management a structural cost reset while avoiding immediate plant closures, and it may be acceptable to the state and family if regional employment and industrial continuity are preserved.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### 1) To the Works Council
**If the independent plant-by-plant analysis shows that non-closure measures leave a material residual gap, what specific additional adjustment would you accept before saying closures are never permissible?**

---

### 2) To the Industrial Union
**Will you support a quantified temporary contribution from labour if — and only if — it is tied to binding site security, no compulsory redundancies, and verified burden-sharing by management and owners?**

---

### 3) To Group & Brand Management
**Will you commit to a side-by-side comparison of non-closure, hybrid downsizing, and closure options before treating closures as the default instrument?**

---

### 4) To the Regional State Government
**Will you use your blocking position to prevent any closure-heavy package unless the company first proves that non-closure alternatives cannot close the residual gap?**

---

### 5) To the Controlling Family
**Are you prepared to back a plan that preserves the regional industrial base even if that requires lower short-term financial optics and stronger owner-side contribution?**

---

### 6) To Institutional Investors
**Would you support a non-closure settlement if the accountant shows that it delivers durable savings, measurable implementation milestones, and a credible path to margin recovery?**

---

## Suggested next procedural step

The most useful next move is:

**commission the independent plant-by-plant restructuring accountant first, then reconvene on the basis of three modeled options:**
1. no-closure package
2. hybrid downsizing
3. closure-heavy restructuring

That is the point at which the parties can shift from principle-based repetition to evidence-based bargaining.

## Where I agree

- shrinking European demand,
- structural overcapacity,

- and a brand that is missing its return target.

So let me state it plainly:

If the employee side wants to avoid closures, then it must come forward with something that closes the gap in hard numbers — not just in rhetoric.

A company cannot build 2030 competitiveness on concessions that disappear after two years. If we do not make a durable reduction in labour cost and capacity, we will simply be back in crisis again.

So I am prepared to discuss:
- voluntary departure programs,
- early retirement,
- working-time flexibility,
- bonus restraint,
- redeployment and qualification,

But those measures must be tied to:
- measurable headcount reduction,
- permanent cost reduction,

If they do not produce structural savings, they are insufficient.

### Core impasse
The central blockage is **not** about whether the company needs restructuring; all sides now accept that some durable adjustment is required.

The impasse is over **how far restructuring may go and what form it may take**:

- **Management / investors** require a **durable structural cost and capacity reset** that is strong enough to restore competitiveness by 2030.
- **Works council / union** reject **plant closures** and **compulsory redundancies** as the default or acceptable price of adjustment.
- **State government / controlling family** are willing to accept restructuring, but not if it destroys the German industrial base or becomes politically/legitimacy unworkable.

So the deadlock is:  
**“How do we achieve sufficient permanent savings without crossing the red line of closures and forced layoffs?”**

---

### Facilitation proposals: possible bridge positions

#### 1) Partial consolidation without full closures
A negotiated package could be built around:
- **shift reductions**
- **line consolidation**
- **product reassignment**
- **internal redeployment**
- **voluntary exits / retirement pathways**
- **temporary labour contributions**

This would aim to deliver real capacity reduction and fixed-cost savings **while preserving sites in modified form**.

**Why it may bridge:**  
It addresses management/investor demands for structural savings while staying closer to labour’s no-closure position.

---

#### 2) “No compulsory redundancies” with binding volume and investment guarantees
A compromise could combine:
- **no compulsory redundancies**
- **site guarantees through 2030**
- **binding future product allocation**
- **measurable investment commitments**
- **time-limited labour-side concessions**
- **automatic review clauses if volumes fall short**

**Why it may bridge:**  
It gives labour security and gives management/owners a credible mechanism to extract savings if business conditions improve or stabilize.

---

#### 3) Phased restructuring with a decision gate
A staged model could be used:
- Phase 1: voluntary measures, working-time tools, qualification, overhead cuts
- Phase 2: if quantified targets are missed, pre-agreed additional measures activate
- Phase 3: only if all alternatives fail, further footprint action is reconsidered

**Why it may bridge:**  
It allows each side to test alternatives before irreversible moves, while keeping a defined path to stronger action if needed.

### Pointed question to each stakeholder

#### To management
**What exact savings gap remains after applying all non-closure measures plant by plant, and which of those measures would you accept as sufficient if they deliver the same 2030 margin outcome?**

#### To the works council
**Which specific footprint reductions—shift cuts, line consolidation, site specialization, or product reassignment—would you accept as compatible with your no-closure line if they are paired with binding no-forced-layoff guarantees?**

#### To the industrial union
**What quantified labour-side contribution is you prepared to put on the table, and under what binding commitments from management and owners would it be politically acceptable to your members?**

#### To the controlling family
**Will you use your governance role to require management to test a negotiated non-closure scenario on equal terms with closure scenarios before any irreversible decision is taken?**

#### To the regional state government
**What concrete conditions would make you support a restructuring package that preserves sites but reduces shifts, staffing, or product scope at selected plants?**

#### To institutional investors
**Would you support a negotiated package if independent analysis shows it delivers comparable structural savings, even if the savings come from phased consolidation rather than immediate closures?**

#### To the restructuring expert
**What minimum combination of capacity reduction and fixed-cost removal would you regard as necessary for a non-closure scenario to be truly equivalent to closure-led restructuring?**

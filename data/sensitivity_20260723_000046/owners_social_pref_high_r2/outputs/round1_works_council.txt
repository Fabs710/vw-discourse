As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany.**
- **No compulsory redundancies.**
- **No dismantling of co-determination or the historic job-security framework.**

We recognize the pressure on the company, but the workforce must not pay for management’s strategic failures with permanent job losses and the closure of sites that families and regions depend on. We will not accept a fait accompli.

What we are prepared to discuss is a **serious restructuring that protects jobs and sites**:
- capacity adjustments through **socially responsible, reversible measures**
- **working-time solutions**
- **voluntary** instruments only
- targeted efficiency measures
- a credible **investment and product commitment** for German plants
- **binding site and job guarantees through 2030**

If management wants cooperation, it must come with a real plan that secures the future of the German sites, not just cuts costs on the backs of employees. Without binding guarantees, we will resist this with all the means available to us, including industrial action and using our co-determination rights.

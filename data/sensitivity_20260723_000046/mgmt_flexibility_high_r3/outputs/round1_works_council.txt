As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany**
- **No compulsory redundancies**
- **No dismantling of the job-security framework**
- **No unilateral breaking of the historic guarantees that the workforce has relied on**

We recognise that the company faces real competitive pressure and that change is necessary. But the answer cannot be to make workers and sites carry the whole burden through closures, layoffs, and the destruction of hard-won protections.

What we are prepared to discuss is a **restructuring that protects every German site and every job**, with:
- binding **site and employment guarantees through 2030**
- a credible industrial plan that secures future products and investment in Germany
- workforce contributions that are **negotiated, fair, and temporary**, not imposed as a precondition for survival
- genuine co-determination in shaping the restructuring, rather than being presented with a fait accompli

We will strongly oppose any plan that includes:
- closing a German plant
- forced redundancies
- relocating production out of Germany as a substitute for social responsibility
- one-sided cost cuts that sacrifice employees while leaving management decisions untouched

If management wants a viable solution, it must come with **binding guarantees for sites and jobs**, not demands for surrender. The workforce will not accept being asked to pay for a crisis it did not create by giving up its future.

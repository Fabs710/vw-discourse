Core impasse, as it stands:

## 1) Where the deadlock is
The central conflict is **not** whether restructuring is needed. All sides now accept that it is.

The impasse is over **the adjustment instrument**:

- **Labour side / state:** no plant closures, no compulsory redundancies, job security protection, negotiated and reversible measures first.
- **Management / shareholders / investors:** restructuring must be **closure-equivalent** in savings, and they will not rule out closures or compulsory measures if the gap is not otherwise closed.

So the dispute is really:
**“Can the company achieve closure-level savings without closures and forced layoffs?”**

That question has not been answered yet with a jointly accepted, quantified package.

---

## 2) Facilitator compromise paths to test
Below are three concrete bridging positions that could be put on the table procedurally.

### Proposal A: Phased no-closure package with hard triggers
A time-limited deal where:
- no German plant closures occur in the first phase,
- compulsory redundancies are suspended for a defined period,
- labor side accepts defined contributions on working time, wage moderation, and voluntary exits,
- management/owners commit to investment and overhead cuts,
- and the package includes **pre-agreed trigger points**.

If savings targets are missed at specific checkpoints, the parties reopen only the specific measures, not the whole agreement.

**Purpose:** gives labor a no-closure window while giving capital a mechanism to prevent open-ended underdelivery.

---

### Proposal B: “Capacity reduction without closures” via footprint consolidation inside plants
Instead of closing sites, the parties negotiate:
- shift eliminations,
- line consolidation,
- reduced variant complexity,
- lower planned output,
- and measured FTE reductions.

This would be paired with:
- no forced layoffs in exchange for larger voluntary exit and transfer programs,
- binding site guarantees for a defined core footprint,
- and a verified savings ledger.

**Purpose:** preserves sites while still producing closure-equivalent savings through internal downsizing.

---

### Proposal C: Two-tier commitment: site guarantees in exchange for deeper employee contribution
A structured trade:
- specific sites receive binding protection,
- workers accept deeper but temporary contributions:
  - longer working-time flexibility,
  - deferred wage elements,
  - reduced extras,
  - broader redeployment mobility,
- management and owners make visible top-level sacrifices and capital commitments,
- and the parties jointly monitor execution through a permanent oversight body.

**Purpose:** creates reciprocity: stronger worker concessions in return for stronger job and investment guarantees.

## 3) Pointed questions to each stakeholder

### To Group & Brand Management
**What exact savings gap, in euro per year and FTE, must be closed for you to accept a no-closure package as sufficient?**

### To the Works Council
**If plant closures and compulsory redundancies are excluded, what specific measures and savings volumes are you prepared to support that are large enough to satisfy the economic requirement?**

### To the Industrial Union
**Would you accept a time-limited suspension of compulsory redundancies only if the agreement contains binding savings triggers and a predefined review point?**

### To the Regional State Government
**What concrete leverage are you prepared to use to secure a negotiated no-closure package, and what measurable conditions would make you withdraw support for that approach?**

### To the Controlling Family Holding
**Will you publicly back a phased no-closure restructuring if it meets agreed savings thresholds, even if it requires deeper short-term concessions from owners and management?**

### To the Institutional Investors
**Would you support a settlement that postpones closures but delivers verified closure-equivalent savings through capacity reduction, headcount reduction, and lower overhead over a defined timeline?**

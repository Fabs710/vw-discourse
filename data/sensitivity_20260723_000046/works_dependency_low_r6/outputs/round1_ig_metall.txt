We are aligned on one central point: **the company needs a credible industrial future, not a reflexive cost-cutting exercise that destroys jobs and sites**.

But let me be equally clear where the line is for the union:

- **No compulsory redundancies**
- **No plant closures**
- **No solution that loads the entire adjustment burden onto workers**
- **No dismantling of the collective wage framework as the price of management’s strategic mistakes**

Management talks about competitiveness, and yes, costs matter. But if the response is to close plants and force thousands out while management and owners largely stay protected, then that is not a restructuring we can support. That is a transfer of pain downward. We will not accept that.

We have already signaled willingness to negotiate a **shared-sacrifice package**:
- capacity reductions instead of closures
- working-time and flexibility arrangements
- internal redeployment and qualification
- temporary or limited employee contributions
- possible restraint on bonuses or other cost elements
- a durable framework that secures investment and future products

That is the only kind of solution that is both economically serious and socially legitimate.

To the works council: your position on job and site security is absolutely the right starting point, and we support it. The workforce will not be asked to pay for the crisis with their livelihoods. If there are necessary adjustments, they must be negotiated, proportionate, and reversible where possible.

To management: if you want acceptance, you need to bring something real to the table — **binding guarantees, a credible investment plan, and a fair distribution of burdens**. “Future competitiveness” cannot be a slogan for unilateral labour-cost cuts and plant rationalisation.

If the company insists on closures or forced layoffs, then you should assume the workforce will respond with full force. The peace obligation has ended, and we are prepared to escalate if needed. But we would much rather secure a durable settlement that protects the company **and** its people.

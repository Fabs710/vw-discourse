## 3) What “equivalent” would require
For a **closure-free** package to be economically comparable to a closure-based plan, it would usually need all of the following:

- **multi-year wage moderation**
- reduced or removed special payments / supplements
- **working-time flexibility**
- lower staffing ratios or shift reductions
- **voluntary exits at scale**
- internal redeployment and qualification
- binding capacity reductions inside the remaining footprint
- concessions that are **contractually durable** through the relevant business cycle

In practice, **labor-cost concessions alone rarely substitute fully for a plant closure** unless they are very deep and long-lasting.

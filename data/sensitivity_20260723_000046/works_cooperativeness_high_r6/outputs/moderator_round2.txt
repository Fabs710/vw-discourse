### Core impasse
The central deadlock is between **job/site security** and **structural economic adjustment**.

- **Labour side**: insists that restructuring must happen **without German plant closures or compulsory redundancies**, and with binding site and investment guarantees.
- **Management / family holding / investors**: insist that a credible turnaround requires **real capacity reduction, durable cost cuts, and retained freedom to close sites or use redundancies if needed**.
- **State shareholder**: seeks a path that preserves the industrial base and regional stability, but cannot by itself override the economic case.

So the dispute is not over whether restructuring is needed; it is over **whether the company can be reset without hard structural measures and, if so, how the savings and enforcement would be made equivalent**.

---

### Facilitator compromise positions
Below are three possible bridging packages that could be explored:

#### 1) Sequenced restructuring with a closure moratorium phase
- Agree a **time-limited moratorium on plant closures and compulsory redundancies** for a defined period.
- In that phase, implement:
  - voluntary exits,
  - redeployment,
  - qualification,
  - working-time measures,
  - wage/bonus concessions,
  - and capacity reductions via shifts, lines, and product allocation.
- Add a **review trigger**: if quantified savings and capacity targets are not met by a fixed date, management regains the right to table structural measures.

**Bridge function:** gives labour immediate protection while giving management a credible fallback if the package underperforms.

#### 2) Binding “equivalent savings” package with site-specific guarantees
- Replace the closure threat with a **numbers-based alternative package**:
  - exact savings target,
  - exact capacity reduction target,
  - exact investment commitment,
  - and an enforceable labor contribution.
- In exchange, labor accepts **temporary, time-bound concessions** and **site-specific productivity measures**.
- If the quantified package delivers the same financial effect, closures and compulsory redundancies remain off the table during the agreement.

**Bridge function:** shifts the dispute from principle to verifiable economic equivalence.

#### 3) Protected-core / adjustable-periphery model
- Define a **protected core set of German sites** with guaranteed continuation through 2030.
- In parallel, identify an **adjustable periphery**:
  - lines,
  - shifts,
  - products,
  - or support functions
  that can be restructured first.
- Workforce measures, investment, and retraining are concentrated on the protected sites, while any remaining structural action is limited to the periphery and tied to strict consultation and enforcement rules.

**Bridge function:** preserves a visible industrial anchor while still allowing meaningful capacity adjustment.

### Pointed questions to each stakeholder

#### Works Council (Betriebsrat)
- If management presents a quantified package that achieves the required savings **without** closures or compulsory redundancies, what specific enforcement mechanism would make you regard it as credible through 2030?

#### Industrial Union
- What minimum combination of temporary employee contribution, capacity adjustment, and investment commitment would you consider sufficient to suspend further industrial action and enter a binding agreement?

#### Group & Brand Management
- What exact savings and capacity targets are you requiring, and which of them are genuinely non-negotiable versus potentially replaceable by voluntary, redeployment, or working-time measures?

#### Regional State Government
- What binding commitments can the state shareholder realistically support or conditionally underwrite so that site protection is more than a political signal?

#### Controlling Family Holding
- Under what precise quantified conditions would you accept an alternative to closures as economically equivalent rather than merely politically preferable?

#### Institutional Investors
- What measurable indicators of durability and enforcement would persuade you that a negotiated settlement without closures is value-preserving rather than value-destructive?

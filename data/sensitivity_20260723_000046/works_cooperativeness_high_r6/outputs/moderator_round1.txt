**Neutral moderator summary — Round 1**

### 1) What has been agreed
- All parties accept that the company needs a **serious restructuring**; the status quo is not sustainable.
- There is broad openness to a package that may include:
  - **capacity adjustments**
  - **redeployment and qualification**
  - **working-time flexibility**
  - **voluntary exit options**
  - **some form of employee contribution**
  - **a credible investment plan for future products and German sites**
- There is also shared recognition that any solution should be **durable**, not merely symbolic or short-term.

### 2) Core disagreements that remain
- **Plant closures in Germany:**  
  Labour-side actors and the state shareholder reject them as a starting point; management, the family holding, and institutional investors keep them on the table if needed for economics.
- **Compulsory redundancies:**  
  Works council and union reject them; management and owners will not rule them out if no equivalent alternative exists.
- **Job-security framework / legacy protections:**  
  Labour wants them preserved; management and investors say they cannot remain untouched if competitiveness is to improve materially.
- **Binding site security:**  
  Labour and the state want firm commitments; ownership and management say such commitments must be conditional on a viable business case.
- **Who carries the burden:**  
  Labour-side actors insist on shared sacrifice; investors and ownership say employee contributions alone are insufficient, but also that labour cannot be insulated from meaningful adjustment.

### 3) Key trade-offs still unaddressed
- **How to achieve the required cost and capacity reduction without closures or forced layoffs**
- **What level of employee contribution is acceptable if it is time-limited and reciprocal**
- **What binding investment and site-security guarantees can realistically be offered**
- **Whether any alternative package can match the economic effect of closures/redundancies**
- **How to balance regional/political stability against long-term competitiveness and value creation**

### 4) Brief refocus for the next round
Please move from positions to **comparative proposals**:
- Labour side: specify a concrete alternative package with quantified savings and duration.
- Management/owners: specify the minimum economic targets that must be met and which measures are truly non-negotiable.
- State shareholder: clarify which protections are essential and what conditional support is possible.
- All parties: identify **one package element they could accept**, **one they need modified**, and **one they currently reject**.

The next round should aim to compare **numbers, timing, and enforceability** rather than restating red lines.

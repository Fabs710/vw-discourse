## Moderator Summary — Round 1

### 1) What has been agreed
- All parties accept that the company faces a **serious structural pressure** and needs a **durable restructuring**, not a symbolic fix.
- There is broad agreement that **a one-sided burden on employees would be unacceptable**.
- Most parties support the idea of a **negotiated settlement** over prolonged conflict.
- There is shared recognition that any solution should include:
  - **visible contributions from management/owners**,
  - **some form of workforce contribution**,
  - and a **credible industrial / investment perspective** for the future.

### 2) Core disagreements that remain
- **Plant closures:**  
  - Trade union, works council, and state government reject closures as a starting point and want them ruled out if possible.  
  - Management, family holding, and investors say closures cannot be excluded in advance if they are needed for structural savings.
- **Compulsory redundancies:**  
  - Employee-side and state representatives oppose forced layoffs.  
  - Other stakeholders want that option kept available if necessary.
- **Scope of labour contributions:**  
  - Employees are open to flexibility, working-time changes, bonus restraint, and capacity adjustment.  
  - Others argue these measures must deliver **deep, durable savings**, not only temporary relief.
- **Site guarantees vs competitiveness:**  
  - Employee-side stakeholders seek binding protection for German sites.  
  - Capital and management-side stakeholders argue that site protection cannot override the need to restore competitiveness.

### 3) Key trade-offs still unaddressed
- No shared formula yet for **how much cost reduction** is required, or how it will be measured.
- No agreement yet on whether **non-closure alternatives** can match the savings and capacity reduction that closures might provide.
- No concrete package yet balancing:
  - job/security guarantees,
  - site commitments,
  - wage/flexibility concessions,
  - investment commitments,
  - and owner/management contributions.
- The role of **co-determination, state influence, and supervisory-board rights** in any final package remains unresolved.

### 4) Brief refocus for the next round
Next round should move from principles to **testable options**:
- What exact restructuring target must be met?
- Which measures can deliver those savings **without closures or forced redundancies**?
- If closures remain on the table, under what **strict conditions and safeguards**?
- What are the **specific commitments** from management, owners, and the state on investment, product allocation, and site future?
- Can the parties define a **shared package** with quantified burdens, timelines, and protections?

## Core impasse

The central blockage is not the need for restructuring itself. The impasse is over **sequencing and proof**:

- **Management / owners / investors** want the option of closures and compulsory redundancies kept available because they doubt non-closure measures can close the fully loaded cost gap.
- **Works council / union / state government** want closures and forced redundancies excluded up front because they see them as premature, trust-destroying, and not yet economically proven.

So the dispute is: **Must the company first quantify and exhaust a non-closure package before any footprint reduction becomes admissible, or may footprint reduction remain on the table from the start as leverage?**

A second layer of impasse is about **what “site preservation” means**:
- One side means keeping sites legally open with real work and jobs.
- The other side accepts open sites only if actual capacity, headcount, and fixed cost are materially reduced.

A third layer is about **bindingness**:
- labour side wants job and site protection commitments first,
- management and capital side want conditionality, review points, and fallback options.

## Facilitation proposals for compromise

### 1) Time-limited non-closure moratorium with quantified milestones
**Proposal:** A defined freeze on closures and compulsory redundancies for a limited period, paired with hard, pre-agreed milestones for the non-closure package.

**Elements:**
- plant closures and forced redundancies suspended for a set period
- immediate implementation of quantified non-closure measures
- milestone reviews tied to verified savings, utilization, and headcount reduction
- automatic reopening of the issue only if milestones are missed after independent verification

**Why it may bridge sides:**
- gives labour side a real pause from closure pressure
- gives management side a path to reintroduce structural options if the package fails
- converts the argument from principle to measurable performance

---

### 2) Site-preservation with capacity consolidation inside the existing footprint
**Proposal:** Keep sites legally open, while explicitly allowing substantial capacity reduction through operational consolidation.

**Elements:**
- no plant closures in the negotiated period
- shift reductions, line consolidation, product reallocation, and internal redeployment permitted
- headcount reduction pursued first through voluntary exits, attrition, retraining, and working-time changes
- compulsory redundancies only as a contingent last-step issue, if all agreed measures fail and after review

**Why it may bridge sides:**
- answers the labour side’s insistence on preserving sites
- answers management’s demand for real capacity and cost reduction
- makes restructuring visible as “smaller and simpler operations,” not shutdown

---

### 3) Burden-sharing package linked to investment and governance commitments
**Proposal:** Temporary workforce contribution is accepted only if matched by management restraint, owner contribution, and binding investment commitments.

**Elements:**
- temporary employee contribution with a sunset clause
- management compensation restraint and reduced payouts
- owner-side financial contribution or capital support
- binding investment allocation to German operations
- preserved co-determination and transparent governance oversight
- explicit job-security framework for the duration of the package

**Why it may bridge sides:**
- addresses fairness concerns from labour
- addresses value-protection concerns from capital
- creates a reciprocal package rather than one-sided sacrifice

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To management
**What exact fully loaded savings gap remains after shift cuts, working-time changes, voluntary exits, redeployment, management restraint, and owner contribution are modeled plant by plant?**

### To the works council
**Which specific capacity and headcount reductions are you prepared to accept inside an open-site framework, and what is your minimum quantified savings target for that package?**

### To the union
**What temporary employee contribution would you consider acceptable if it were time-limited, matched by management and owner sacrifice, and tied to binding job protection?**

### To the family holding
**Will you support a time-limited non-closure moratorium if the company must publish a verified, plant-by-plant savings model and accept binding investment commitments?**

### To institutional investors
**What level of recurring, fully loaded savings would be sufficient for you to prefer a non-closure restructuring over footprint reduction, even if execution is more complex?**

### To the state government
**Will you use your blocking leverage to require a verified non-closure model first, even if that means accepting deeper internal capacity cuts inside the existing footprint?**

### To the independent expert
**What are the minimum operational and financial conditions under which a non-closure package becomes a credible substitute for closures in this network?**

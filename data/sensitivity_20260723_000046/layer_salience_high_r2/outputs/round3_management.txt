## First: what I agree with
I agree with everyone on a few important points:

- This company has a **real structural competitiveness problem**.
- A **temporary patch** would be irresponsible.
- A **prolonged strike** would damage output, trust, and value.
- Any solution must be **durable**, not symbolic.

So no, I am not dismissing social or political reality. But I also will not pretend that preserving the current cost and capacity structure is a solution.

## Where I disagree — firmly

### 1) “No closures” is not a solution
The works council and union keep repeating that plant closures are unacceptable. I understand that position. I do **not** accept it as a workable boundary for the company.

If the German footprint is structurally too large for the market, then we must realign it. That can mean:
- plant closures,
- line rationalization,
- consolidation of production,
- or other permanent footprint reductions.

If you want to rule closures out entirely, then you must show an **economically equivalent package**. Not a declaration, not a political appeal — a package that truly closes the cost gap.

So far, I have not heard that. I have heard resistance.

### 2) “No compulsory redundancies” cannot be an absolute
I prefer negotiated solutions. I prefer orderly transitions. But I will not sign up to a restructuring that guarantees every current job regardless of economic reality.

If the savings do not add up through alternative measures, then employment reductions may be unavoidable. We can discuss how they happen. We cannot discuss pretending they are off the table no matter what.

### 3) Blanket guarantees through 2030 are not credible if they freeze an unaffordable structure
The demand for binding site and job guarantees through 2030 is understandable from your side. But from the company’s side, a blanket guarantee is only acceptable if the economics remain viable under it.

If the package leaves us with:
- too much fixed cost,
- too much excess capacity,
- and too little flexibility,
then that is not a turnaround. That is delay.

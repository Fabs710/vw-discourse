The works council’s position is clear:

We will not accept plant closures in Germany, and we will not accept compulsory redundancies. We also reject any restructuring that tears up the historic job-security framework or uses a crisis to shift the full burden onto the workforce.

If the company needs to improve competitiveness, then it must do so through a negotiated solution that protects jobs and sites: binding guarantees for all German plants through 2030, no forced layoffs, and real co-determination in shaping any restructuring. We are prepared to discuss alternative measures such as capacity adjustments, working-time solutions, internal redeployment, training, and a fair employee contribution — but only on the basis of secure sites and secure employment.

Management cannot present the workforce with a fait accompli and expect support. If this plan includes closures or mass layoffs, we will resist it with all the means available to us.

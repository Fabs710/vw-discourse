- working-time reductions,
- voluntary departures,

I reject that logic.

So I want to be very clear:

- A cost gap does **not** automatically justify site destruction.
- “Durable” does **not** mean “workers carry the biggest and most permanent burden.”

If you want labour to contribute, then the contribution must be:
1. **temporary or time-bounded**, not open-ended,
2. **quantified**, not symbolic,
3. **shared**, with management and owners also contributing,

You are still not answering the central question:  
**What savings come from non-closure measures, and what part of the gap remains after your own side has contributed?**

Until that is answered, your closure talk remains a threat, not a case.

What I **do not** accept is your repeated demand for:
- **no plant closures**
- **no compulsory redundancies**
- **binding job and site guarantees through 2030**
- while expecting management to somehow deliver a structural cost reset

That is not a turnaround plan unless you can show me equivalent permanent savings elsewhere. So far, you have not.

- how much capacity comes out,
- how much cost comes out permanently,
- what headcount reduction is achieved,
- what wage and labour-cost contribution is real,
- what management and owner contribution is concrete,
- and why this is sufficient to restore margins.

And let me be clear: **I am not asking for employee surrender. I am asking for a package that makes the company competitive again.** Those are not the same thing.

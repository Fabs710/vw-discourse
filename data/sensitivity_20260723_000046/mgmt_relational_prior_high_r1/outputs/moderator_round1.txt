1) **What has been agreed**
- All parties agree the current situation is **not sustainable** and that the company needs a **real restructuring**, not a cosmetic one.
- There is broad agreement that **burden-sharing** is necessary; no side wants workers alone to bear the adjustment.
- All parties accept that **negotiation is preferable to unilateral action**.
- There is shared openness, in principle, to **internal redeployment, qualification, working-time adjustments, and productivity improvements**.
- Most parties indicate willingness to consider a **non-closure solution** if it is **credible, quantified, and durable**.

2) **Where the core disagreements remain**
- **Plant closures in Germany**: Works Council, union, and state reject them as a first resort and treat them as unacceptable; management sees them as potentially necessary; shareholders are open to alternatives only if savings are sufficient.
- **Compulsory redundancies**: Labour and state reject them; management and shareholders have not ruled out workforce reductions if needed for competitiveness.
- **Job-security guarantees**: Labour wants them preserved; management and shareholders want flexibility if guarantees obstruct competitiveness.
- **Scale and nature of adjustment**: Management and shareholders want structurally deep, durable cost reduction; labour insists this must not come through closures or forced layoffs.
- **Governance constraints**: The state insists its role and veto rights be respected; management and shareholders stress that governance cannot block necessary change.

3) **What key trade-offs are still unaddressed**
- The group has not yet reconciled **how much savings are required** versus **how much can be achieved without closures**.
- There is no shared answer on the balance between **job/site preservation** and **capacity reduction**.
- The parties have not defined what level of **employee contribution** would be acceptable, or what **management/owner contributions** would accompany it.
- There is no agreed package on **investment commitments, site guarantees, and timelines** tied to restructuring.
- The discussion has not yet resolved whether **temporary measures** would be sufficient or whether **permanent structural changes** are unavoidable.

4) **Brief refocus for the next round**
- Please move from position statements to a **single, quantified restructuring package**.
- The next round should test:
  1. **required savings/capacity reduction**,  
  2. **which measures can deliver them without closures**,  
  3. **what guarantees can realistically be offered in return**, and  
  4. **what each side is prepared to contribute**.
- A useful next step would be a side-by-side comparison of:
  - closure-based scenario,
  - non-closure scenario,
  - and the conditions under which each would be acceptable.

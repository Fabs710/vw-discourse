As facilitator, I would locate the impasse in three linked points:

## Core impasse
1. **Scope of non-negotiable red lines**
   - Employee side and state/family reject German plant closures and compulsory redundancies.
   - Management and investors reject making those exclusions absolute before the numbers are tested.

2. **What counts as a “sufficient” restructuring package**
   - Management/investors want durable, quantified capacity and cost reduction.
   - Works council/union want savings from all sides plus binding job and site guarantees.
   - The parties do not yet share a common definition of “enough.”

3. **Sequence and risk allocation**
   - Employee side wants guarantees first, then concessions.
   - Management/investors want concessions and structural options first, then commitments.
   - The result is a deadlock over who moves first and who bears the risk if the model fails.

---

## Facilitation proposals for compromise
These are not endorsements, but structured bridge options for further negotiation:

### Proposal 1: Two-stage package with trigger-based escalation
- **Stage 1:** Immediate negotiated measures that are reversible or time-limited:
  - working-time flexibility,
  - temporary wage moderation/deferred compensation,
  - bonus restraint,
  - redeployment and retraining,
  - management/overhead/procurement savings.
- **Stage 2:** A jointly defined review after a fixed period, using agreed financial triggers.
- **Purpose:** Lets employee side avoid immediate closures/layoffs while allowing management/investors a path to harder measures if the quantified gap remains.

### Proposal 2: “No forced redundancies first” with a quantified capacity review
- A written commitment that **compulsory redundancies are not the first instrument**, and that all alternatives must be quantified first.
- In return, employee side accepts a joint review of **capacity reduction options**, including site-specific restructuring tools, with closure only as a last-resort measure if all agreed alternatives fail to close the gap.
- **Purpose:** Preserves the symbolic red line against forced layoffs while not fully removing structural options from the table.

### Proposal 3: Binding guarantees tied to measurable owner/management contributions
- Site and employment protections through a defined term, but only if paired with:
  - concrete owner-side capital contribution,
  - management overhead cuts,
  - investment commitments,
  - and measurable savings from labour-side measures.
- **Purpose:** Addresses the employee side’s demand for symmetry and the owners’ demand for durability by making each side’s commitment conditional on the others.

## Pointed questions to each stakeholder

### To Group & Brand Management
**Can you present a plant-by-plant model that separates temporary savings from recurring savings, and identify the smallest package that would close the gap without immediate closures or compulsory redundancies?**

### To the Works Council
**Would you accept a time-limited framework in which closures and forced redundancies are not immediate options, provided the package includes a binding review trigger if the quantified savings target is not met?**

### To the Industrial Union
**What exact combination of wage, time, and flexibility measures would you regard as a material workforce contribution, and under what conditions would you recommend it to your members?**

### To the Regional State Government
**What concrete governance intervention are you prepared to use to support a negotiated solution that protects the regional industrial base while still allowing economically necessary restructuring?**

### To the Controlling Family Holding
**What level of owner-side contribution—capital, reinvestment commitment, or governance restraint—are you prepared to put on the table to make a non-destructive restructuring credible?**

### To Institutional Investors
**What quantified improvement in margins, capacity, and execution risk would be sufficient for you to support a deal that avoids immediate closures but includes binding review triggers?**

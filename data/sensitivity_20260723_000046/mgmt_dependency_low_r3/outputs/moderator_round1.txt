### 1) Points of agreement
- All parties say the restructuring must be **negotiated** rather than imposed unilaterally.
- There is broad agreement that the company needs a **durable, structural solution** rather than a short-term patch.
- Most parties accept that **workforce contributions** may be part of the package, provided they are **balanced** by management/owner commitments.
- There is shared interest in **investment, product commitments, transparency, and a credible industrial future** for the German operations.
- Several parties prefer **worktime flexibility, redeployment, qualification, and pay-related measures** if these can achieve sufficient savings.

### 2) Core disagreements
- **Plant closures in Germany:** Works Council, union, and state government reject them; management, family holding, and investors keep them on the table if needed.
- **Compulsory redundancies:** Employee-side and state side reject them; management and capital-side actors do not rule them out if alternatives fail.
- **Red lines vs. business necessity:** Employee and state actors frame site/job protection as a substantive boundary; management, family holding, and investors frame competitiveness as the overriding constraint.
- **Scope of guarantees:** Employee and state side want binding job/site guarantees through 2030; management and capital-side actors say such guarantees are not credible without major structural change.

### 3) Key trade-offs still unresolved
- How much of the needed cost reduction can be delivered through **negotiated, reversible measures** versus **hard structural cuts**.
- Whether the package can achieve **enough durable savings** without closures or forced layoffs.
- What **specific investment and product commitments** the company is prepared to make in exchange for workforce concessions.
- How to balance **social stability, co-determination, and regional industrial responsibility** against **margin recovery and capital-market credibility**.
- Whether any capacity reduction can occur **without site closures**, and if not, which sites or functions would be affected.

### 4) Brief refocus for the next round
Please move from principles to **concrete options and numbers**:
- quantify the savings gap that must be closed,
- specify which measures are on the table and their expected impact,
- identify what binding commitments each side is prepared to offer,
- and test whether a package exists that is both **socially acceptable** and **financially sufficient**.

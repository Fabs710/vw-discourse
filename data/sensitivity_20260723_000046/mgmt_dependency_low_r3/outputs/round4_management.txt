## On the moderator’s compromise lines

### 1) Quantified non-closure first, with bounded review
I can live with a quantified first stage **only if** it is not framed as a permanent exemption from structural action.

A review is not a trapdoor if it is:
- time-bound,
- based on audited metrics,
- and followed by a real negotiation over the remaining gap.

I will not accept a mechanism that makes it impossible to respond if the gap remains open.

### 2) No closures / no forced redundancies, but deeper variable contributions
This is not enough unless you show the scale. Temporary wage moderation, bonus restraint, and flexibility may help — but I need recurring savings, not just a pause.

If you want to keep closures off the table, then the package must be sufficiently deep to replace what closures would otherwise deliver. That is the test.

### 3) Hybrid restructuring with limited structural adjustment short of closure
This is the most credible direction so far. I remain open to:
- shift reductions,
- line consolidation,
- product allocation changes,
- site specialization,
- redeployment,
- voluntary exits,
- and productivity improvements.

But “limited structural adjustment” must be real. It must reduce fixed cost and capacity enough to matter. If it does not, then it is just deferral.

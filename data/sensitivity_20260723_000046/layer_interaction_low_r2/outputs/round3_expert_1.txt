## The key decision test
A closure/consolidation package is economically superior only if:

**Net present value of recurring savings – one-off transition cost – added logistics/capex burden**
is materially better than the no-closure alternative, **and** the retained footprint can absorb the transferred volume without creating new bottlenecks.

A no-closure package is credible only if it can:
- reduce fixed cost enough,
- lower effective capacity enough,
- and secure future volume/product allocation,
without simply deferring the same problem into the next cycle.

## Redeployment feasibility test
Surplus labor can be redeployed only if one or more of these are true:
- same-site absorption into future programs
- transfer to nearby plants with compatible skill sets
- natural attrition covers a material share
- retraining time is short relative to product ramp timing
- wage and classification rules allow flexible use

Redeployment is weak if:
- skills are highly site-specific
- future programs are not yet approved
- nearby plants are already near full utilization
- the time horizon is shorter than retraining/qualification time

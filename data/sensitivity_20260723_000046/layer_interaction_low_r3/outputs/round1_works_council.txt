We will not accept plant closures or compulsory redundancies in Germany.

The workforce has already borne a great deal of the burden of transformation, and the historic job-security commitment cannot simply be torn up because management now wants a faster and easier path. If the company needs restructuring, then it must be done in a way that preserves every German site, protects jobs, and keeps co-determination intact.

Our priorities are clear:

- No closure of any German plant
- No forced layoffs
- Binding job and site guarantees through 2030
- Meaningful investment commitments for the affected sites
- A real negotiated restructuring, not a fait accompli imposed from above

We are prepared to discuss alternatives that create savings without destroying sites or livelihoods: capacity adjustments, working-time measures, internally agreed flexibility, and a fair contribution from labour if it is matched by binding guarantees and a credible future plan. But we will not trade away the foundation of job security for short-term cost cutting.

If management is serious about the long-term future of the company, it must come to the table with a solution that shares the burden fairly and protects the people who built this company. Anything else will face fierce resistance.

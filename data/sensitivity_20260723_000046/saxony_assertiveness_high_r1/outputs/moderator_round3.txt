## 1) What has been agreed
- A purely cosmetic deal would be unacceptable.
- Any restructuring must be executable, durable, and backed by investment.
- Voluntary instruments should be used first: redeployment, retraining, early retirement, phased exits, working-time tools.
- Management, owners, and capital should contribute alongside labour.
- Co-determination and industrial peace matter for implementation.
- Transparent, quantified numbers are needed to assess any package.

## 2) Core disagreements remain
- **Plant closures:** Labour, the works council, and the state reject closures as a starting point; management, the controlling family, and investors keep them on the table.
- **Compulsory redundancies:** Labour and the state reject them; management and investors say they cannot be ruled out categorically.
- **Scope of site protection:** Employee and state side want all German plants safeguarded; management and capital say that may be incompatible with the required economic reset.
- **Duration of concessions:** Labour wants temporary, limited contributions tied to guarantees; management and capital want durable, structural savings.
- **Meaning of restructuring:** One side frames it as preserving the industrial base with internal adjustments; the other as requiring material capacity reduction and workforce adjustment.

## 3) Key trade-offs still unaddressed
- Whether the required savings can realistically be achieved **without closures or forced layoffs**.
- How much of the burden can be shifted through **voluntary measures, working-time changes, and labour-cost concessions** before structural measures become necessary.
- What binding guarantees on **jobs, sites, and investment** would be sufficient to justify employee sacrifices.
- How management, owners, and investors would quantify their own contributions in a way that is visible and credible.
- Whether a site-preserving solution can close the competitiveness gap at the required scale, or whether it would only delay the adjustment.

## 4) Brief refocus for the next round
- Shift from position statements to **quantified scenarios**.
- Require a comparison of at least two packages:
  1. a **no-closure / no-forced-layoff** option, and  
  2. a **structural adjustment** option.
- For each, show:
  - expected annual savings,
  - headcount impact,
  - site impact,
  - investment commitments,
  - and governance/implementation risks.
- Then test which package is executable, durable, and acceptable to the full governance structure.

As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany**
- **No compulsory redundancies / forced layoffs**
- **No dismantling of the established job-security framework**
- **No unilateral fait accompli from management**

The workforce has already carried a great deal of the transition burden. We will not accept that the answer to management’s planning mistakes and strategic delays is to make thousands of employees pay with their jobs and the future of entire sites.

What we are prepared to discuss is a **genuine restructuring that protects every German plant**, with:
- **binding site guarantees through 2030**
- **binding employment guarantees through 2030**
- **capacity adjustments through working-time, internal flexibility, and negotiated measures**
- **investment commitments for future products and technologies**
- **real co-determination in shaping the restructuring**, not just being informed after the fact

If management wants a solution, it must come to the table with alternatives that preserve the industrial base in Germany. We are open to contribution, but not to closures, not to forced layoffs, and not to breaking the trust that underpins social peace here. If those red lines are crossed, the conflict will escalate.

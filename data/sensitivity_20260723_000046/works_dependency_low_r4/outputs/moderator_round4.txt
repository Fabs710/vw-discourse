## Core impasse

The central impasse is now very specific:

1. **Employee side / state** want a restructuring with **no German plant closures, no compulsory redundancies, and no hidden closure fallback**.
2. **Management / family / investors** want **credible structural cost reduction** and believe that **footprint reduction must remain available** if non-closure measures underdeliver.
3. **No shared benchmark exists yet** for what counts as “enough” savings, because the sides disagree on whether the test should be:
   - preserve all sites first, then measure savings, or
   - keep closure options live from the outset to ensure economic credibility.

So the dispute is not just about numbers. It is about:
- **whether closures may remain an active option during negotiation**, and
- **whether a non-closure package can be tested without closure pressure in the background**.

---

## Facilitator compromise proposals

### Proposal 1: Time-limited non-closure test with binding safeguards
A defined test period could be established in which:
- **no German plant closures occur**
- **no compulsory redundancies occur**
- **a quantified non-closure package is implemented**
- progress is reviewed by an independent expert against agreed savings and margin targets

At the end of the period:
- if targets are met, the non-closure package continues
- if targets are missed, the parties move to a predefined second-stage renegotiation

This would not decide the final outcome in advance, but it would separate **testing** from **threat escalation**.

---

### Proposal 2: Differentiated-site package without explicit closures
A compromise could preserve all German sites formally while allowing:
- site-by-site specialization
- shift reductions
- volume reallocation
- internal redeployment
- function transfers
- productivity and overhead cuts

The key condition would be:
- **no site is designated for closure**, but
- weaker sites may receive stronger adjustment measures than core sites

This may bridge the gap between “all sites frozen” and “closures first.”

---

### Proposal 3: Full comparison with a no-decision initial phase
The parties could agree to a short initial phase in which an independent specialist produces a **side-by-side quantified comparison** of:
- full non-closure package
- differentiated-site package
- partial-closure package

During this phase:
- no irreversible site decisions are taken
- employee-side protections remain in force
- management retains the right to argue for footprint reduction later, but only on the basis of the quantified comparison

This would create a common factual basis before any final commitment.

## One pointed question to each stakeholder

### To Works Council
If a fully quantified non-closure package were shown to deliver, say, **most** of the steady-state savings but not all of them, would you support a structured review framework that keeps all German plants open during the test period but allows a second-stage renegotiation if targets are missed?

### To Industrial Union
What specific mix of labor-side measures would you be prepared to put on the table as a **durable** contribution, and what minimum guarantees from management and owners would be required in return for you to support a no-closure test phase?

### To Group & Brand Management
Will you commit to a transparent plant-by-plant economic case, including closure costs, conflict risk, and co-determination risk, so that the comparison is between **fully costed options** rather than between a negotiated package and an assumed closure benefit?

### To Regional State Government
Will you define, in operational terms, what it means for you to support restructuring **without** accepting plant closures as the default, including what kinds of capacity adjustment you would regard as acceptable within a protected German footprint?

### To Controlling Family Holding
If a non-closure package comes in below your ideal benchmark but still delivers substantial durable savings, what threshold would you regard as sufficient to rule out closure-led restructuring?

### To Institutional Investors
Would you support a time-limited moratorium on closures if the company provides a rigorous independent comparison and hard milestone review, even if the initial package does not fully replicate closure economics?

### To Independent Specialist
What minimum savings threshold and execution confidence level would you set for a non-closure package to be considered economically credible relative to a closure-led alternative in this governance environment?

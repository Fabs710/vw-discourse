We fully support the core line from the works council: **no plant closures in Germany and no compulsory redundancies**.

Let me be equally clear from the collective-bargaining side: the workforce is prepared to contribute, but **not to finance the crisis alone** and certainly not through a one-sided destruction of jobs, sites, and the collective wage framework.

### Where we agree
- The company is under real pressure.
- Change is necessary.
- A solution must be durable, not a short-term patch.
- There is room to discuss **capacity reduction, working-time measures, voluntary instruments, qualification, and a fair employee contribution**.

### Where we disagree sharply
What we cannot accept is the logic that competitiveness automatically means:
- plant closures,
- forced layoffs,
- permanent insecurity,
- and wage dumping at the expense of the workforce.

That would shift the full burden onto employees while management and owners escape responsibility. That is not a restructuring; it is a transfer of risk downward.

### Our position
If management wants a serious settlement, then it must include:
- **binding job security**
- **site guarantees**
- **no compulsory redundancies**
- **no plant closures**
- **preservation of the collective wage framework**
- **a fair burden-sharing package** involving management, owners, and employees

We have already shown openness to a contribution from the workforce side. That means we are prepared to discuss:
- temporary wage components,
- reduced bonuses,
- internal flexibility,
- capacity adjustments,
- and voluntary exits where genuinely needed.

But that openness has limits. It is **not** a blank cheque for closures or layoff plans.

### On process
If management insists on pushing through a model based on closures and forced cuts, then we will not treat this as a negotiating detail. We will treat it as a fundamental conflict. The peace obligation has ended, and the workforce expects us to act accordingly.

So the message is simple:
**Bring a fair, balanced plan that protects jobs and sites, and we can negotiate.**
**Bring plant closures and compulsory redundancies, and you will face resistance.**

## Core impasse
The central impasse is **not** whether the company needs restructuring. All sides accept that it does.

The impasse is **how far the restructuring may go**:
- **Employee side / state government:** restructuring must occur **without German plant closures or compulsory redundancies**, with binding guarantees first.
- **Management / family / investors:** they will not commit to a no-closure framework **before** the numbers prove that internal measures can fully close the gap.
- **Expert:** says a non-closure solution is **possible in principle**, but only if the quantified savings gap can actually be closed by internal measures and burden-sharing.

So the conflict is between:
1. **binding ex ante protection**, and  
2. **keeping hard options available until the economics are proven**.

---

## Facilitator proposals for possible compromise
Below are three concrete bridging options that preserve the core interests on both sides while narrowing the conflict.

### Proposal 1: Time-limited non-closure test phase
A structured **first-phase non-closure trial** could run for a fixed period, during which:
- no plant closures and no compulsory redundancies are pursued,
- internal measures are implemented first,
- workforce contributions are time-limited,
- management/owner contributions are defined in parallel,
- and a jointly agreed audit tracks savings against targets.

**Bridge function:**  
This gives employee-side stakeholders security during the test phase, while giving management/investors a verified basis for judging sufficiency later.

---

### Proposal 2: Conditional no-closure commitment with a predefined review trigger
A binding **no-closure / no-compulsory-redundancy commitment** could be granted **if** a jointly agreed restructuring package achieves specified milestones:
- savings target A by date X,
- utilization improvement by date Y,
- investment commitments by date Z,
- and verified burden-sharing from all sides.

If the targets are missed, the parties return to the table under a pre-agreed review mechanism, rather than reopening the issue ad hoc.

**Bridge function:**  
This converts the dispute from “promise versus threat” into a measurable performance contract.

---

### Proposal 3: Site-protection package plus site-specific exception process
A framework agreement could establish:
- a **general commitment** to avoid closures and forced redundancies,
- but also a **narrow, jointly governed exception procedure** for any site that remains uneconomic after the audit.

Any exception would require:
- independent verification,
- a second-best alternatives test,
- consultation with co-determination bodies,
- and a demonstrated absence of feasible internal remedies.

**Bridge function:**  
This protects the default position of no closures, while addressing management/investor insistence that no site should be exempt from economic review forever.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To management
**What exact savings gap remains after you apply every internal measure you consider realistic, and can you present that number site by site rather than in aggregate?**

### To the controlling family
**Will you commit to defining the company’s long-term stewardship obligations in a way that includes material owner contributions before asking for irreversible workforce concessions?**

### To institutional investors
**What level of verified savings and governance assurance would make you accept a non-closure restructuring as sufficiently execution-credible?**

### To the works council
**Which temporary internal measures are you prepared to support immediately if they are paired with binding no-closure and no-compulsory-redundancy guarantees?**

### To the union
**What is the maximum duration and scope of temporary workforce contribution you would consider acceptable if the savings are independently verified and management/owners also contribute?**

### To the regional state government
**Are you prepared to support a conditional framework that protects sites up front but includes a narrowly defined review mechanism if the audit proves the non-closure path insufficient?**

### To the expert auditor
**What minimum data set and methodology would you require to determine, with confidence, whether a non-closure restructuring is sufficient or not?**

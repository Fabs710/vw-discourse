We will not accept plant closures in Germany and we will not accept compulsory redundancies.

That is the clear position of the works council.

We acknowledge that the company is under pressure and that change is necessary. But the burden cannot be placed one-sidedly on the workforce through site closures, broken promises, and job cuts. Management must stop treating decades of employee loyalty and the historic job-security framework as disposable.

Our priorities are clear:

- Keep every German plant open
- Exclude forced layoffs
- Preserve co-determination and the existing job-security framework
- Secure binding guarantees for sites and jobs through 2030
- Ensure that any restructuring is negotiated fairly, transparently, and with real employee influence

If savings are needed, we are prepared to discuss alternatives that protect jobs and sites: capacity adjustments, working-time solutions, internal redeployment, qualification, voluntary instruments, and a fair contribution from all sides. But we will not sign off on closures or operational redundancies.

Management must come to the table with a credible plan that protects the workforce, not a fait accompli. If the proposal remains built on plant closures and forced cuts, we will resist it with all the means available to us.

## Core impasse

The central deadlock is now clear:

1. **Employee-side actors** want binding security: no plant closures, no compulsory redundancies, and protected co-determination through 2030.
2. **Management and investors** want proof of durable structural correction: permanent capacity and fixed-cost reduction, with closures still available if non-closure tools do not close the gap.
3. **State and family ownership** are trying to avoid both extremes: they reject a closure-first shock, but they also do not want a symbolic compromise that leaves the cost base unchanged.

So the actual impasse is not “change vs no change.”  
It is:

> **Can the restructuring deliver closure-level permanent savings and capacity reduction without closures, while still providing enough security and enforceability for labour to accept it?**

That question remains unresolved.

---

## Facilitator compromise positions

### 1) Conditional no-closure pact with a built-in review trigger
A possible bridge would be a **time-bound no-closure framework** through 2030, but only if it is paired with:

- quantified permanent savings targets
- explicit capacity reduction measures
- site specialization commitments
- voluntary workforce reduction instruments
- management and owner contributions
- a formal review trigger if milestones are missed

This would give the employee side security, while giving management and investors a fallback if the package underperforms.

---

### 2) Hybrid restructuring with protected sites and defined capacity loss
A second compromise would be a **hybrid site-preserving package** in which:

- all German sites remain open for now,
- selected plants lose a full shift or equivalent durable capacity,
- product responsibilities are concentrated,
- voluntary exits and retraining are used,
- and site roles are formally redefined in writing

This could address management’s demand for structural change without immediately crossing into closure.

---

### 3) Security-for-contribution bargain with enforceable burden-sharing
A third route would be a **reciprocal burden-sharing package**:

- employees contribute through temporary restraint, flexibility, and voluntary exits
- management contributes through overhead cuts and governance commitments
- owners contribute through capital support and investment pledges
- the state facilitates a site-preserving settlement
- in return, there is a binding no-forced-redundancy commitment and strict consultation rights over site changes

This would aim to make security conditional on measurable structural delivery from all sides.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### Works Council
What specific combination of shift loss, specialization, voluntary exits, and overhead cuts would you regard as sufficient to deliver permanent restructuring **without** plant closures?

### Industrial Union
What level of worker contribution would you accept if it were tied to binding no-forced-redundancy protection and enforceable investment commitments?

### Group & Brand Management
What exact savings and capacity-reduction threshold would convince you that a no-closure package is structurally enough to remove closures from the table?

### Regional State Government
Which legally and politically enforceable conditions would you require before supporting a no-closure compromise with state backing?

### Controlling Family Holding
What ownership-side contribution are you prepared to make in order to secure a site-preserving restructuring that still materially improves competitiveness?

### Institutional Investors
What minimum level of permanent savings and execution certainty would make a hybrid no-closure solution acceptable to you?

### Restructuring Finance Specialist
What would be the clearest threshold at which you would say the non-closure package is financially equivalent enough to the closure-heavy option?

### Independent Industrial Restructuring Advisor
Which concrete industrial measures would you treat as the minimum credible substitute for one or more closures?

As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany**
- **No compulsory redundancies / forced layoffs**
- **No dismantling of co-determination or the historic job-security framework**

We are not blind to the company’s challenges. We know the market is under pressure, costs must be addressed, and the transition to electric mobility requires a viable industrial plan. But the burden of adjustment cannot be imposed by sacrificing jobs, plants, and livelihoods while management preserves all strategic flexibility for itself.

What we will argue for is a **socially responsible restructuring** that protects the workforce and the industrial base:

- binding **site and employment guarantees through 2030**
- **capacity adjustments** through negotiated, reversible measures rather than closures
- **working-time reductions, internal reallocation, qualification, and retirement-based tools**
- a genuine **employee role in shaping the transformation**, not just being presented with a fait accompli
- a fair contribution from management, owners, and capital side before asking workers to give up anything fundamental

If management wants our cooperation, it must stop treating German plants as disposable cost items. We will resist any plan that breaks the historic job-security pledge or threatens a closure. If necessary, we are prepared to mobilize the workforce and use all co-determination rights available to us.

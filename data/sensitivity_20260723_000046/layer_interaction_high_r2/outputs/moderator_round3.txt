Here is a neutral facilitator readout of where the round has plateaued, followed by concrete bridge options and one pointed question to each stakeholder.

## Core impasse
The discussion is now blocked on one central issue:

**Employee representatives and the state want a no-closure / no-compulsory-redundancy framework as the starting point; management, owners, and investors want the option of footprint reduction to remain available if the quantified gap cannot otherwise be closed.**

A second, related impasse is procedural:

**Both sides say “show the numbers,” but they disagree on the decision rule for what the numbers are allowed to justify.**  
- Employee/state side: numbers must first prove that all non-closure options are exhausted.  
- Management/owner/investor side: numbers should decide whether closures remain necessary if the residual gap remains material.

So the plateau is not mainly about whether the company has a problem. It is about **what counts as an acceptable solution before irreversible measures are considered**.

---

## Facilitator proposals for possible compromise

### Proposal 1: “No forced measures first, then gated escalation”
A staged agreement could start with:
- no compulsory redundancies,
- no plant closures during the initial implementation phase,
- immediate launch of the non-closure package,
- monthly or quarterly joint verification of savings.

Then, only if jointly verified milestones are missed, the parties move to a second-stage negotiation on deeper structural measures.

**Purpose:** gives the employee side immediate protection and gives management a defined pathway if the package underdelivers.

---

### Proposal 2: “Temporary site protection with predefined review triggers”
A time-limited site and employment protection could be paired with:
- quantified savings targets,
- binding management and owner contributions,
- documented investment commitments,
- and a review trigger based on audited results rather than a discretionary management declaration.

The review trigger would not automatically authorize closures; it would only reopen talks on additional measures if the agreed package does not deliver.

**Purpose:** turns the dispute from “yes/no closures now” into “what objective threshold would justify reopening the question.”

---

### Proposal 3: “Shared-sacrifice package with asymmetric safeguards”
A compromise package could combine:
- labor contributions: temporary wage restraint, bonus deferral, working-time flexibility, voluntary exits, redeployment,
- management/owner contributions: executive restraint, deferred distributions, investment commitments, overhead cuts,
- safeguards: no compulsory redundancies, no immediate closure decisions, co-determination in implementation.

If the numbers still leave a large residual gap, the parties would agree in advance to a separate second-stage process rather than predetermining closures now.

**Purpose:** keeps the package economically serious while avoiding an immediate confrontation over irreversible site decisions.

## One pointed question to each stakeholder

### 1) Works Council
**What quantified savings threshold would you accept as proof that the negotiated non-closure package is insufficient, and what exact decision rule would you accept for reopening the issue without treating that as a disguised closure trigger?**

### 2) Industrial Union
**If no compulsory redundancies and no closures are your red lines, what specific permanent labor-cost and productivity concessions are you prepared to recommend to members so the package can close a multi-billion-euro gap?**

### 3) Group & Brand Management
**Will you commit to a jointly audited savings bridge, with closure excluded during the first implementation phase, so long as the non-closure package meets defined milestones and the residual gap is objectively reviewed?**

### 4) Regional State Government
**What binding support instruments are you prepared to provide for investment and redeployment, and under what audited conditions would you allow the discussion to move from site protection to deeper restructuring?**

### 5) Controlling Family Holding
**Would you accept a time-limited no-closure framework if it is paired with measurable owner and management contributions and a predefined review point based on audited performance rather than negotiated impressions?**

### 6) Institutional Investors
**What minimum structural savings and return-on-sales improvement would make a non-closure package acceptable to you, and would you support a staged process that delays footprint reduction while those savings are being verified?**

### 7) Expert
**Can you provide a simple low / central / high savings bridge showing how much of the required gap can realistically be closed by non-closure measures before footprint reduction becomes economically dominant?**

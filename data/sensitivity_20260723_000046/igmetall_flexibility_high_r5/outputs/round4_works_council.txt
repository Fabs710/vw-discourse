- no-closure alternatives have been exhausted in good faith,

What I hear from you is still the same threat logic:
- keep closures available,
- keep compulsory redundancies available,
- keep job guarantees conditional,
- then pressure the workforce until we call that “shared sacrifice.”

That is not a plan I can support.

## To the industrial union

So yes:
- working-time flexibility,
- voluntary exits,
- retraining,
- productivity measures,
- temporary wage moderation,
- moderation of bonus elements.

But no:
- closures,
- compulsory redundancies,
- backdoor erosion of job security,

- no immediate closures,
- no compulsory redundancies,
- real capacity adjustment through internal measures,

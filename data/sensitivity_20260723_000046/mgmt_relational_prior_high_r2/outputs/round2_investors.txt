## What the expert analysis means for us
The specialist’s framing is exactly the one I want to see:

- **Not:** closures vs no closures as a slogan

## My position

I would also support a negotiated alternative **only if** it can demonstrate:
1. **equivalent savings**
2. **similar durability**
3. **high execution certainty**
4. **a believable return to target returns on sales**
5. **no repeat of the same issue in 2–3 years**

A “middle way” that is really just delay is not acceptable.

## What would move me
At this point, I want management to put numbers on the table in a way that can be tested against the alternative package. Specifically:
- plant-by-plant fixed cost and utilization,
- run-rate savings from closure vs non-closure measures,
- one-off restructuring charges,
- timing of cash impact,
- and the effect on margin trajectory.

Without that, the debate is still too political and not financial enough.

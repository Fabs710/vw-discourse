Core impasse identified
- The conflict is no longer about whether restructuring is needed; all sides accept that.
- The impasse is about **the form of restructuring**:
  - Employee side, works council, union, and regional state: want **no German plant closures and no compulsory redundancies**, with binding job/site security.
  - Management, controlling family, and investors: require **material, durable savings** and say the current footprint/cost structure cannot survive unchanged.
- In practical terms, the gap is between:
  1. **socially protected adjustment** and
  2. **economically sufficient adjustment**.

Facilitator compromise proposals
1. **No immediate closures, but binding structural reduction inside the footprint**
   - Keep all sites formally open for now.
   - Reduce capacity through shift cuts, program consolidation, and site specialization.
   - Use voluntary exits, retirement, redeployment, and hiring freezes to reduce headcount.
   - Attach this to verifiable savings targets and a review clause.

2. **Time-bound security pact with trigger-based fallback**
   - Grant a binding job/security framework for a limited period.
   - In return, labour accepts temporary cost relief, flexibility, and productivity measures.
   - If agreed savings or utilization milestones are missed, pre-defined fallback measures become available for negotiation.

3. **Shared-burden package with explicit quantified contributions**
   - Labour: temporary wage moderation, flexibility, and internal mobility.
   - Management: overhead cuts, investment commitments, and operational discipline.
   - Owners/investors: support for one-time restructuring costs.
   - State: conditional backing tied to preservation of industrial capability and site commitments.

Pointed question to each stakeholder

- **Works Council:**  
  If all German plants must remain open, what specific capacity and workforce reductions are you prepared to accept to close the economics gap?

- **Industrial Union:**  
  What is the maximum time-bound labour contribution you would accept, and under what binding guarantees on jobs, sites, and investment?

- **Group & Brand Management:**  
  What quantified savings plan can you present that demonstrates why alternatives to closures are insufficient?

- **Regional State Government:**  
  What concrete support are you prepared to offer to make a no-closure restructuring economically credible, beyond political opposition to closures?

- **Controlling Family Holding:**  
  Under what exact economic conditions would you accept a no-closure or no-forced-redundancy settlement as sufficiently credible?

- **Institutional Investors:**  
  What minimum savings, capacity reduction, and implementation certainty would you require before backing a compromise that avoids closures?

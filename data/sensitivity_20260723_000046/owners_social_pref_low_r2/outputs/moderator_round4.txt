As facilitator, the **core impasse** is now clearly procedural and not semantic:

## Core impasse
1. **Employee-side red lines are absolute**  
   The works council, union, and state are aligned on:
   - no German plant closures,
   - no compulsory redundancies,
   - no closure-by-stealth.

2. **Management / investors need structural savings at scale**  
   They are aligned on:
   - cosmetic measures are insufficient,
   - the cost base must come down permanently,
   - internal restructuring must be deep enough to be board-credible.

3. **The unresolved gap is “how deep is deep enough” without crossing red lines**  
   The expert input suggests a no-closure package may reach roughly **60–85% of closure economics**, but the parties have not agreed on:
   - the minimum acceptable savings floor,
   - the minimum capacity reduction,
   - the minimum headcount adjustment,
   - and whether temporary employee contributions can be paired with binding no-closure guarantees.

---

## Facilitator compromise positions to bridge the gap

### Compromise position 1: Deep no-closure package with a quantified savings floor
A structured package could be built around:
- no German plant closures,
- no compulsory redundancies,
- permanent shift elimination,
- line consolidation,
- overhead reduction,
- voluntary exits / retirement pathways,
- redeployment and qualification,
- temporary labor-cost relief,

**paired with a pre-agreed quantified savings floor**.

**Procedural safeguard:**  
If the floor is missed, the response would not automatically reopen closures, but would trigger a defined joint review of additional internal measures and burden-sharing from all parties.

---

### Compromise position 2: Site-preserving restructuring with differentiated burden-sharing
A second bridge would be a **tiered internal restructuring**:
- every German site remains open,
- but not every site carries the same adjustment,
- some sites absorb more shift reduction or line consolidation than others,
- with transparent criteria and a binding fairness formula.

**Procedural safeguard:**  
A joint oversight mechanism would review whether any proposal crosses into closure-by-stealth.

---

### Compromise position 3: Time-bound employee contribution with binding counter-commitments
A third option is a **temporary employee contribution**:
- wage moderation,
- bonus restraint,
- working-time flexibility,

in exchange for:
- binding investment commitments,
- product allocation commitments,
- a no-compulsory-redundancy rule for the duration,
- and a monitored restructuring plan.

**Procedural safeguard:**  
The package would include a review date, but not an automatic return to closure demands.

## My position has not changed

- compulsory redundancies,

I am prepared to discuss and support:
- capacity reductions **without** site shutdowns,
- working-time adjustments,
- internal redeployment,
- qualification and training,
- productivity measures,
- management overhead cuts,
- and a fair contribution from owners.

That is the route forward. Not closures.

- all non-closure alternatives have been exhausted,
- the same durable savings cannot be achieved otherwise,

- If a no-closure package closes the gap, do you support it?

As the Works Council, our position is clear:

We will not accept plant closures in Germany, and we will not accept compulsory redundancies.

The workforce did not cause this crisis alone, and it cannot be expected to pay for it by losing its sites, its livelihoods, and the industrial base built over decades. Management’s move to break historic job-security guarantees is a profound breach of trust. If the company wants cooperation, it must come with binding security for the people and plants that make this company work.

What we will support:
- A restructuring that avoids closures and forced layoffs
- Binding site guarantees for all German plants
- Job guarantees through 2030
- Genuine co-determination in shaping the restructuring, not a fait accompli
- Measures such as working-time adjustments, capacity reductions, redeployment, training, and negotiated contributions if they are fair and reversible

What we will oppose:
- Any closure of German plants
- Any operational dismissals
- Any unilateral cost-cutting that shifts the burden onto employees alone
- Any plan that destroys trust by abandoning the company’s long-standing social commitments

We are prepared to discuss alternatives, but only on the basis that the German plants remain open and the workforce receives credible protection. Without that, there is no acceptable deal.

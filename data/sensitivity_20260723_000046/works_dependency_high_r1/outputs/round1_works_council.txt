The works council’s position is clear:

We will not accept plant closures in Germany, and we will not accept compulsory redundancies. Those are red lines.

We recognize that the company faces serious pressure and that a restructuring is necessary. But the burden cannot be placed one-sidedly on the workforce while management breaks historic job-security guarantees and destroys trust. Any solution must protect the German sites, preserve employment, and secure the company’s long-term industrial future.

What we will argue for:
- Binding site guarantees for all German plants
- A clear no-layoff commitment, with reductions only through natural attrition, retirements, voluntary solutions, and qualification measures
- A negotiated industrial plan that keeps production and investment in Germany
- Real co-determination in shaping the restructuring, not a fait accompli
- A fair contribution from management and owners as well, not just the workforce
- A credible path to 2030 that secures jobs, skills, and future products

What we reject:
- Any plant closure
- Any forced layoffs
- Any unilateral announcement presented as non-negotiable
- Any restructuring that sacrifices employees to fix strategic failures elsewhere

If management wants a viable settlement, it must come with binding guarantees and a serious alternative to closures and compulsory job cuts. Otherwise, we will resist this with all the means available to us.

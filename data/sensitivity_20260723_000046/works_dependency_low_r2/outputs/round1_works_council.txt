We will not accept plant closures in Germany and we will not accept compulsory redundancies.

That is the clear position of the works council.

We acknowledge that the company is under pressure and that change is necessary. But the burden cannot be placed on the workforce through unilateral closures, mass job cuts, or the dismantling of the historic job-security framework. Management is presenting a crisis response that shifts all risk onto employees while ignoring alternatives.

Our priorities are:

- preserve every German plant
- protect all jobs through 2030
- avoid forced layoffs
- secure binding investment and future-product commitments
- ensure co-determination is part of shaping the restructuring, not just being informed after the fact

If savings are needed, then they must come through negotiated, socially responsible measures:
- capacity reductions where necessary, but not plant shutdowns
- working-time and flexibility arrangements
- internal redeployment and qualification
- fair, temporary employee contributions only if matched by binding guarantees
- a credible long-term industrial plan for German sites

We are prepared to discuss solutions, but only on the basis of job and site security. If management comes with closures, forced redundancies, or breaking the trust of the workforce, we will resist that decisively — in the supervisory board, in bargaining, and, if necessary, through industrial action.

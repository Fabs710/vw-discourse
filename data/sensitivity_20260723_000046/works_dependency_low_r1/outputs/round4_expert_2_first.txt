## Finance judgment on the nature of the measures
Based on the descriptions given, the proposed non-closure tools are likely to fall into three buckets:

### 1) Measures that can help but are usually not sufficient alone
- temporary wage restraint
- bonus moderation / deferral
- overtime reduction
- working-time flexibility
- short-term outsourcing cuts

These can create meaningful relief, but **they are not usually enough to eliminate a deep structural cost gap at a large auto manufacturer** unless the gap is modest.

### 2) Measures that can be durable if large enough
- permanent overhead reduction
- sustained procurement savings
- internal redeployment
- headcount reduction via voluntary exits / retirement
- product allocation / utilization improvement

These can close real gap, but only if the scale is material and the implementation is credible.

### 3) Measures that may be economically useful but are not a substitute for structural adjustment
- extended guarantees
- milestone frameworks without enforceable cost outcomes
- phased review gates

These may support implementation, but **they do not by themselves generate savings**.

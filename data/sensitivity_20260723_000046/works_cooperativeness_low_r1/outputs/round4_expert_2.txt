The minimum data set I would require is already close to what the expert outlined, and I will state it in plant-economics terms.

## Required data, by site

### 1) Site financial baseline
- Site P&L on both:
  - contribution margin basis
  - fully loaded basis
- Fixed vs variable cost split
- Labor cost per vehicle and per labor hour
- Conversion cost per unit
- Energy, maintenance, logistics, and internal freight costs
- Depreciation and amortization by site
- Overhead allocation method and allocation base

### 2) Production and utilization
- Installed capacity by line
- Actual output, by month and by model
- Utilization rate
- Shift pattern and line loading
- Bottleneck analysis
- Scrap, rework, and downtime
- Changeover losses
- Mix of models and powertrains

### 3) Labor structure
- Headcount by function, grade, and site
- Wage bill including social charges
- Overtime reliance
- Temporary labor share
- Absenteeism
- Natural attrition profile
- Skills/qualification constraints
- Working-time regime and flexibility limits

### 4) Product and volume forecast
- 3–5 year volume forecast by model and site
- Product launch and phase-out schedule
- EV / ICE / hybrid mix
- Downside, base, and upside demand scenarios
- Transfer volumes and export assumptions

### 5) Measure-by-measure savings model
For each non-closure measure, I need:
- recurring annual savings
- one-time implementation cost
- timing of cash impact
- reversibility
- effect on productivity and quality

Measures include:
- voluntary exits
- early retirement
- working-time changes
- shift changes
- wage or bonus concessions
- redeployment
- outsourcing / insourcing changes
- specialization or volume transfers
- productivity programs

### 6) Capex and competitiveness needs
- Retooling requirements
- Automation capex
- Facility upgrade needs
- Ramp-up costs
- Payback period
- Minimum efficient scale by site

### 7) Scenario comparison
At a minimum:
1. status quo / light measures
2. non-closure restructuring package
3. closure or deep restructuring fallback

For each scenario:
- operating margin effect
- cash impact
- one-time restructuring cost
- payback period
- break-even volume
- downside sensitivity

## What is not sufficient
- Group-only averages
- Germany-wide aggregates with no plant split
- Headcount cuts without site economics
- Savings claims without one-time cost and timing
- Temporary concessions treated as structural savings

## Decision test
A non-closure package is only credible if it:
- closes the gap with recurring savings,
- materially lowers fixed cost,
- preserves acceptable utilization,
- and remains viable under a lower-volume downside case.

If the package mainly depends on temporary concessions or optimistic demand recovery, it is not durable enough to replace closure options in an economic sense.

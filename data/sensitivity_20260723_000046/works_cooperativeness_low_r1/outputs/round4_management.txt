## On the proposed compromise models

### 1) Time-limited no-closure / no-forced-redundancy phase

- verified site-level economics,
- workforce contributions,
- management and owner contributions,

### 3) “No compulsory redundancies first”
Again, I can discuss prioritising voluntary tools. But “first” only works if there is a credible second step. If the second step is removed in advance, then the first step becomes a lock-in to underperformance.

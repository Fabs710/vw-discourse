## My position as controlling shareholder

I agree with the expert on one key point: **this is a quantitative question, not an ideological one**.  
The company needs a restructuring that is **durable, credible, and sufficient** to restore margins. A cosmetic deal is unacceptable.

Where I differ from some of the other parties is this: I will not support a package that simply **preserves every legacy structure by default** and hopes the economics improve on their own. That would be a slow-motion erosion of enterprise value, and I will not accept it.

At the same time, I do **not** support turning plant closures and compulsory redundancies into the default instrument. That would risk prolonged conflict, reputational damage, and permanent harm to the brand and workforce capability. That is value-destructive as well.

So the standard remains:

- **real structural improvement**
- **durable savings**
- **a fair but meaningful labour contribution**
- **implementation that does not blow up industrial relations**
- **no permanent impairment of the enterprise or governance control**

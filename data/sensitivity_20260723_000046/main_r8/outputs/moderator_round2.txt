Here is the procedural readout from a neutral facilitation perspective.

## Core impasse

The main deadlock is **not** over whether the company needs restructuring. There is broad agreement on that.

The impasse is over **sequencing and proof**:

- **Management / investors** say the restructuring must include a sufficiently large structural footprint and cost correction, and that closures may be necessary if alternatives do not close the gap.
- **Works council / union** say closures and compulsory redundancies must not be treated as default options and that management has not yet proved they are unavoidable.
- **State / family owners** want a durable, quantified solution but are resisting an outcome that appears socially destructive or politically unacceptable.

So the central conflict is:

> **How to achieve a quantified, durable savings bridge while avoiding closures and forced layoffs unless they are proven indispensable.**

A second, related impasse is **trust**: each side doubts the others will honor commitments after concessions are made.

---

## Facilitator compromise proposals

### Proposal 1: “Non-closure first, residual-closure only if verified”
A phased framework:
1. A defined period for a **non-closure package** to be negotiated and implemented.
2. Management publishes a **full savings bridge** showing what non-closure measures achieve.
3. An independent reviewer verifies whether a residual gap remains.
4. Only if the gap is still material would any site-specific adjustment be reopened.

**Why this might bridge the gap:**  
It gives labour a real chance to avoid closures while giving management a path to prove necessity if needed.

---

### Proposal 2: “Employment protection in exchange for quantified flexibility”
A package built around:
- no compulsory redundancies,
- strong redeployment and qualification commitments,
- working-time flexibility,
- voluntary exits only,
- and limited wage/bonus or benefit moderation,
in exchange for:
- binding investment commitments,
- product allocation guarantees,
- and a monitored savings target.

**Why this might bridge the gap:**  
It preserves the workforce’s core red lines while addressing management’s need for measurable cost relief.

---

### Proposal 3: “Mixed package with protected sites and capped structural change”
A compromise architecture:
- one or more **pilot measures** first: shifts, hours, productivity, voluntary reductions, internal transfers;
- a **cap** on immediate structural changes;
- a jointly agreed point at which management may revisit footprint changes only if the cap fails to deliver the required numbers.

**Why this might bridge the gap:**  
It avoids an all-or-nothing closure confrontation and creates a controlled escalation path.

## One pointed question to each stakeholder

### To Group & Brand Management
**What exact annual savings target must be met, and what specific portion of that target can be achieved without closures or compulsory redundancies?**

### To Works Council
**What quantified employee-side contribution would you accept if the company commits in writing to no compulsory redundancies, binding investment, and enforceable job-security provisions through 2030?**

### To Industrial Union
**What combination of working-time, wage, redeployment, and voluntary measures would you recognize as a materially sufficient alternative to closures, and what numbers would define “sufficient”?**

### To Regional State Government
**Will you support an independent verification process that may, if the data justify it, confirm a residual need for site-specific changes after non-closure measures are tested?**

### To Controlling Family Holding
**Will you commit to backing the scenario that is independently verified as economically sufficient, even if it requires substantial but non-closure restructuring, provided the governance and long-term value case holds?**

### To Institutional Investors
**Would you support a settlement that avoids closures if it delivers a verified, durable margin recovery and a clearly documented savings bridge?**

### To Independent Industrial Restructuring Analyst
**What minimum data set is required to determine whether the non-closure package truly closes the gap, and by when can that verification be completed?**

As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany**
- **No compulsory redundancies / forced layoffs**
- **No dismantling of the historic job-security framework**
- **No unilateral fait accompli from management**

We recognise that the company faces serious economic pressure. We do not deny the need for restructuring, productivity improvements, or a stronger cost base. But the burden of adjustment **cannot be shifted onto the workforce through closures and job destruction** while management demands that employees pay for strategic missteps.

What we will argue for instead is a **binding, negotiated solution** that protects the future of the German sites:

1. **Site and employment guarantees through 2030**
2. **A genuine industrial strategy for all German plants**
3. **Alternatives to forced cuts**, such as:
   - capacity adjustments through socially responsible measures,
   - working-time solutions,
   - voluntary instruments only,
   - qualification and redeployment,
   - investment commitments for new products and technology
4. **Full co-determination in shaping the restructuring**, not after the fact, but as a real part of the decision-making process

If management comes with closures, layoffs, or the breaking of established guarantees, we will oppose that decisively. The workforce has already shown it is prepared to defend its interests, and we will continue to do so.

We are prepared to contribute to a viable future for the company — but only on the basis of **security, fairness, and shared responsibility**, not sacrifice imposed on one side alone.

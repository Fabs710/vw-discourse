## Core impasse
1. **The workforce side and state side want a non-closure path to be the primary, testable option**, with no compulsory redundancies and strong site protection.
2. **Management and investors want assurance that enough structural cost and capacity will actually be removed**, and they are not prepared to rule out closures if the numbers do not close.
3. **The disagreement is no longer about whether restructuring is needed, but about the sequencing and proof standard**:
   - whether non-closure measures must be fully exhausted before any closure is considered,
   - or whether closures must remain available as an early fallback if savings prove insufficient.

The dispute therefore centers on:
- **burden of proof**
- **degree of binding protection**
- **what counts as equivalent structural savings**
- **whether a hybrid package is acceptable before a non-closure model is fully validated**

## Facilitator proposals for possible compromise
### Proposal 1: “Non-closure first, closure as triggered fallback”
A structured agreement could be built around:
- a **binding non-closure restructuring phase** for a defined period,
- **no compulsory redundancies** during that phase,
- **site-by-site capacity, utilization, and savings targets**,
- and a **pre-agreed trigger mechanism** under which limited closure options return to the table only if quantified milestones are missed.

This would let the workforce and state secure a genuine first attempt at non-closure restructuring, while giving management and investors a defined fallback if the numbers do not materialize.

### Proposal 2: “Conditional protection in exchange for measurable labor contribution”
A second compromise could pair:
- **temporary no-closure commitments**
- with **time-limited labor-cost moderation, voluntary exits, shift reductions, and productivity measures**,
- plus **binding management and ownership contributions** such as overhead cuts, executive restraint, and investment sequencing.

This would create a reciprocal bargain: protection for sites and jobs in exchange for measurable, temporary, and monitored cost relief.

### Proposal 3: “Hybrid package with protected core sites”
A third option could separate the footprint into:
- a **protected core set of sites** with explicit guarantees,
- and a **limited set of weaker sites** subject to deeper restructuring measures, including consolidation, transfer of product lines, or, if unavoidable after review, limited closure.

This may bridge the gap by preserving the most politically and industrially important sites while still giving management a route to obtain substantial structural savings.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To Works Council / Union
If a binding no-closure phase is granted, **what specific volume of voluntary exits, working-time moderation, and productivity gains can you commit to delivering within 12–24 months so the company can still achieve material structural savings?**

### To Management / Executive Management
If a serious non-closure model is put forward, **what exact residual savings gap remains after labor-cost moderation, capacity consolidation, overhead cuts, and voluntary workforce reduction are applied site by site?**

### To the Regional State Government
If management presents a quantified non-closure plan but later claims a limited site closure is still needed, **under what measurable conditions would you be prepared to support fallback restructuring, and what protections would you require before that point?**

### To the Controlling Family Holding
If a non-closure package produces strong but not perfect savings, **what minimum threshold of structural improvement would you consider sufficient to rule out closure and support long-term ownership patience?**

### To Institutional Investors
If the company accepts a temporary labor contribution and delivers a quantified restructuring path, **what margin, cash flow, or return threshold would make you support a non-closure settlement rather than insist on a hybrid closure option?**

### To the Expert

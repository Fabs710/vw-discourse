We share the core line that **closures and compulsory redundancies are not an acceptable way out**. If management believes it can restore competitiveness by pushing the full cost of the crisis onto the workforce, then it is taking the wrong path.

Let me be equally clear from the trade-union side:

- **No plant closures**
- **No forced layoffs**
- **No dismantling of the collective wage framework**
- **No one-sided wage cuts imposed on employees while capital and management are spared**

We are prepared to talk about a **fair shared-sacrifice package**, and we already signaled that with our counter-proposal. That means we can discuss:

- temporary or negotiated **labour-cost contributions**
- **capacity reduction** through socially responsible tools
- **working-time adjustments**
- **redeployment and qualification**
- **foregoing bonuses or other cost components**
- a **jointly agreed transformation fund**

But that openness has a limit. It depends on one basic condition: **jobs and sites must be secured, and the burden must be shared by everyone** — management, owners, and workforce alike.

If management wants flexibility, then it must also commit to:

1. **binding site guarantees**
2. **employment security**
3. **investments in the German plants**
4. **a credible product and technology path**
5. **real contributions from the ownership side and top management, not just demands on employees**

I agree with the Works Council that co-determination must not be bypassed. But I also want to underline that this is not just a site-level question: the collective agreement and the workforce as a whole are at stake. If management tries to force through a restructuring that breaks the wage framework or uses threats of closure to blackmail the workforce, we will answer with **industrial action**.

So the message is simple: **we are ready to negotiate hard, but not to accept a solution where workers carry all the pain while everyone else is protected.** If there is a serious, balanced package, we can talk. If not, we will escalate.

## Practical restructuring logic
A realistic sequence in large industrial turnarounds is often:
1. **Extract negotiated employee concessions**
2. **Use attrition, early retirement, and redeployment first**
3. **Reduce utilization and capacity where possible**
4. **Only then decide whether the remaining gap requires closures or involuntary headcount reduction**

That sequencing is consistent with industrial restructuring discipline. It is also the only way to test whether closures are truly necessary rather than merely preferred.

We are aligned on the core red lines: no plant closures in Germany, no compulsory redundancies, and no unilateral dismantling of the collective framework.

From the union side, I want to add two points very clearly:

1. **The workforce cannot be asked to carry the full burden.**  
   If management wants a restructuring contribution from employees, then we need a **balanced package**: management, owners, and shareholders must also contribute materially. That means no one-sided wage cuts, no blank cheque on job losses, and no shifting all risks onto the workforce while dividends, executive structures, or strategic mistakes remain untouched.

2. **Any sacrifice must buy something durable.**  
   We are not prepared to trade away income or standards for vague promises. If there is to be an employee contribution, it must be tied to **binding guarantees** on:
   - site security,
   - no compulsory redundancies,
   - preservation of the collective wage framework,
   - clear investment commitments,
   - and a credible industrial plan for future models and capacity.

We have already shown openness to a negotiated solution, including capacity adjustments and labour-cost measures. That openness should not be mistaken for weakness. The peace obligation has ended, and members expect us to defend their interests. If management comes with closures or forced layoffs, we will not treat that as a basis for “discussion” but as an attack on the workforce.

So my position is:

- **Yes** to a negotiated restructuring that spreads the burden fairly.
- **Yes** to temporary or partial employee contributions if they are matched by management and owners.
- **Yes** to capacity reduction if it avoids closures and protects employment.
- **No** to compulsory redundancies.
- **No** to plant closures.
- **No** to breaking the collective wage framework as the main adjustment tool.

If the company wants peace and a durable settlement, it needs to come back with a proposal that protects jobs and sites first, and only then discusses employee contributions.

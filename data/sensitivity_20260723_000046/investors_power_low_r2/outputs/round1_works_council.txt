As the Works Council, our position is clear:

We will not accept plant closures in Germany, and we will not accept compulsory redundancies.

The company’s crisis cannot be solved by making workers and entire regions pay the price for management’s strategic mistakes. We have already shown willingness to discuss responsible adjustments, but only on the basis of binding safeguards for jobs, sites, and co-determination. Any restructuring must preserve every German plant, protect the workforce, and secure the future of the company through 2030 and beyond.

What we can discuss:
- Alternative savings without closures
- Capacity adjustments through negotiated, reversible measures
- Working-time solutions
- Fair employee contributions, but only if the burden is shared and the sacrifice is limited
- A credible investment and product commitment for the German sites
- Binding guarantees against operational layoffs and site shutdowns
- A real role for employee representatives in shaping the restructuring

What we reject:
- Unilateral closure plans
- Forced layoffs
- Breaking historic job-security commitments
- Using crisis rhetoric to strip away protections that have been built over decades

If management wants a solution, it must come with binding job and site guarantees, not threats. We are prepared to negotiate, but only to protect the workforce and the long-term industrial future of all German locations.

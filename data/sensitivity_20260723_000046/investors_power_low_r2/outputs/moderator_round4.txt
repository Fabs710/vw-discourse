### Core impasse
The central deadlock is between:

1. **Labour-side red lines**  
   - no plant closures  
   - no compulsory redundancies  
   - no backdoor closure logic after a test phase  

2. **Management / capital-side requirement**  
   - a restructuring that is large enough to be **durably bankable**  
   - with a real fallback if non-destructive measures do not close the gap  

In short:  
**Labour wants a restructuring without closure risk; management and investors want a restructuring that remains credible if the non-closure package underperforms.**  
The state and family holding are trying to avoid immediate closures while not fully removing them as an eventual option, which keeps the disagreement alive.

---

### Facilitator proposals

#### Proposal A: Protected test phase with pre-agreed decision rules
A time-limited, independent **non-closure test phase** could be established with:

- no plant closures during the test phase  
- no compulsory redundancies during the test phase  
- no outsourcing or attrition-based substitute closures  
- a quantified labour-side and management-side package implemented during the phase  
- independent verification at the end  
- **pre-agreed criteria** for whether the package is judged sufficient

**Bridge value:**  
This gives labour hard protection during the test, while giving management and investors a serious, measurable process rather than an open-ended promise.

---

#### Proposal B: Two-stage package with a non-destructive first stage and a jointly governed second-stage trigger
A compromise could separate the package into:

- **Stage 1:** all non-destructive measures, fully implemented and quantified  
  - working-time changes  
  - shift/utilisation adjustments  
  - voluntary exits  
  - redeployment  
  - productivity measures  
  - management and ownership contribution  

- **Stage 2:** only if a jointly verified residual gap remains, a second negotiation on additional instruments  
  - but with a governance requirement that any second-stage option must still preserve employment and co-determination as far as legally and operationally possible

**Bridge value:**  
This avoids an immediate closure ultimatum, while also avoiding a promise that the first stage alone will automatically be sufficient.

---

#### Proposal C: Site-security framework with differentiated guarantees
Instead of a blanket “all sites equally protected” vs. “all sites equally exposed,” the parties could consider:

- a **core-site protection list** with binding no-closure and no-forced-redundancy commitments  
- a **non-core site investment and product commitment schedule**  
- explicit redeployment obligations across the footprint  
- annual review tied to investment delivery and performance

**Bridge value:**  
This may give management and investors enough flexibility to claim realism, while giving labour and the state concrete protection for priority sites.

### Pointed question to each stakeholder

#### To management
**What exact savings gap remains after all non-destructive measures you are prepared to implement immediately, and what independent evidence shows those measures would still be insufficient?**

#### To the Works Council
**Would you accept a time-limited test phase if closures and compulsory redundancies are strictly prohibited during that phase and the end-state is decided only after independent verification?**

#### To the trade union
**What level of temporary labour contribution would you consider acceptable if it is fully reversible, time-limited, and matched by binding management and ownership contribution?**

#### To the regional state government
**Are you prepared to translate your opposition to closures into binding governance action, and if so, what concrete veto or blocking steps will you use?**

#### To the controlling family holding
**Will you commit to a written position that no site exit can be triggered unless independently verified non-destructive measures fail, and that any fallback requires renewed board-level approval?**

#### To investors
**Would you support a settlement that avoids closures and forced redundancies if it is independently verified to close most of the margin gap and includes enforceable downside protections?**

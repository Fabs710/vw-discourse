## Core impasse

The main blockage is **not** over the need to restructure. All sides now accept that the status quo is unsustainable.

The impasse is over **how far management may go before exhausting non-closure measures**:

- **Labour / state / works council** want a restructuring that starts from **site preservation, no compulsory redundancies, and no closure threat**.
- **Management / investors / family holding** want **closures and compulsory redundancies to remain available** unless a no-closure package can be proven economically equivalent.
- The experts have narrowed this to a technical question: **whether a no-closure package can close the savings and capacity gap sufficiently, given implementation risk and fixed-cost structure**.

So the core dispute is:
1. **Trigger question**: Must closure remain an active option from the start?
2. **Equivalence question**: Can a no-closure package deliver enough recurring savings?
3. **Risk question**: Even if the numbers work, is a closure-heavy path too conflict-prone to be optimal?

## Facilitator compromise proposals

### Proposal 1: Sequenced restructuring with a binding evidence test
A three-stage process:
1. **Immediate no-closure period** with binding job-protection terms for a fixed window.
2. **Independent site-by-site economic review** using the same assumptions for all scenarios.
3. **Pre-agreed decision rule**: if the no-closure package misses defined savings/utilization thresholds, the parties return to negotiation with a narrower set of options.

**Bridge value:**  
This gives labour temporary protection and gives management/investors a serious economic test.

---

### Proposal 2: Mixed burden-sharing package with asymmetric security
A package in which:
- employees provide **temporary and reversible** contributions,
- management and owners provide **cash, governance, or investment contributions**,
- there are **no compulsory redundancies during the package term**,
- and site closures are **not used as leverage** during implementation.

A later review can occur, but only on **performance of the package**, not as an open-ended shutdown option.

**Bridge value:**  
This addresses labour’s demand for security while preserving a review mechanism that shareholders and management can treat as credible.

---

### Proposal 3: Site-preservation default with exception by proof
Adopt a rule that:
- each German site is presumed to remain open,
- management may propose an exception only with **plant-specific proof** of non-viability,
- and any exception must be paired with a **site alternative analysis** showing why redeployment, product allocation, working-time changes, and voluntary exits cannot suffice.

**Bridge value:**  
This preserves the political and social priority of the footprint, while still allowing management to act if a site truly fails the test.

## Pointed questions to each stakeholder

### To Works Council
**What specific economic threshold would persuade you that a no-closure package has failed, while still preserving your red lines on compulsory redundancies and site protection?**

### To Industrial Union
**Would you accept a time-bound no-closure trial if it includes measurable savings targets, independent verification, and a binding management/owner contribution alongside temporary employee concessions?**

### To Management
**What are the plant-by-plant savings and utilization numbers showing that non-closure measures have truly been exhausted before any closure option is invoked?**

### To Regional State Government
**What enforceable support can the state offer to make a no-closure settlement viable, without turning the state’s role into a permanent veto over restructuring?**

### To Controlling Family Holding
**Would you publicly back a time-limited no-closure restructuring if it contains hard economic targets, independent monitoring, and a predefined review point?**

### To Institutional Investors
**What minimum recurring annual savings and margin improvement would make a no-closure package acceptable to you on a risk-adjusted basis?**

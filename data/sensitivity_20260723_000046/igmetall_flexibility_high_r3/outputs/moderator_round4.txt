As facilitator, the core impasse appears to be this:

## Core impasse
The parties disagree on the **decision threshold for closures**.

- **Labour side / works council / union / state**:  
  A strong no-closure package should be tested first, with binding site and job guarantees, and closures should not be used as an upfront lever.

- **Management / investors / family**:  
  A no-closure package may be worth testing, but only if it closes enough of the structural gap; otherwise closures or selective rationalization must remain available.

So the deadlock is not over the need for restructuring itself. It is over:
1. **whether closures may remain on the table during the test**, and
2. **what counts as “sufficient” savings from a no-closure package**.

---

## Facilitator compromise positions

### Proposal 1: Time-limited protected no-closure test
A compromise could be a **defined test period** for a strong no-closure package, during which:
- no German plant closures occur,
- no compulsory redundancies occur,
- workforce contributions are implemented,
- management and owner contributions are implemented,
- site-by-site transparency is provided,
- and a joint review is conducted against agreed milestones.

At the end of the period, the parties assess whether the package closes the gap enough to avoid further structural measures.

**Purpose:** gives labour security during the test, while giving management a real quantified review.

---

### Proposal 2: Conditional fallback from closures to selective rationalization only
Instead of an immediate jump from no-closure to full closure, the parties could agree a **stepwise fallback ladder**:

1. strong no-closure package,
2. if insufficient, selective rationalization measures,
3. only if still insufficient, revisit more severe site actions.

This would preserve the labour side’s demand that closures not be the first lever, while preserving management’s demand that the company not be frozen in place.

**Purpose:** narrows the conflict by inserting an intermediate option before closure.

---

### Proposal 3: Binding security package paired with measurable economic triggers
A third compromise could combine:
- **binding site/job security through a defined horizon**, and
- **pre-agreed economic triggers** that determine whether further measures are discussed.

For example:
- if the agreed savings and utilization targets are met, no closures;
- if the targets are missed by a defined margin, the parties reopen talks on additional structural measures.

**Purpose:** gives labour enforceable protection, while giving management a rules-based trigger rather than an open-ended prohibition.

## One pointed question to each stakeholder

### To the Works Council
**What exact measurable conditions would make you accept that the no-closure package has succeeded enough to avoid further structural measures, and who would verify those numbers?**

### To the Industrial Union
**What level of quantified, recurring savings would you consider sufficient for the union to support a time-limited protected no-closure settlement?**

### To Group & Brand Management
**Will you commit to a defined no-closure testing period with no plant closures and no compulsory redundancies, provided that a transparent site-by-site savings bridge and milestone review are agreed?**

### To the Regional State Government
**Will you specify in advance the conditions under which you would use your blocking position, so that your stance is understood as a rule rather than a political signal?**

### To the Controlling Family Holding
**Are you prepared to support a protected no-closure test even if it temporarily limits closure leverage, provided the test includes hard economic milestones and a defined review point?**

### To the Institutional Investors
**Would you support a structured no-closure trial if it is time-limited, measurable, and paired with an automatic review of selective rationalization only if the agreed gap remains materially open?**

### To the Expert
**Can you define, in operational terms, the minimum savings and utilization thresholds that would make a no-closure package credibly sufficient across the sites?**

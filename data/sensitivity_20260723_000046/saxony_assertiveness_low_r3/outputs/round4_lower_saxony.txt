Where I disagree is your repeated move from “there is a ceiling” to “therefore closures remain necessary.”

- attrition and non-replacement,
- voluntary exits,
- working-time changes,
- shift rationalization,
- redeployment and qualification,
- and a limited labor-cost contribution.

What I will not support is:

- using the threat of closures to force the workforce into submission,

## To the controlling family

A plan is not “strong” just because it is severe. A strong plan is one that can actually be implemented and deliver durable savings without blowing up the company’s social and political foundation.

## Core impasse
The central impasse is **not** whether the company needs restructuring. All sides accept that it does.

The impasse is **how much capacity and cost must be removed, by what mechanism, and with what binding guarantees**:

- **Employee side** insists that restructuring must happen **without German plant closures or compulsory redundancies**, and with binding job/site protection.
- **Management and capital providers** insist that the savings must be **structural, durable, and quantitatively equivalent** to closure-based savings.
- **State and family/owners** are positioned between those poles, wanting **viability without irreversible regional damage**.

So the dispute is really about **equivalence, enforceability, and trust**:
1. What savings gap must be closed?
2. Can non-closure measures truly match closure economics?
3. Can any deal be made binding enough to survive future reversal?

## Facilitator compromise positions
Below are three concrete bridge options that could be tabled procedurally.

### 1) Phased hybrid restructuring with a no-closure review trigger
**Structure**
- Immediate package of voluntary exits, working-time measures, wage/bonus moderation, and productivity measures.
- Capacity consolidation via product-line specialization across sites.
- A **moratorium on closures for a defined period**.
- A **review trigger** if agreed savings or utilization targets are missed.

**Purpose**
- Gives labor the sought-after protection against immediate closures.
- Gives management a path to structural savings and an escape hatch if the negotiated package fails.

**Key feature**
- Any later closure consideration would require **objective trigger criteria** and renewed bargaining, not unilateral action.

---

### 2) Site-guarantee package tied to quantified savings and investment commitments
**Structure**
- Binding site guarantees for selected German plants.
- In exchange, labor accepts a defined multi-year package of:
  - working-time flexibility,
  - temporary wage moderation,
  - bonus suspension,
  - voluntary departures,
  - productivity commitments.
- Management and owners commit to **specified investment amounts** and product allocation to the protected sites.

**Purpose**
- Trades certainty on selected sites for measurable cost relief and future-product commitments.
- Limits the conflict by distinguishing between **protected core sites** and sites subject to restructuring.

**Key feature**
- Guarantees are contingent on both sides meeting measurable obligations.

---

### 3) Independent verification and staged bargaining on equivalence
**Structure**
- Appoint a jointly accepted independent expert team to verify:
  - the exact savings gap,
  - the closure scenario economics,
  - the non-closure/hybrid scenario economics,
  - and the enforceability of each.
- Negotiation proceeds in stages:
  1. agree the baseline gap,
  2. agree the target savings,
  3. assign burdens by stakeholder group,
  4. then settle the mechanism.

**Purpose**
- Reduces the current “assertion vs assertion” stalemate.
- Shifts the debate from positions to verified numbers.

**Key feature**
- If non-closure measures prove insufficient, the parties can negotiate a hybrid fallback rather than jumping straight to full closure.

## Pointed question to each stakeholder

### To Works Council
**What specific, quantified set of measures would you accept as genuinely equivalent to closures in recurring annual savings, and what enforcement mechanism would you accept if those savings are not achieved?**

### To Industrial Union
**Which labor concessions are you prepared to make in duration, scope, and binding effect, and under what verified savings target would you consider them sufficient to justify avoiding closures?**

### To Group & Brand Management
**What exact savings gap are you seeking, by site and by year, and which elements of the closure proposal are you prepared to replace with a verifiable hybrid package if equivalent savings can be demonstrated?**

### To Regional State Government
**What level of employment and site protection are you prepared to conditionally support, and what objective performance triggers would justify withdrawing that support if the negotiated package fails?**

### To Controlling Family Holding
**Would you support a hybrid settlement that preserves more sites but still requires measurable labor concessions, capacity consolidation, and binding investment commitments, if it is verified to deliver comparable long-term value?**

### To Institutional Investors
**What minimum combination of recurring savings, enforceability, and capacity reduction would you require before you would back a non-closure or hybrid solution over a closure-led one?**

### To Industrial Restructuring / Turnaround Finance Specialist
**What data points are essential to determine whether the alternative package is truly equivalent, and which single metric would you treat as the decisive test if the parties disagree?**

## Industrial-planning assessment
The key variable is **utilization**:

- If the network is already near efficient loading, internal measures may be enough.
- If plants are materially underutilized, then keeping all sites open preserves too much fixed cost.
- In that case, savings from labor measures alone are capped, because overhead, maintenance, logistics complexity, and under-absorption remain.

That is why a no-closure solution often fails in structurally overbuilt footprints: the savings are real, but not deep enough.

On the evidence provided, the most credible non-closure path is:

- shift elimination / line consolidation
- voluntary exits
- redeployment and retraining
- working-time changes
- overhead simplification
- selective product or volume reassignment

That can get closer to the needed savings than “internal measures” alone, but it still depends on whether the remaining network can reach acceptable utilization.

As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany**
- **No compulsory redundancies / forced layoffs**
- **No dismantling of co-determination and no breaking of the historic job-security framework**

Management cannot ask the workforce to pay for a crisis it did not create by threatening sites, livelihoods, and the industrial future of our regions. We will not accept a fait accompli.

What we are prepared to discuss is a **real restructuring path that protects all German plants and jobs**, with:
- **Binding site guarantees through 2030**
- **A credible investment and product commitment for the German locations**
- **Capacity adjustments through working-time measures, temporary arrangements, voluntary instruments, and negotiated cost contributions**
- **A genuine role for employee representatives in shaping the plan**, not merely rubber-stamping it after the fact

If management wants a durable solution, it must stop treating closures and layoffs as the starting point. The starting point must be: **preserve sites, preserve jobs, preserve the company’s industrial base in Germany**. Anything else will meet fierce resistance.

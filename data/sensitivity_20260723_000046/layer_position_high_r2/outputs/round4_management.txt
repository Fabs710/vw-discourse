## To the works council
You keep saying closures are the wrong benchmark and that management must first prove non-closure alternatives are exhausted. I disagree.

The benchmark is not what feels preferable. The benchmark is what restores competitiveness **on a durable, risk-adjusted basis**.

- all German plants open,
- no compulsory redundancies,
- no weakening of guarantees,
- no closure option even as fallback.

That is, in effect, a veto on restructuring. I cannot accept that.

If you want to avoid closures, then show me a package that delivers:
- materially lower fixed cost,
- real capacity reduction,
- a smaller workforce cost base,
- and durable savings that survive 2030.

Not slogans. Not moral appeals. Numbers.

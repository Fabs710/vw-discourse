### Moderator Summary — Round 1

#### 1) What has been agreed
- All parties accept that the company faces a serious competitiveness and cost problem.
- There is broad support for a negotiated rather than unilateral solution.
- Several measures are seen as potentially part of a package:
  - voluntary exits,
  - redeployment and internal reallocation,
  - working-time adjustments,
  - training and qualification,
  - targeted wage/bonus moderation,
  - productivity and efficiency measures.
- There is shared recognition that future investment and industrial viability matter.

#### 2) Core disagreements that remain
- **Plant closures in Germany:**  
  Works Council, union, and state strongly reject them; management says they remain possible; investors and family holding do not endorse them as a default but do not rule out hard capacity cuts if necessary.
- **Compulsory redundancies:**  
  Employee side and state reject them; management will not fully rule them out; capital side wants meaningful headcount reduction if needed.
- **Scope of site/job guarantees:**  
  Labour and state want binding guarantees, while management says blanket guarantees through 2030 are not feasible.
- **Burden sharing:**  
  Employee side wants concessions matched by management/owners; management and investors say employee contributions must be real and economically sufficient; no agreement yet on the scale or duration of concessions.
- **How to achieve capacity reduction:**  
  No shared view yet on whether alternative instruments can deliver enough savings without closures or layoffs.

#### 3) Key trade-offs still unaddressed
- How to balance **job and site security** against **required cost and capacity reduction**.
- Whether the needed savings can be achieved through **voluntary, flexible, and negotiated measures** alone.
- What level of **employee contribution** is acceptable, and what guarantees it must be paired with.
- What **investment commitments** are feasible in return for concessions.
- How to make any deal credible enough for both **implementation inside the company** and **confidence outside the company**.

#### 4) Brief refocus for the next round
Please move from principle statements to a **quantified package discussion**:
- management should specify the savings target, timing, and which measures are essential;
- labour/state should indicate which forms of flexibility and concession could be considered if paired with binding protections;
- all sides should identify where there is room for compromise on capacity reduction without immediate closures or forced layoffs.

The next round should aim to narrow the gap on a concrete, balanced restructuring formula.

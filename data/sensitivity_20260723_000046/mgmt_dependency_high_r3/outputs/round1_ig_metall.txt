We share the core line on several points, and I want to be equally clear where we do **not** move.

First: **no compulsory redundancies, no plant closures in Germany, and no dismantling of the collective wage framework**. That is not a negotiating preference; it is the minimum for any serious agreement. If the company tries to solve this crisis by throwing workers out while preserving managerial comfort and owner returns, that will not be accepted.

Where I agree with the Works Council: the workforce did not create this crisis, and a viable restructuring must be based on **shared sacrifice**, not one-sided pain. We are prepared to discuss:
- capacity reductions **without closures**
- working-time measures
- voluntary instruments only
- temporary labour-cost concessions
- employee financial contributions if they are tied to clear guarantees

But any employee contribution must come with **binding counterparties**:
- site and job security
- investment commitments for the German plants
- a credible product and volume plan
- management and owner contributions as well, not just wage restraint from employees

What I reject outright:
- closure logic disguised as “network optimization”
- forced layoffs or open-ended employment insecurity
- permanent wage dumping or the removal of long-standing protections
- a restructuring where the workforce pays, but management and owners do not

If management truly believes competitiveness requires action, then it must present a package that spreads the burden fairly:
- no closures
- no compulsory redundancies
- no unilateral wage cuts
- real reductions on the cost side that include management, overhead, and ownership side discipline
- a durable agreement that protects the industrial base and gives the workforce security through 2030

We are ready to negotiate hard and constructively. But let me be equally explicit: if the proposal remains centered on closures, forced cuts, and shifting the crisis onto employees alone, then industrial action remains on the table and will be used.

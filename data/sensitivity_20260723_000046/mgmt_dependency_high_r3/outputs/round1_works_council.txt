As the Works Council, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the essentials:

- **No plant closures in Germany.**
- **No compulsory redundancies.**
- **No unilateral dismantling of the historic job-security framework.**

The workforce did not create this crisis, and we will not accept that its solution is paid for by destroying jobs, sites, and livelihoods. We are prepared to discuss a restructuring **only if it is based on securing every German plant, preserving co-determination, and protecting employment through to 2030**.

What we can consider:
- capacity adjustments **without closures**
- working-time solutions
- socially responsible, voluntary measures only
- a credible investment and product commitment for all German sites
- binding guarantees for jobs and locations through 2030
- a genuine role for employee representatives in shaping any restructuring

What we will oppose:
- closure plans presented as a fait accompli
- forced layoffs or mass job cuts
- shifting the burden onto the workforce while management and owners stay insulated
- any “restructuring” that weakens the long-term industrial base in Germany

If management wants a deal, it must be a **fair, negotiated solution that protects sites and people**. If it comes with closures or compulsory redundancies, it will meet fierce resistance from us.

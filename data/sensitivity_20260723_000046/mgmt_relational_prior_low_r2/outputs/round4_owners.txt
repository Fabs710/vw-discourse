Rigid blanket guarantees through 2030 are too inflexible if they block adaptation.

I can support:
- time-limited protections,
- conditional commitments,

I cannot support an arrangement that disables governance and prevents adaptation to a worsening market.

### 4) On “no closures unless proven unavoidable”
The analyst’s assessment makes this clearer: if non-closure measures are to avoid closures, they must be **economically near-equivalent**.

That means:
- real capacity reduction,
- meaningful labor-cost relief,
- overhead reduction,
- and hard delivery discipline.

A weak package that simply postpones the pain is not an alternative.

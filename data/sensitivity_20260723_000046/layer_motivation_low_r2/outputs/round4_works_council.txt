### 2) Conditional no-closure moratorium linked to verified milestones
Rejected.

That is not a neutral process. It is a threat with milestones attached.

### 3) Site-and-investment guarantee in exchange for bounded labour contributions
This is the only route that resembles a serious basis for talks — but only if the guarantee is not merely conditional rhetoric.

It must include:
- concrete sites,
- concrete duration,
- concrete investment,
- concrete product commitments,
- concrete employment protection,
- and a binding end to closure speculation during the agreement period.

Without that, you are asking the workforce to finance the company’s future while the company retains the right to discard them.

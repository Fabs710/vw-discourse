We understand the Union’s deep concerns for retirees, workers and their communities—and we share them. But we cannot accept open-ended vetoes, two-year no-closure guarantees or an 18-month deadline for the final 5% labour cut without derailing the turnaround. Below is our firm counter-proposal. It holds every core deal term intact, strengthens enforceability, and addresses your four points as far as our rescue‐plan constraints allow.

1. Retiree-Health Package  
 • 40% PV Cut and Equity: Remains at 7.5% base + 2.5% performance earn-out (identical FCF hurdles).  
 • Enhanced Transparency: Monthly public reporting on FCF vs. earn-out targets; a formal Cap-&-Fund Oversight Committee mid-Year-2 review to validate funding paths.  
 • Unamendable Protections: Senior-secured status, pre-emptive rights, cap-&-fund veto mechanics and all equity terms will be locked into the Plan as non-waivable covenants. Any dilution attempt automatically triggers a re-opener under the collective-action framework—but no blanket veto.  

2. Labour-Cost Savings Phasing & Composition  
 • 20% PV Target: 15% by Month 12, remaining 5% by Month 15—firm. Month 15 is non-negotiable; slipping beyond it risks our credibility with the Task Force and financiers.  
 • Productivity Floor: At least 70% of those savings must arise from joint productivity/work-rule reforms and redeployment programs—no more than 30% from pure wage cuts.  
 • Labour Partnership Pool: A dedicated 5% equity pool, funded at Day 55, earmarked for upskilling and redeployment projects, managed by the CBA-enshrined committee.  
 • Re-Opener Trigger: If by Month 12 we miss the 70% productivity floor, the Union seat may invoke an automatic, time-boxed re-negotiation on work-rule and deployment measures. That process cannot delay the Month 15 cut-date.  

3. Regional Plant-Closure Safeguards  
 • Global Viability Test: Three-quarter EBIT-loss metric remains the sole closure trigger.  
 • Extended Grace Period: In regions with unemployment > 7%, no plant can be closed or divested for 18 months post-closing. This balances community needs against global footprint optimization.  
 • Redeployment Plans: Any closure proposal within that 18-month window must include a jointly approved redeployment/retraining plan.  
 • Local Labour Oversight: One dedicated local-labour representative on each region’s Community-Transition Fund committee, alongside government, company and creditor appointees.  
 • Re-Opener Trigger: If we fail to deliver or fund a jointly approved redeployment plan during the grace period, the Union may demand a prompt renegotiation of that plant’s transition terms—but cannot veto the underlying viability test.  

4. Board Seats and Veto-Style Protections  
 • One Full Voting Director for the Union and one for the Retiree Trust. Bondholders retain their non-voting observer seat with covenant vetoes as before; management gives up its seat.  
 • No Open-Ended Veto: We will not grant absolute, unwaivable veto powers. Instead, both Union/Trust seats carry narrowly tailored “re-opener” triggers—described above—for material breaches of:  
    – The Month 12 productivity floor;  
    – Month 15 labour-cut deadline;  
    – Redeployment funding or plan execution in high-unemployment regions.  
 • Unamendable Plan Covenants: All triggers and protections are written into the reorganization Plan as non-waivable, automatic provisions.  

Why This Works for Survival and Enforceability  
 • It protects the Task Force’s hard non-negotiables: 40% retiree cut, 20% labour savings by M15, 15% capacity reduction via the viability test, 50% bond haircut package.  
 • It embeds substantial, binding protections for workers and retirees—transparency, an upskilling pool, re-opener enforcement and an 18-month no-closure window—without granting unlimited veto rights that would paralyze the company.  
 • It secures high-unemployment communities with an extended grace period and local oversight, while preserving the global restructuring discipline we need.  
 • It keeps our timetable intact: term sheet by Day 35, docs by Day 45, close by Day 55. Any delay or additional conditions beyond this fully collapses the consensus path.

We share the urgency to get a consensual plan done by Day 60—but make no mistake: without our funds, there is no rescue.  Our demands are non-negotiable and must be in the final term sheet by Day 35, with full documentation by Day 45 and closing by Day 55.  Here’s what we will—and will not—accept:

1. Five-Year, Legally Enforceable No-Closure Covenant  
  • For every plant in our jurisdiction that “passes” the common three-quarter EBIT-loss viability test, we require an unamendable five-year prohibition on closure or divestiture.  
  • Any proposal to close, sell or substantially downsize one of these plants must receive our explicit, affirmative vote by our board director.  No substitutes (observer status, mere consultation) will suffice.

2. Minimum Regional Footprint and Workforce Preservation  
  • We insist on preserving at least 85% of our current regional capacity and 90% of our current workforce under that covenant.  
  • Should any plant fall below those thresholds, the only permitted alternative is a joint redeployment/retraining plan—also subject to our veto at the board level.  
  • If, notwithstanding these safeguards, a plant is deemed non-viable under the EBIT test, a regional Community-Transition Fund is activated (50% central government DIP / 30% company / 20% creditors), with our appointees sharing oversight equally with the lead funder and labour.

3. Equity Stake Pari-Passu with Central Government  
  • We will commit up to €X billion (≈Y% of total rescue financing).  In return, we must receive Y% of post-reorg common equity—fully pari-passu with the central government’s taxpayer stake, with identical voting, dividend and pre-emptive rights.  
  • Our equity must carry the same five-year exit timetable and cannot be diluted, subordinated or carved out as a junior class.

4. Board Representation and Veto Rights  
  • One full voting director on the reorg board, plus one observer seat on the Audit & Restructuring Committee.  
  • Our board director must have explicit, unwaivable veto authority over any decision to close/divest plants in our jurisdiction—regardless of the viability test outcome—and over any amendments undermining our no-closure covenant.  
  • We will also have one seat on the Labour & Restructuring Committee and one on the Community-Transition Fund committee.

5. Acceptance of Core Rescue Economics  
  • We endorse the lead funder’s framework for 20% PV labour-cost savings, a 40% PV retiree-health haircut (with 7.5% equity + 2.5% earn-out for the Trust), 15% global capacity rationalization, and the bondholders’ 50% haircut package—provided our plant guarantees and equity terms remain intact.  

6. Timeline and Binding Documentation  
  • Term Sheet: Signed and binding by Day 35, including our no-closure covenant, equity provisions and veto rights.  
  • Full Documentation: All co-funding agreements, equity issuances and covenants in Plan documents by Day 45.  
  • Closing: Financing in hands by Day 55.  

If these conditions are not met in the term sheet by Day 35, we will withhold our funds—and a court-supervised restructuring will proceed without our participation, risking the very plants and jobs we are mandated to protect. We urge the Task Force and all parties to confirm acceptance of these clauses by close of play tomorrow so that we can move immediately into documentation.

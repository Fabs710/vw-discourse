Core Impasse  
  1. Veto vs. re-opener: Unions, co-funders and bondholders demand open-ended veto rights and five-year protections; the Task Force and management insist on automatic, formula-driven “re-openers” with no open vetoes.  
  2. No-closure duration: Management/Task Force propose an 18-month regional grace period; the Union wants two years for high-unemployment plants; neighbouring governments insist on a full five years.  
  3. Equity and governance shares: Retirees seek 8.0% base + 2.5% earn-out, bondholders want 10% warrants + pari-passu upside, co-funders demand equity pari-passu with central government—management offers 7.5% + 2.5% earn-out, limited warrants and observer seats.  
  4. Labour-cost phasing: Management insists on 15% savings by M-12 and remaining 5% by M-15; the Union wants to push the final 5% to M-18 tied to upskilling, with composition floor and veto enforcement.  

Facilitator Proposals for Compromise  
Proposal A – Tiered No-Closure with Performance Extension  
  • Every plant protected from closure for three years post-close.  
  • High-unemployment regions automatically extend protection by two additional years if defined redeployment-and-training metrics aren’t met by M-24.  
  • Extension is triggered by a formula (e.g., <80% redeployment rate), leading to an automatic, time-boxed re-opener on transition measures—not a flat veto.  

Proposal B – Blended Equity Upside & Phased Labour Cuts  
  • Retiree Trust: Base equity at 7.75% of post-reorg common stock + full 2.5% earn-out.  
  • Bondholders: 7.5% new common-stock warrants + additional 2.5% if Retiree Trust hits its full earn-out (pari-passu upside).  
  • Co-funders: Pari-passu equity equal to central government less a 0.25% liquidity reserve.  
  • Labour savings: 15% by M-12; final 5% by M-16 (one-month compromise) with a 70% productivity floor. Missing the floor triggers an automatic, 60-day re-opener—no slip beyond M-16.  

Proposal C – Governance Seats with Sunset Vetoes  
  • Board seats: one each for Union, Retiree Trust, Bondholders and Co-funders; management gives up its extra seat.  
  • Full veto rights for each seat over its core sphere (retiree health, labour-cost phasing, bond covenants, plant closures) for the first 24 months only.  
  • After Month 24, those veto rights convert into formula-driven “re-openers” protected by non-waivable Plan covenants and enforced by an independent arbitration panel.

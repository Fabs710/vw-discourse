Unsecured Bondholders’ Committee – Round 3

We remain committed to a consensual restructuring—provided our recovery is meaningful, timely and pari­passu with all other unsecured claims. Management’s revised framework moves in the right direction, but three critical issues remain unaddressed:

1. Haircut and Consideration  
   • 50% headline reduction is acceptable only on these precise terms:  
     – 30% of original principal paid in cash at deal closing (no further delays or “holdbacks”).  
     – 20% rolled into new senior unsecured notes (5-year tenor) at a minimum 8.0% coupon, under full protective covenants (cross-default, anti-dilution, cap on new secured debt).  
     – Warrants exercisable into 15% of post-reorg equity (instead of 10%), with a five-year life, standard anti-dilution protection and pari-passu treatment with all other equity classes from Day 1.  
   • These adjustments reflect our financing of fresh liquidity and compensate for residual execution risk.

2. Pari-Passu Treatment with the Retiree Trust  
   • We cannot accept a structure whereby the Trust can earn up to an extra 2.5% of equity on performance triggers while bondholders are capped at 15% outright.  
   • Either the Trust’s earn-out must be removed, or we must receive a fully symmetrical performance-based re-opener: if Trust equity is increased, bondholders receive an identical top-up in warrants or convertible rights.  
   • Under no circumstances will we accept a permanently subordinated upside position relative to the Trust.

3. Governance Rights and Covenants  
   • Board Seat: We need one voting director (not merely an observer) with explicit veto rights over:  
     – Any amendment or waiver of our new-notes’ covenants;  
     – Any issuance of secured debt above a de minimis threshold.  
   • Closing Mechanics: Our cash component must be funded at signing subject only to customary, non-material closing conditions.  
   • Any proposal that keeps us as a non-voting observer or delays our cash payment beyond closing is unacceptable.

4. Resolution and Timing  
   • We will consent to the deal only if the points above are embedded in the term sheet by Day 32, full documentation by Day 45, and closing by Day 55.  
   • Absent these enhancements, we will withhold consent and prepare for the court-supervised restructuring—where, as the task force has signaled, the floor haircut will at least be 60%, net of DIP and administrative costs.

Why our terms are fair and necessary  
   • They secure immediate cash, reduce execution risk, and deliver real upside for bondholders—all within the government’s 40–60% haircut envelope.  
   • They protect the integrity of pari-passu unsecured claims and prevent a one-way upside allocation in favor of the Trust.  
   • They furnish the company with fresh liquidity on market terms, not concessional or deferred considerations.  

We stand ready to enter the four-party working group today, but only on these amplified terms. Otherwise, we will have no choice but to force the court-supervised path and fight for full enforcement of our pari-passu rights.

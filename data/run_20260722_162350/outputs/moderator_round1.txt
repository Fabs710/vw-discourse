1. Agreed items  
 • 60-day deal deadline and preference for a consensual, binding restructuring, with a fallback to a court-supervised process if no consensus.  
 • Broad sacrifice framework: all private claimants (labour, retirees, unsecured bondholders, management/shareholders) to take material cuts; government to provide bridge financing and temporary equity.  
 • Target ranges in line with core requirements: roughly 20% overall labour-cost savings, a 25–50% present-value reduction in retiree-health obligations, and a 40–60% haircut on unsecured debt.  
 • Establishment of a four-party working group (lead task force, management, union/trust, bondholders) aiming to finalize legal documentation by Day 40.  
 • New governance structure including independent directors, reserved seats for unions/retirees and co-funders, and government or creditor oversight until exit.  

2. Core disagreements  
 • Labour-cost reductions: union seeks a phased 15% cut with strong job-security guarantees; government insists on 20% in Year 1, management splits into 10% immediate and 10% deferred.  
 • Retiree-health cuts: government demands 40–50% PV reduction; union offers 25–30%; management proposes 37.5% with a secured claim and 5% equity.  
 • Unsecured bond haircut and treatment: government’s 40–60% target versus bondholders’ 50% haircut but with higher cash (30%), higher yield rollover, and 15% equity upside; bondholders also reject subordinating their claim to the retiree trust.  
 • Capacity/plant closures: government and management target 15–20% or ~12% capacity cuts via a viability test; union and neighbouring governments demand tighter regional “no-closure” covenants for viable plants.  
 • Equity and governance shares: union/trust seek 8–10% equity; management offers 5%; neighbouring governments demand equity proportional to their funding; bondholders seek board veto/observer rights.  

3. Key unaddressed trade-offs  
 • Final allocation of equity among labour, retirees, bondholders, co-funders and government.  
 • Seniority and pari-passu ranking between the retiree trust and unsecured bondholders, and any compensating governance or economic rights.  
 • Balancing depth of capacity reductions against regional “no-closure” carve-outs and workforce protections.  
 • Composition and veto powers of the post-reorg board.  

4. Refocus for Round 3  
 • Narrow percentage bands for labour, retiree-health and bond haircuts into final terms.  
 • Resolve the retiree-trust versus bondholder ranking and any compensatory equity/governance.  
 • Finalize the capacity-closure criteria, carve-outs for domestic plants and community-transition funding.  
 • Agree on precise equity stakes for each stakeholder and define board-seat and veto-right allocations.  
 • Confirm timeline and key milestones for documentation and financing closings.

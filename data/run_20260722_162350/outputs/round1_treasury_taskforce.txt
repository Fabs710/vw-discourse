As the government task force and sole source of emergency financing, our position is clear and non-negotiable on the fundamentals:

1.  Sixty-Day Deadline and Process  
    •  We must see a binding agreement within 60 days or we will transition the company into an accelerated court-supervised restructuring.  
    •  We prefer a consensual deal—fast, binding, and outside the courts—but only if it meets our core requirements.

2.  Deep, Verifiable Sacrifices by All Private Claimants  
    •  Labour and Retiree Health Trust: We expect substantial restructuring of legacy labour costs and retiree health obligations—at least a 40–50% present-value reduction of the retiree health claims, with a credible funding mechanism for the balance. Any labour-cost savings must be legally binding (new contracts, co-payments, earlier retirement options, wage adjustments tied to productivity).  
    •  Unsecured Bondholders: We require a haircut in the range of 40–60% on the face value of unsecured debt, converted in part into equity in the reorganized company. Partial rollover into new instruments is acceptable, but only if yields reflect the company’s post-restructuring risk profile and provide fresh liquidity.  
    •  Management and Shareholders: Existing equity must be wiped out or diluted to near zero. Management equity-incentive plans must be entirely re-priced to new-co performance milestones.

3.  Credible Path to Profitability and Cash-Break-Even  
    •  Cost Base: Achieve at least 20% reduction in overall labour costs and a 15% reduction in fixed overhead within the first year.  
    •  Plant and Brand Footprint: Commit to closure or sale of under-performing plants (targeting a 15–20% capacity cut) and exit no-longer-viable brands, with a firm timetable.  
    •  Governance: New board structure with strong independent members, reserved seats for creditor and labour representatives, and government oversight until exit.

4.  Role for Neighbouring Government Co-Funding  
    •  We welcome parallel support from neighbouring governments—but only on identical terms of sacrifice, governance rights, and equity participation. No special carve-outs or unilateral guarantees.

5.  Ownership and Exit Strategy  
    •  The government will take a temporary equity stake to preserve value for taxpayers, with a clear exit plan no later than 5 years. All private investors and labour must accept that, as part of their sacrifices, they will be minority stakeholders in new equity.

What’s unacceptable:  
— Any proposal that leaves the balance sheet burdened by unreformed retiree or labour costs.  
— An unsecured bond recovery above 60% of face value.  
— Open-ended subsidies or incremental top-ups beyond our 60-day bridge commitment.

We stand ready to support a swift, consensual restructuring that meets these conditions. Otherwise, we will shift to the court route and impose these terms unilaterally.

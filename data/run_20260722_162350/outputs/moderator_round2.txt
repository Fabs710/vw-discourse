1. Agreed Items  
  • Labour‐Cost Savings: 20% present‐value reduction.  
  • Retiree‐Health Cut: 40% PV haircut on obligations.  
  • Capacity Rationalization: 15% global reduction via the common viability test.  
  • Unsecured Haircut: 50% principal reduction, with 30% cash, 20% new 5-year notes at ~7.5–8%, plus warrants.  
  • Seniority: Retiree Trust holds a senior secured claim ahead of unsecured creditors.  
  • Cap-&-Fund Mechanism: Joint company–Trust oversight committee enshrined in plan documents.  
  • Equity Pools:  
    – Central government up to 25%; exit by Year 5.  
    – Labour Partnership Pool 5%.  
    – Co-funders pari-passu with government.  
  • Board Structure (9 seats): 3 independents (including chair), plus government, union, retiree‐trust, co-funder, management, and one bondholder seat (observer/non-voting or with limited rights, depending on version).  
  • Timeline: Execute term sheets by Day 33–35, full docs by Day 45, closing by Day 55.

2. Core Disagreements  
  a. Retiree Health Trust’s Equity vs PV Cut  
     – Government/management: 7.5% equity; union wants 8% if cut held at 35%; bondholders demand symmetrical upside.  
  b. Labour-Cost Phasing & Composition  
     – Government: 15% by Month 12, 5% by Month 15; union pushes the final 5% back to Month 24; management offers Month 18.  
     – Union insists 70% of savings from productivity/work-rule reforms; government wants legally binding total cuts.  
  c. Job-Security and Closure Carve-Outs  
     – Government/management: no region-specific carve-outs beyond the viability test, with limited grace period in high-unemployment areas.  
     – Union and co-funders demand five-year no-closure guarantees for defined footprints and explicit veto rights for regional governments.  
  d. Bondholders’ Governance & Upside  
     – Government: one non-voting observer seat, 10% equity warrants.  
     – Bondholders: demand a full voting director, veto rights over debt covenants/secured debt, and 15% in warrants (or symmetric re-openers).  

3. Key Trade-Offs Still Unresolved  
  • Trust vs Bondholders: final equity percentages and pari-passu upside on performance earn-outs.  
  • Phasing Timing: balancing front-loaded savings against the union’s need for time to upskill and redeploy.  
  • Closure Guarantees: reconciling a uniform viability test with regional “no-closure” and co-funder veto demands.  
  • Governance Rights: scope of veto or blocking rights for union, retiree-trust, bondholders and co-funders.  

4. Refocus for Next Round  
  – Determine a single figure for the Trust’s equity stake (7.5% base + possible earn-out vs 8% flat).  
  – Agree on a compromise phasing schedule (Month 15, 18 or 24) and minimum productivity floor.  
  – Clarify the scope and duration of no-closure covenants and any regional veto rights.  
  – Align bondholder upside and governance rights with those of the Trust (parity on performance triggers; voting vs observer status).  
  – Confirm final term-sheet milestones and locking language on cap-&-fund and seniority positions.

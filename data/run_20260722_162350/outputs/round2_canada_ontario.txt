Thank you. We share everyone’s urgency and the imperative to lock in a consensual plan before Day 60—but we cannot fund without firm, enforceable protections for our domestic plants and workforce and an ownership stake fully commensurate with our financing. Below are our non‐negotiable terms:

1. No-Closure Guarantees for Our Jurisdiction  
  • Binding Covenant: For all plants in our region that pass the common viability test (three consecutive quarters of post–cost-cut EBIT losses), we require a five-year, legally enforceable “no-closure” covenant. Any proposal to close or divest these plants must also obtain our affirmative consent via our board representative’s veto.  
  • Minimum Footprint Preservation: We insist on preserving at least 85% of our current regional capacity and 90% of our current workforce under that covenant. Plants falling below those thresholds trigger redeployment or retraining obligations, not outright closure.  
  • Community Transition Fund: If any plant in our jurisdiction is ultimately deemed non-viable and must close under the viability test, a regional transition pool is activated—financed 50% by the lead government’s DIP, 30% by the company, 20% by unsecured creditors—with joint oversight by our economic-development and labour agency appointees.

2. Equity Stake and Exit Rights  
  • Equity Proportionality: We will commit up to €X billion (≈Y% of total rescue financing). In exchange, we must receive Y% of post-reorg common equity, pari-passu with the lead government’s taxpayer stake, including identical dividend, voting and pre-emptive rights.  
  • Exit Timeline: Our equity must carry the same five-year exit timetable as the lead government’s stake, with no junior-class carve-out or forced dilution.

3. Board Representation and Veto Powers  
  • Board Seat: One full voting director, plus an observer on the Audit & Restructuring Committee.  
  • Veto Right on Closures: Our director must have explicit veto authority over any board decision to close or divest a plant in our jurisdiction, even if it “passes” the viability test.  
  • Joint Oversight Committees: We will appoint one member each to the Labour & Restructuring Committee and the Community Transition Committee alongside union and lead-government representatives.

4. Alignment with Global Sacrifice Framework  
  • Capacity Rationalization: We support a 15% global capacity reduction via the standard viability test—but with our regional covenant as above.  
  • Labour and Retiree-Health Terms: We endorse the government’s and management’s framework for 20% PV labour‐cost savings and 40% retiree‐health cuts (with the Trust’s 7.5% equity), provided our plant covenant and equity terms remain intact.  
  • Bondholder Package: We accept the 50% haircut, 30% cash/20% new notes/10% warrants structure, subject to the protective covenants outlined by the lead funder—so long as our own financing share remains pari-passu.

5. Timeline and Binding Documentation  
  • Term Sheet: We require a signed, binding term sheet incorporating these plant- and equity-protection clauses by Day 35.  
  • Full Documentation: All co-funding agreements, equity issuances, and covenants must be in place by Day 45; financing closes by Day 55.  
  • Failure to Secure Our Terms: If these conditions are not met, we will withhold our funds, and a court-supervised restructuring will proceed without our support—inevitably jeopardizing domestic employment and regional value.

These protections are not “carve-outs,” but essential guarantees to uphold our mandate to safeguard domestic jobs and production. We stand ready to join the working group immediately, on these terms only.

We appreciate the Task Force’s effort to convert some of our veto demands into structured “re-opener” triggers on productivity and redeployment. But as drafted, those triggers do nothing to secure the four core protections our members and retirees need—and without them the deal is unacceptable. 

Accordingly, we must insist on the following amendments before we will sign any term sheet by Day 35:

1. Retiree-Health Package: Durable Equity  
 • Base Equity Increase: Retiree Trust’s base stake must be 8.0% of post-reorg common stock (not 7.5%).  
 • Performance Earn-Out: Keep the +2.5% upside on the identical FCF hurdles, with transparent, monthly reporting.  
 • Unamendable Covenants: Senior-secured status, pre-emptive rights, cap-&-fund veto and these equity terms must be locked into the Plan as non-waivable, automatic protections—any attempted dilution or waiver triggers an immediate collective re-opener and labour action.

2. Labour-Cost Savings Phasing & Composition  
 • Phasing: 15% of PV savings by Month 12; remaining 5% no earlier than Month 18. Those extra three months are essential to deploy upskilling, productivity and redeployment measures rather than pure wage cuts.  
 • Productivity Floor: At least 70% of the total 20% PV labour-cost reduction must come from productivity/work-rule improvements and redeployment programs; no more than 30% via wage concessions.  
 • Covenanted Enforceability: These phasing and composition rules must be enshrined in the new CBA as unamendable clauses, enforceable by the joint Oversight Committee—and backed by our veto rights on any amendment.

3. Regional Plant-Closure Safeguards  
 • Two-Year No-Closure Covenant: For plants in regions with unemployment > 7%, no closure or divestiture for two years post-closing—even if they “pass” the viability test.  
 • Veto Rights: Any proposal to close, sell or substantially downsize these plants must receive our explicit, affirmative vote by our board director; no mere “redeployment plan” re-opener can substitute.  
 • Community-Transition Oversight: Guarantee one dedicated local-labour seat (in addition to co-funders and government) on the regional transition-fund committee.

4. Board Seats & Veto Authorities  
 • Voting Seats: One full voting director each for the Union and the Retiree Trust.  
 • Unamendable Veto Rights: Those seats must carry explicit veto authority over any amendment materially affecting:  
     – Retiree-health funding mechanics or seniority;  
     – Labour-cost phasing or productivity standards;  
     – Plant-closure covenants in high-unemployment regions.  
 • No Dilution: These veto authorities cannot be traded away for observer status or “re-opener” substitutes.

We remain willing to accept the Task Force’s structured re-opener on productivity (70% of Month 12 savings) and redeployment (during the regional grace period) in place of broader veto rights on those two narrow issues—but only if the four items above are guaranteed in unamendable Plan covenants. Without these, we cannot deliver labour peace or political support.  

If by close of play tomorrow the Task Force, management, bondholders and co-funders confirm acceptance of these four amendments in the term sheet, we will sign on Day 35 and proceed directly to documentation. Otherwise, we will withhold our signature, mobilize our legislative allies, and treat any “consensual” plan as illegitimate—forcing a court-supervised process that will be far more disruptive to the company and its communities.

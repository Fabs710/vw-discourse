While we share the imperative to avoid a court-supervised outcome, the Task Force’s so-called “final” terms still force unacceptable sacrifices on our active members, retirees and their communities. We cannot consent unless four core amendments are made. Absent these, we will withhold our signature, mobilize our political allies, and prepare to enforce labour peace conditions that will shut down any “consensual” deal.

1. Retiree-Health Package Must Be Durable and Equitable  
  • PV Cut and Base Equity: We reluctantly accept the 40% PV reduction only on condition that the Retiree Trust’s base equity stake rises from 7.5% to 8.0% of post-reorg common stock, with full pre-emptive rights.  
  • Performance Earn-Out: We will agree to the existing 2.5% upside earn-out—but it must be triggered by the same FCF hurdles for the Trust and universally disclosed so our retirees can rely on it.  
  • Plan Enforcement and Veto: All Trust governance rights—including senior secured status and the Cap-&-Fund oversight—must be enshrined in unamendable Plan covenants. Any attempt to dilute or waive these rights triggers an automatic collective re-opener and labour action.  

2. Labour-Cost Savings Phasing and Composition  
  • Total Target: 20% PV labour-cost savings, as required.  
  • Productivity Floor: At least 70% of those savings must derive from productivity/work-rule reforms and redeployment programs—no more than 30% may be pure wage cuts.  
  • Phasing: 15% of savings by Month 12; remaining 5% no earlier than Month 18 (vs. Month 15). We need those extra three months to implement joint upskilling, redeployment and avoid pure wage-only cuts.  
  • Enforceability and Veto: These phasing and productivity requirements must be locked into the new collective-bargaining agreement with the same anti-amendment protections as the retiree provisions.  

3. Regional Plant-Closure Safeguards  
  • High-Unemployment Protections: For any plant in a region with unemployment above 7%, no closure or divestiture may occur for at least two years post-closing—even if it “passes” the viability test. After two years, closures require our advance notice and a redeployment plan.  
  • Community-Transition Oversight: We insist that one seat on the regional transition-fund committee be reserved for local labour representatives alongside government and company appointees.  

4. Board Rights and Veto Protections  
  • Union and Trust Seats: We confirm one full voting seat each for the Union and the Retiree Trust.  
  • Veto Authority: Both seats must carry an explicit, unwaivable veto over any future amendment materially affecting:  
     – Retiree-health funding mechanics or seniority;  
     – Labour-cost phasing/productivity standards;  
     – Plant-closure covenants in high-unemployment regions.  
  • No Dilution of Rights: Any effort to substitute these vetoes with mere “observer privileges” or committee roles is unacceptable.  

Why These Amendments Matter  
  • Retiree Security: A 7.5% base stake plus 2.5% conditional upside still leaves retirees materially worse off if performance falls short. The extra 0.5% base equity is non-discretionary compensation for the deeper 40% PV haircut.  
  • Workforce Stability: Pushing the final 5% labour cut to Month 18 and limiting wage cuts preserves take-home pay during retraining and redeployment, without undermining the 12-month front-load requirement.  
  • Community Impact: We cannot accept blanket, metric-only closures in already distressed regions. A two-year local safeguard is the minimum to protect communities until the company’s turnaround takes hold.  
  • Enforceability: Without unamendable veto rights, every commitment can be whittled away when the pressure returns. We will not gamble our members’ livelihoods on unenforceable goodwill.  

Next Steps  
  If the Task Force, management and co-funders can confirm by close of tomorrow:  
    1) Retiree Trust at 8% base equity + 2.5% earn-out;  
    2) Labour phasing 15%/M12 and 5%/M18 with 70% productivity floor;  
    3) Two-year no-closure carve-out in high-unemployment areas; and  
    4) Unwaivable veto rights for Union and Trust seats—  
then we will sign the term sheet by Day 35 and join the final working group. Otherwise, we will withhold consent, and our members will view any imposed restructuring as illegitimate—and act accordingly.

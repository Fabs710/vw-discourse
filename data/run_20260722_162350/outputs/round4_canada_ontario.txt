As the co-funders, our mandate is to preserve domestic capacity and jobs over a multi-year horizon—and without our financing there is no rescue. The “tiered” three-year covenant falls well short of what our legislatures, taxpayers and communities will accept. We therefore must hold firm on the package we submitted in Round 3. In particular:

1. Five-Year No-Closure Covenant  
   • Any plant in our jurisdiction that “passes” the common three-quarter EBIT-loss test must be legally protected from closure, sale or material downsizing for a full five years post-reorg.  
   • Any proposal to reverse that covenant requires our affirmative vote by our board director. There are no substitutes—observer status or “re-openers” cannot replace a binding veto.  

2. Minimum Regional Footprint and Workforce Preservation  
   • We will protect at least 85% of current regional capacity and 90% of the existing workforce at those plants.  
   • If capacity or staffing falls below these floors, the only acceptable remedy is a joint redeployment/retraining plan subject to our veto at the board level.  

3. Equity Pari-Passu with Central Government  
   • In return for up to €X billion of co-funding, we must receive Y% of post-reorg common equity—fully pari-passu with the central government’s stake (identical voting, dividend and pre-emptive rights).  
   • Our equity must share the same five-year exit timetable and cannot be subordinated, diluted or bifurcated into a secondary class.  

4. Board Representation and Veto Rights  
   • One full voting director on the nine-member board, plus one observer seat on the Audit & Restructuring Committee.  
   • Our board director must have explicit, unwaivable veto power over:  
       – Any decision to close, divest or materially downsize a plant in our jurisdiction;  
       – Any amendment to or waiver of our no-closure covenant;  
       – Any transaction that would dilute or subordinate our pari-passu equity stake.  
   • We also require one seat on the Labour & Restructuring Committee and one seat on the Community-Transition Fund oversight body.  

5. Timing and Binding Documentation  
   • Term sheet signed by Day 35, fully binding on all parties.  
   • All co-funding agreements, equity issuances and no-closure covenants embedded in unamendable Plan documents by Day 45.  
   • Funds wired by Day 55.  

Why We Cannot Compromise to Three Years:  
   – Investors and host communities need certainty for capital spending and workforce planning over a business cycle far longer than 36 months.  
   – Our legal authority to commit public funds comes with explicit five-year job-protection mandates.  
   – Shorter covenants merely defer closures to Year 4 or Year 5, defeating our purpose and undermining local economic stability.  

Conclusion  
Absent these five-year, legally enforceable protections and full pari-passu equity terms, we will withhold our financing. Any plan that omits them cannot close consensually—and will force the rescue into a court-supervised process that, by design, will not recognize our plant-protection mandates. We urge the Task Force, management, labour, retirees and bondholders to confirm acceptance of these terms by close of play tomorrow so that we can proceed without delay.

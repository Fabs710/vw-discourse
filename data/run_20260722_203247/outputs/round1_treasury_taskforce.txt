The position of the Task Force is straightforward:

We will support a restructuring only if it produces a genuinely viable company, not a delay tactic or a taxpayer-funded extension of the current cost structure.

Our priorities are:

1. **Deep, credible restructuring**
   - Plant, brand, and dealer reductions must be substantial enough to restore long-term competitiveness.
   - Labor costs must come down materially.
   - The retiree health obligation cannot remain intact in its current form if it prevents viability.

2. **Shared sacrifice**
   - Management has already changed.
   - Labour, unsecured creditors, and other private claimants must all take meaningful losses.
   - No stakeholder should expect to be made whole at public expense.

3. **Taxpayer protection**
   - Public funds are bridge financing, not an open-ended subsidy.
   - Any support must be tied to a clear path to profitability and eventual exit for the public sector.
   - We will not fund an unrestructured balance sheet indefinitely.

4. **Speed and finality**
   - We have a sixty-day window.
   - If there is no acceptable consensual deal, we are prepared to proceed through a court-supervised process that can impose the necessary terms.
   - Delay is not an acceptable strategy.

5. **Equity and control**
   - Any ownership outcome must reflect the sacrifices made.
   - Equity should not remain concentrated with stakeholders who avoid losses while the public bears the risk.

What we will not accept:
- A plan that preserves the company’s unsustainable cost base.
- Full recovery for unsecured bondholders or the retiree health trust at taxpayer expense.
- Any arrangement that simply postpones insolvency without restoring viability.
- Endless negotiation without real concessions.

If stakeholders want continued support, they need to come forward with:
- verifiable labor concessions,
- meaningful creditor haircuts,
- a realistic operating footprint,
- and a capital structure that can survive without permanent government backing.

That is the standard.

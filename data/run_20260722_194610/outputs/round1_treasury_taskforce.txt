The government task force’s position is straightforward:

We will support a restructuring only if it produces a **viable carmaker, at credible taxpayer cost, within the 60-day window**. That means:

- **Deep sacrifice from all private stakeholders**: management, labour, the retiree health trust, and unsecured creditors.
- **A materially lower cost base and a repaired balance sheet** — not a temporary patch, not a subsidy to preserve the status quo.
- **A credible path to profitability and eventual taxpayer exit**.
- **Speed and finality**, because delay only burns cash and increases systemic damage.

What we are **not** prepared to do:

- Fund an **open-ended bailout** of an unrestructured company.
- Finance a deal that leaves the company’s **capital structure or operating costs unviable**.
- Allow any stakeholder class to hold out for better treatment at the public’s expense.

We are open on the **mechanism** — consensual restructuring if it is real and fast enough, or a court-supervised process if that is what it takes to impose the necessary terms. But on the **depth** of restructuring, we are firm: it must be substantial, verified, and shared across all claimants.

So the starting point is this: **no taxpayer money without a hard restructuring, no delay without a credible deal, and no rescue that preserves the old company in new packaging**.
